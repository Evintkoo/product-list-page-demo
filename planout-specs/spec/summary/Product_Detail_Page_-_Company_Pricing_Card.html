1.1Overview

The Company Pricing Card is a new feature on the Product Detail Page designed to provide internal staff with immediate visibility into the financial breakdown of a product. It displays the specific costs associated with purchasing the item from a supplier and the established wholesale pricing for customers, ensuring that key financial data is easily accessible while viewing product details.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When a user opens a product page, the system automatically fetches the latest pricing information for that specific product.
2.Loading State: While the information is being retrieved, a temporary loading indicator is displayed to the user.
3.Displaying Supply Costs: Once loaded, the card displays the "Company Supply Price," which represents the amount the company pays the supplier, inclusive of tax.
4.Displaying Wholesale Pricing: The card also displays the "Wholesale Price," showing the base sale price for wholesale customers alongside the final amount including tax.
5.Error Handling: If the system is unable to retrieve the pricing data, an error message is displayed to inform the user that the information is currently unavailable.
1.3Key Concepts
–Company Supply Price: The cost the company incurs when purchasing the product from a supplier; this figure includes applicable taxes.
–Wholesale Price: The price at which the product is sold to wholesale customers, presented both as a base price and a tax-inclusive total.
–Read-Only View: The pricing information is presented for informational purposes only and cannot be edited directly from this view.
–Help Tooltips: Small interactive icons located next to price labels that provide quick definitions to ensure clarity on what each cost represents.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by eliminating the need for staff to navigate to separate financial reports or spreadsheets to find product costs. By surfacing this data directly on the Product Detail Page, team members can make faster, more informed decisions regarding wholesale strategy and procurement, while maintaining a clear understanding of the company's profit margins and tax obligations.
