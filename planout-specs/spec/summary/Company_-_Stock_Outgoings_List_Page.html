1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Stock Outgoings List" page provides a centralized dashboard for managing inventory departures. It allows users to view, search, and filter all outgoing stock records within the company. This page is designed to help inventory managers quickly locate specific outgoing shipments, track their status, and navigate to detailed records for further action.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you first open the page, the system automatically displays a list of all stock outgoings, sorted by the most recently created items first.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list using the filter bar. You may search by reference code, specify a date range, or select specific entities, sources (stores/warehouses), or document statuses.
3.Applying Filters: Once you have entered your criteria, click the "Search" button to refresh the list. The system will only show records that match your selections.
4.Sorting: You can click on any column header (such as Date, Status, or Entity) to re-sort the list. Clicking once sorts the list in ascending order; clicking again reverses it to descending order.
5.Pagination: The list displays 20 records at a time. You can use the pagination controls at the bottom of the page to move between pages or jump to specific sections of the list.
6.Viewing Details: If you need to see more information about a specific shipment, simply click on any row in the table to be taken directly to that document's details page.
7.Creating New Records: If you need to initiate a new outgoing shipment, click the "New Stock Outgoing" button to be redirected to the creation form.
1.3Key Concepts
–Reference Code: A unique identifier generated by the system for every outgoing stock document, used to track and locate specific shipments.
–Source: The specific location (either a store or a warehouse) from which the stock is being removed.
–Status: The current stage of the outgoing document, which can be Draft, Confirmed, Approved, or Cancelled.
–Entity: The legal business unit or company associated with the stock movement.
–Empty State: A helpful message displayed if your search filters return no results, providing an easy way to clear your filters and reset the view.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management by providing a high-level overview of all stock departures. By offering robust search and filtering capabilities, it reduces the time spent manually hunting for records. The ability to quickly sort and paginate through large volumes of data ensures that inventory teams can maintain accurate records and respond to operational needs with greater speed and efficiency.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Stock Outgoings List Page provides a centralized view for tracking all inventory items leaving your facility. This feature allows warehouse staff and managers to easily browse, search, and filter outgoing stock records, ensuring better visibility into the movement of goods.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Requesting Data: When you open the Stock Outgoings page, the system automatically requests a list of outgoing records.
2.Applying Filters: You can narrow down the list by entering a specific reference number in the search bar or by selecting a particular status (e.g., pending, completed) to view only relevant items.
3.Pagination: To keep the page performance fast and easy to read, the system displays the records in manageable chunks (pages). You can navigate through these pages to view older or newer entries.
4.Data Display: The system retrieves the relevant records and organizes them into a clear table format, showing key details such as the reference code, the date the outgoing was created, and its current status.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of records into smaller, numbered pages so you don't have to scroll through an endless list.
–Filtering: The ability to refine the list of results based on specific criteria, such as status or reference numbers, to find exactly what you need quickly.
–Reference Number: A unique identifier assigned to each outgoing stock movement, used to track and verify specific shipments.
–Status: The current stage of an outgoing shipment in the workflow, helping you identify which items are still in progress and which have been finalized.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Efficiency: By providing search and filter capabilities, staff can locate specific outgoing shipments in seconds rather than manually scanning through logs.
–Better Inventory Oversight: Real-time visibility into outgoing stock helps management monitor warehouse activity and identify potential bottlenecks in the shipping process.
–Scalability: The use of pagination ensures that the system remains fast and responsive, even as your company’s volume of outgoing stock grows over time.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Stock Outgoings List page provides a centralized view for tracking all inventory leaving your facilities. This feature allows warehouse managers and staff to easily browse, search, and organize outgoing stock records. By providing robust filtering and pagination, the system ensures that users can quickly locate specific shipments or monitor recent activity without being overwhelmed by large volumes of data.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you open the page, the system automatically retrieves the most recent outgoing stock records, sorted by date (newest first).
2.Search and Filter: You can narrow down the list by entering a specific reference number, selecting a status, or defining a date range.
3.Data Retrieval: The system sends your criteria to the database to fetch the relevant records.
4.Display: The results are presented in a clear list, showing key details such as the reference number, current status, destination warehouse, date of creation, and the total number of items included in the shipment.
5.Navigation: If there are many records, you can use the pagination controls to move through the list page by page.
3.3Key Concepts
–Stock Outgoing: A record representing inventory that has been dispatched or is scheduled to leave a warehouse.
–Reference Number: A unique identifier assigned to each outgoing shipment for easy tracking and cross-referencing.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of records into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
–Filtering: The ability to refine the list of records based on specific criteria, such as date, status, or destination.
–Sorting: The ability to reorder the list based on specific columns, such as the date the record was created.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing real-time visibility into outgoing inventory. By enabling staff to quickly filter and find specific shipments, the system reduces the time spent on administrative tasks and helps prevent errors in tracking. Furthermore, the organized list view supports better decision-making by allowing managers to monitor warehouse throughput and verify the status of outgoing goods at a glance.
