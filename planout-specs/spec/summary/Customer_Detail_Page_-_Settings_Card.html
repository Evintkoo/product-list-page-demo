1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Customer Settings Card is a read-only information panel located within the Customer Detail page. Its purpose is to provide a quick, at-a-glance summary of specific business classifications and tax configurations assigned to a customer. This component ensures that staff can easily verify a customer's status without needing to edit or modify the underlying data.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the specific configuration profile associated with the selected customer.
2.Wholesale Verification: The system checks if the customer is classified as a wholesale buyer and displays "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
3.Franchise Verification: The system checks if the customer is a franchise store owner and displays "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
4.Tax Status Display: The system retrieves and displays the current service tax status (e.g., whether tax is always applied) for that specific customer.
5.Display: All information is presented in a clean, read-only format within the Customer Detail page.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: Information is displayed for viewing purposes only; it cannot be changed directly from this specific card.
–Wholesale Customer: A business classification used to identify customers who purchase goods in bulk, often qualifying for different pricing or terms.
–Franchise Store Owner: A classification identifying if the customer operates as part of a franchise network.
–Service Tax Status: A label indicating the specific tax rules or requirements that apply to the customer’s transactions.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing critical customer profile information in one location. By providing clear, immediate visibility into a customer's wholesale status, franchise affiliation, and tax requirements, staff can make informed decisions during customer interactions and order processing without having to search through multiple screens or records.
