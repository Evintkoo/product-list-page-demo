1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Addresses Card is a user interface component designed to help you manage and view a customer's contact locations. It provides a clear, organized table that lists all saved addresses, including their specific types (e.g., Home or Office) and contact details. The feature also includes a simple way to add new addresses directly through an interactive form.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.View Addresses: When you open the customer profile, the system automatically loads and displays all saved addresses in a table format.
2.Review Details: You can scan the table to see the address type, primary contact numbers, fax numbers, and indicators showing which address is designated as the "Main" or "Shipping" location.
3.Initiate New Entry: If you need to add a new location, click the "Add Address" button located below the table.
4.Enter Information: A form overlay will appear, allowing you to input the address type, phone numbers, and fax number.
5.Validation: As you type, the system checks your entries (such as ensuring phone numbers meet the required length) to prevent errors.
6.Save: Once the form is complete, you can submit the information, which saves the new address to the customer's record and closes the form.
1.3Key Concepts
–Address Table: A structured list that organizes all customer locations, making it easy to compare contact details and status indicators at a glance.
–Status Indicators: Simple "Yes/No" markers that identify which address is the primary location and which is used for shipping, helping to avoid confusion.
–Form Validation: An automated check that runs while you are typing to ensure the information entered is accurate and complete before it is saved.
–Modal Overlay: A pop-up window that allows you to perform a specific task—like adding an address—without leaving the current page or losing your place.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines customer data management by centralizing all location-based information in one place. By providing clear indicators for primary and shipping addresses, it reduces the risk of shipping errors or miscommunication. Additionally, the built-in validation and easy-to-use form ensure that customer records remain accurate and consistent, saving time for staff and improving the overall quality of the customer database.
