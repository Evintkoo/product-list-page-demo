1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Store Stocks" feature provides a centralized view of how a specific product is distributed across your various store locations. It allows you to monitor inventory levels, track costs, and manage stock thresholds (minimum and maximum quantities) for each store directly from the dashboard.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you open the page, the system automatically retrieves the current stock data for the selected product and populates a list of all available stores.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list by selecting a specific store, searching by name or store code, or toggling a view to see only relevant stock conditions. Clicking "Search" updates the table based on your criteria.
3.Viewing Data: The table displays key metrics for each store, including current quantity, reserved stock, items in transit, and financial data like average and total costs.
4.Editing Thresholds: If you need to adjust stock management settings, click the "Edit" button. This turns the "Min Qty" and "Max Qty" columns into editable fields.
5.Saving Changes: After updating the desired values, click "Save" to commit your changes to the system. You can also click "Cancel" at any time to discard any unsaved edits.
6.Navigation: If the list of stores is long, you can use the pagination controls at the bottom to move between pages. You can also quickly jump to QR code or barcode printing tools from the header.
1.3Key Concepts
–Min/Max Qty: The minimum and maximum stock levels defined for a product at a specific store, used to help manage replenishment and inventory health.
–Reserved Stock: Items that are currently allocated to orders but have not yet left the store.
–In Transit: Items that have been ordered or transferred but have not yet arrived at the store.
–Average/Total Cost: Financial metrics that calculate the value of the stock currently held at each location.
–Pagination: A navigation tool that breaks large lists of data into smaller, manageable pages.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Inventory Control: By setting and monitoring minimum and maximum stock levels, you can prevent stockouts and avoid overstocking at individual locations.
–Operational Efficiency: The ability to filter and search through store data allows staff to quickly locate specific inventory information without manual searching.
–Financial Visibility: Real-time access to total cost data per store helps management make informed decisions regarding stock distribution and capital allocation.
–Streamlined Workflow: Integrated access to QR and barcode tools ensures that staff can manage labeling and inventory tracking from a single, unified screen.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Store Stocks feature provides a centralized view for monitoring inventory levels across different store locations for a specific product. It allows users to view real-time stock data, filter results by store or product details, and manage inventory thresholds (minimum and maximum quantities) directly within the interface.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: When the page loads, the system automatically retrieves the current stock levels for the product and populates a list of all available stores to be used as filter options.
2.Filtering: Users can narrow down the stock list by applying filters such as Store Name, Store Code, or specific stock visibility settings. Clicking "Search" updates the table to display only the relevant records.
3.Navigation: If there are many records, users can navigate through pages. The system maintains the active filters while loading the requested page of data.
4.Editing: Users can modify the minimum and maximum quantity thresholds for one or more stores.
5.Validation & Saving: Upon clicking "Save," the system performs a check to ensure that no minimum quantity exceeds its corresponding maximum quantity. If valid, the changes are saved in bulk, the table refreshes with the updated data, and the view returns to read-only mode.
2.3Key Concepts
–Stock Thresholds: The minimum and maximum quantity levels defined for a product at a specific store, used to help manage inventory replenishment.
–Bulk Update: The ability to modify multiple stock records at once and save them in a single action, rather than updating each store individually.
–Data Filtering: Tools that allow users to isolate specific stock information based on store identity or product attributes.
–Pagination: A method of breaking large lists of stock data into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management by providing a clear, actionable view of stock distribution. By allowing users to adjust min/max thresholds directly, the system helps prevent stockouts and overstocking, ensuring that inventory levels remain optimized across all store locations. The bulk-update capability significantly reduces the administrative effort required to maintain accurate stock settings.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Store Stocks feature provides a clear, real-time view of inventory levels for a specific product across all your business locations. It allows staff to quickly see how many units of a product are available at each individual store, as well as the total quantity available across the entire company.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When you open the product detail page, the system automatically retrieves the current stock levels for that specific item.
2.Data Aggregation: The system calculates the total quantity of the product across all locations to provide an immediate summary.
3.Displaying Details: The system populates a table showing the breakdown of stock by store name, the specific quantity at each location, and the date the information was last updated.
4.Filtering: The system retrieves a complete list of all your stores, which populates a filter dropdown menu, allowing you to easily narrow down the stock view to specific locations.
3.3Key Concepts
–Stock Level: The current count of a specific product held at a particular store location.
–Total Quantity: The sum of all stock for a single product across every store in the network.
–Store Filter: A selection tool that allows users to view inventory data for specific locations rather than the entire list.
–Last Updated: A timestamp indicating when the inventory count for a specific store was most recently recorded.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by eliminating the need to manually check inventory across different locations. By providing a centralized, accurate view of stock levels, your team can make faster decisions regarding product transfers, restock orders, and customer inquiries, ultimately ensuring that products are available where customers need them most.
