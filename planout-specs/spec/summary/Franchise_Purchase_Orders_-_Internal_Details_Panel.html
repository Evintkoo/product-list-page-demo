1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal Details panel is a read-only information display designed to provide a comprehensive view of a specific purchase order. It acts as a centralized "fact sheet," allowing staff to quickly verify the origin, destination, timeline, and administrative history of an order without needing to edit or modify the data.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Identification: The panel displays the unique Document Number and any associated Reference Number to help users identify the specific order.
2.Logistics Details: The system shows the source (Warehouse and Supplier) and the final destination (Deliver To) to clarify the movement of goods.
3.Timeline Tracking: The panel lists critical dates, including when the order was created, updated, sent, received, and when payment or fulfillment is due.
4.Audit and Administrative Data: The system displays who created or updated the record, the associated journal entry, and any additional notes provided for the order.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only Display: Information is presented for viewing purposes only; users cannot change these values directly within this panel.
–Audit Trail: A collection of timestamps and user identifiers that provide transparency regarding who managed the order and when actions were taken.
–Logistics Mapping: A clear breakdown of the supply chain path, showing exactly where an order originated and where it is intended to be delivered.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a "single source of truth" for franchise purchase orders. By consolidating logistical, historical, and administrative data into one view, staff can resolve inquiries faster, reduce the need to search through multiple screens or documents, and maintain better oversight of the procurement lifecycle.
