1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Sales Deliveries List Page provides a centralized view for tracking all outgoing shipments. It allows users to search, filter, and organize delivery records, making it easy to monitor the status of orders as they move through the fulfillment process.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you open the page, the system automatically retrieves the full list of sales deliveries and populates the "Warehouse" dropdown menu with available options.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list by entering specific criteria, such as a delivery number, customer name, reference number, sales order, sales invoice, date range, warehouse location, or current status.
3.Search: Once you have entered your desired filters, clicking the "Search" button updates the table to display only the records that match your criteria.
4.Sorting: You can click on any column header (such as Date or Status) to reorder the list, helping you prioritize or find specific records more quickly.
5.Navigation: If you need to see more information about a specific shipment, you can click on any row in the table to open the detailed view for that delivery.
6.Pagination: If there are many deliveries, the system breaks the list into pages, allowing you to navigate through large volumes of data efficiently.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filters: Tools that allow you to narrow down a large list of deliveries based on specific data points like dates, customers, or status.
–Warehouse Dropdown: A selection menu that ensures you only search for deliveries associated with valid, existing warehouse locations.
–Sortable Columns: A feature that lets you click table headers to organize data alphabetically or chronologically.
–Pagination: A method of splitting long lists into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
–Row Selection: The ability to click on a specific delivery record to view its full, detailed information.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Efficiency: Users can quickly locate specific shipments without manually scanning through long lists.
–Better Visibility: By linking deliveries to sales orders and invoices, the page provides a clear audit trail of the fulfillment process.
–Operational Control: The ability to filter by status and warehouse helps managers monitor bottlenecks and ensure that deliveries are being processed on time.
–Data Accuracy: Standardized search and filter options reduce the risk of human error when looking up order information.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales Deliveries List page provides a centralized view for users to track and manage outgoing shipments. This page allows users to search for specific deliveries, filter results by various criteria (such as customer, warehouse, or status), and organize the data through sorting and pagination. It is designed to give staff quick access to delivery details, including associated sales orders, invoices, and current workflow statuses.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page opens, the system automatically retrieves the full list of sales deliveries and populates the "Warehouse" filter dropdown menu with available options.
2.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list by entering specific criteria, such as a delivery number, reference number, customer, date range, or workflow status. The system updates the table to show only the records that match these selections.
3.Sorting: Users can click on any column header (e.g., Date, Status, or Customer) to reorder the list, making it easier to find the most relevant or urgent deliveries.
4.Pagination: To keep the page performance fast, the system displays deliveries in manageable chunks. Users can navigate through these chunks using page controls at the bottom of the table.
5.Data Display: The system maps the raw delivery information into a clear, readable table format, showing key details like the delivery number, associated sales order, invoice, customer name, and current status.
2.3Key Concepts
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down a large list of deliveries based on specific attributes like date, customer, or warehouse.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list into smaller, numbered pages to improve loading speed and readability.
–Sorting: The ability to organize the table rows in ascending or descending order based on a selected column.
–Workflow State: The current stage of a delivery in the business process (e.g., pending, shipped, or delivered).
–Reference Number: A unique identifier used to cross-reference the delivery with external documents or internal records.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by allowing sales and warehouse teams to quickly locate and monitor the status of any delivery. By providing robust filtering and sorting capabilities, the page reduces the time spent searching for information, minimizes manual tracking errors, and ensures that staff have real-time visibility into the fulfillment process, ultimately leading to better customer service and faster issue resolution.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales Deliveries List page provides a centralized view of all outgoing shipments within the Lumi ERP system. This feature allows users to quickly monitor the status of deliveries, track which orders have been fulfilled, and see exactly where and to whom goods are being sent. By consolidating information from various parts of the system—such as warehouses, customers, and invoices—this page serves as a single source of truth for the logistics team.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system gathers all delivery records from the database, pulling in relevant details like the delivery number, date, and current status.
2.Information Consolidation: The system automatically links each delivery to its associated records, including the original sales order, the specific warehouse involved, the customer, and the corresponding invoice.
3.Sorting: The list is automatically organized to show the most recent deliveries at the top, ensuring users see the latest activity first.
4.Data Formatting: The system organizes this combined information into a clear, readable format that is ready to be displayed on the screen for the user.
3.3Key Concepts
–Sales Delivery: A record representing the shipment of goods to a customer, including its unique tracking number and current progress.
–Workflow State: The current status of a delivery (e.g., pending, in transit, or completed), which helps users track progress.
–Reference Number: An additional identifier used to cross-reference the delivery with external documents or internal tracking systems.
–Linked Records: The connection between a delivery and other business entities, such as the customer who ordered the items or the warehouse that fulfilled the request.
3.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves operational efficiency by eliminating the need to manually search through separate order, invoice, and warehouse logs. By providing a unified list, staff can quickly answer customer inquiries regarding delivery status, identify bottlenecks in the shipping process, and maintain better oversight of the entire fulfillment lifecycle.
