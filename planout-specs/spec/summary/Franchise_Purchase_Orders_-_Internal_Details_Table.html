1.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Details Table is a component designed to provide a clear, itemized view of inventory orders within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to track the lifecycle of a purchase order—from the initial request to the final receipt—while providing automated calculations to ensure accuracy across all line items.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Loading: The system receives a list of purchase order items associated with a specific franchise order.
2.Itemized Display: Each item is rendered as a row in the table, displaying key details such as the SKU, product name, product code, quantities (ordered, sent, and received), pricing, and any relevant remarks from the store or warehouse.
3.Automated Calculation: As the table loads, the system automatically aggregates the values for all quantity and amount columns across every row.
4.Summary Presentation: A dedicated footer row displays these calculated totals, providing an immediate snapshot of the entire order's status and financial impact.
1.3Key Concepts
–Line Item Tracking: A detailed breakdown of individual products within an order, including specific identifiers like SKU and product codes.
–Lifecycle Quantities: Columns that track the progression of stock, specifically showing what was ordered, what has been dispatched (sent), and what has been successfully received.
–Financial Aggregation: The automatic summing of order amounts, sent amounts, and received amounts to provide a high-level financial overview of the purchase order.
–Operational Remarks: Dedicated fields for both store and warehouse staff to communicate notes or updates regarding specific items.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Accuracy: By automating the calculation of totals, the system eliminates the risk of manual arithmetic errors, ensuring that financial reporting and inventory counts are always precise.
–Enhanced Visibility: The table provides a single source of truth for both store and warehouse teams, allowing them to see exactly where an order stands in the fulfillment process.
–Operational Efficiency: With all necessary data—including remarks and financial totals—presented in one view, staff can quickly identify discrepancies between ordered, sent, and received quantities, leading to faster resolution of inventory issues.
