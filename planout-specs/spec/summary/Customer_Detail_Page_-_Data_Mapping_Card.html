1.1Overview

The Data Mapping card is a new component on the Customer Detail page within the Lumi ERP system. Its primary purpose is to provide a clear, at-a-glance view of how a specific customer is categorized or assigned within our internal records. Currently, this section focuses on identifying the specific staff member responsible for the account.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When you open a customer's profile, the system automatically requests the relevant account details from our database.
2.Loading State: While the system gathers this information, a brief loading message is displayed to ensure you know the data is being retrieved.
3.Display: Once the data is ready, the "Data Mapping" section appears on the page, marked by a visual icon for easy identification.
4.Information Presentation: The system displays the name of the assigned Salesperson. If no salesperson has been assigned to that customer, the system will clearly indicate that the field is "Unassigned."
5.Read-Only Access: The salesperson information is presented as a read-only field, meaning it is intended for viewing purposes only and cannot be edited directly from this specific card.
1.3Key Concepts
–Data Mapping: A dedicated section of the customer profile that organizes key administrative or organizational details about the account.
–Read-Only: A display format where information is visible for reference but cannot be modified by the user in that specific view.
–Unassigned: A default status shown when the system finds no specific salesperson linked to the customer record.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational transparency by ensuring that any team member viewing a customer profile can immediately identify who is responsible for that account. By centralizing this information in a dedicated "Data Mapping" section, we reduce the time spent searching for account ownership details, leading to faster internal communication and better customer service coordination.
