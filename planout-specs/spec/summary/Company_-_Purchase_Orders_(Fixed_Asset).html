1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Purchase Orders (Fixed Asset)" feature provides a dedicated workspace within the Inventory module for managing purchase orders specifically related to fixed assets. This page allows users to view, search, and filter these orders in a structured table format, ensuring that asset-related procurement is tracked separately from general inventory.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Accessing the Page: Users navigate to the Inventory section and select the "Fixed Asset" tab to view the relevant purchase orders.
2.Automatic Data Loading: Upon opening the page, the system automatically retrieves all purchase orders categorized as fixed assets.
3.Filtering and Searching: Users can refine the list using the toolbar, which includes search text, status selection, and date range pickers. The list updates only after the user clicks the search button.
4.Viewing Details: The table displays key information for each order, including document numbers, supplier details, status, financial totals, and counts for deliveries, invoices, and returns.
5.Navigation: Users can browse through the list using the pagination controls, which display 20 records at a time.
6.Creating New Orders: A dedicated button is available to redirect the user to a separate page to initiate a new fixed asset purchase order.
1.3Key Concepts
–Fixed Asset Purchase Orders: A specific category of procurement orders used for long-term company assets, kept distinct from standard inventory orders.
–Toolbar Filtering: A set of search and filter tools that allow users to narrow down the list of orders based on specific criteria without affecting the underlying data.
–Pagination: A display method that breaks large lists of data into manageable chunks (20 records per page) to improve performance and readability.
–Workflow State: The current status of a purchase order (e.g., pending, approved, or completed), helping users track the progress of their procurement.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Organization: By separating fixed asset purchase orders from general inventory, the finance and procurement teams can manage high-value company assets more effectively.
–Enhanced Visibility: The clear, tabular layout provides immediate insight into the status of orders, including how many deliveries or invoices have been processed against each asset purchase.
–Operational Efficiency: Built-in search and filter capabilities allow users to quickly locate specific documents, reducing the time spent manually searching through records.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

This feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to browse and manage purchase orders specifically related to Fixed Assets. By filtering out standard operational purchases, this tool allows your team to focus exclusively on high-value asset acquisitions, ensuring better tracking and oversight of long-term company investments.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: When you navigate to the Fixed Asset Purchase Order page, the system first verifies that you are logged in and authorized to view this information.
2.Data Retrieval: The system automatically requests a list of all purchase orders from the database, specifically filtering for those marked as "Fixed Assets."
3.Information Mapping: The system organizes the raw data into a clear, readable format, matching specific details like document numbers, supplier names, and financial totals to the columns in your view.
4.Display: The processed information is rendered into a table on your screen, allowing you to review the status, delivery progress, and invoicing for each asset purchase.
2.3Key Concepts
–Fixed Asset Purchase Order: A specific type of purchase order used for acquiring long-term company assets (such as equipment or machinery) rather than day-to-day supplies.
–Workflow State: The current stage of the purchase order (e.g., pending, approved, or completed), helping you track where each order stands in the procurement process.
–Document/Reference Numbers: Unique identifiers used to cross-reference the internal system record with the supplier’s own documentation.
–Activity Counters: Summary columns that show the number of deliveries, invoices, and returns associated with a specific order, providing an at-a-glance health check of the transaction.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Visibility: By isolating Fixed Asset orders, your team can easily monitor high-value capital expenditures without the clutter of routine operational purchases.
–Operational Efficiency: The clear summary of deliveries, invoices, and returns allows for faster reconciliation and auditing of asset acquisitions.
–Better Decision Making: Having immediate access to the status and financial totals of asset purchases ensures that stakeholders have the accurate, real-time data needed to manage the company’s asset lifecycle effectively.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

This feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to browse and search purchase orders specifically related to Fixed Assets. By filtering out standard inventory or operational purchases, this tool allows your team to focus exclusively on high-value asset acquisitions, such as equipment, machinery, or property, ensuring better oversight of long-term company investments.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system receives a request to view the Fixed Asset purchase order list.
2.The system automatically filters the entire purchase order database to isolate only those records marked as "Fixed Asset."
3.The system retrieves the relevant details for these specific orders, including dates, supplier information, financial totals, and current workflow status.
4.The data is organized into a clear, readable format (including counts for deliveries, invoices, and returns) and displayed in the user interface for your review.
3.3Key Concepts
–Fixed Asset: Long-term physical assets owned by the company that are not intended for immediate resale, such as office furniture or heavy machinery.
–Purchase Order: A formal document issued by the company to a supplier indicating the types, quantities, and agreed prices for products or services.
–Workflow State: The current stage of the purchase order in the procurement process (e.g., pending approval, received, or completed).
–Reference Number: A unique identifier provided by the supplier, used to cross-reference the order with their own internal documentation.
3.4Business Value
–Improved Asset Tracking: By separating Fixed Asset orders from general procurement, finance and operations teams can more easily track capital expenditures.
–Enhanced Visibility: Users can quickly see the status of an asset purchase, including how many invoices have been processed or deliveries received, without digging through unrelated operational data.
–Operational Efficiency: Centralizing this information reduces the time spent searching for specific high-value orders, allowing for faster reporting and better budget management.
