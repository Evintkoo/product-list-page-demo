1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Wholesale Customer List feature provides a centralized dashboard for managing wholesale business accounts. It allows users to view a comprehensive list of customers, search for specific records using various criteria, and perform quick administrative actions like deactivating accounts. The interface is designed to help sales and administrative teams efficiently locate and manage customer information.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page opens, the system automatically retrieves and displays the first page of wholesale customers.
2.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list by entering criteria into the filter bar (such as Name, Code, Group, or Salesperson) or by using the "Address Filter" popup for location-based searches.
3.Search Execution: Once the desired filters are set, clicking the "Search" button updates the table to show only the matching results.
4.Navigation: Users can click on any row in the table to view the full details of a specific customer.
5.Pagination: If the list is long, users can navigate through multiple pages using the pagination controls at the bottom of the screen.
6.Account Management: Users can click the "Deactivate" button on any row to open a confirmation window to change the status of a customer account.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of input fields that allows users to refine the customer list based on specific data points like tax numbers, birthdates, or active status.
–Address Filter: A specialized tool that allows for granular location-based searching, ranging from broad regions down to specific street addresses.
–Pagination: A navigation system that breaks large lists into smaller, manageable pages to ensure the application remains fast and easy to read.
–Customer Detail View: A dedicated page for a specific customer, accessed by clicking their name or row in the main list.
–Deactivation: A status management action that allows users to mark a customer as inactive without deleting their record from the system.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines wholesale operations by providing a "single source of truth" for customer data. By enabling quick filtering and direct access to customer details, staff can reduce the time spent searching for records. Additionally, the ability to manage account status (active/inactive) directly from the list view ensures that customer records remain accurate and up-to-date, supporting better sales tracking and account management.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Wholesale Customer List feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to help your team manage and track business-to-business (wholesale) clients. This tool automatically filters your entire customer database to display only those categorized as "Wholesale," ensuring that sales and account management teams have quick access to the specific information they need to support these high-volume accounts.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system scans the master customer database.
2.It applies a filter to isolate only those records marked as part of the "Wholesale" customer group.
3.The system gathers the relevant details for each identified wholesale customer, including their contact information, assigned salesperson, and current account status.
4.This organized list is then presented to the user, providing a clear overview of all active and inactive wholesale accounts.
2.3Key Concepts
–Wholesale Segment: A specific classification used to distinguish high-volume or business-to-business clients from standard retail customers.
–Account Status: A toggle indicating whether a customer account is currently active or inactive, allowing for better management of your client list.
–Assigned Salesperson: The specific staff member responsible for managing the relationship with a particular wholesale account.
–Customer Profile Data: The collection of identifying details—such as name, region, and contact information—that allows your team to effectively communicate with and service these clients.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines operations by eliminating the need to manually sort through your entire customer base to find wholesale partners. By providing a clean, focused list, your team can improve response times, ensure that account management responsibilities are clear, and maintain accurate records for your most important business relationships.
