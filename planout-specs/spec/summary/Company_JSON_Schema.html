1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Company JSON Schema feature establishes a standardized structure for managing company information within the Lumi ERP system. This ensures that all company-related data—such as contact details, tax configurations, product settings, and operational preferences—is stored and retrieved consistently. This module acts as the foundation for creating, updating, and viewing company profiles, ensuring that the system remains organized and reliable as the business grows.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Entry/Update: When a user creates or updates a company profile, the system validates the information against a strict set of rules (the "Schema") to ensure all required fields (like company name, address, and tax information) are present and correctly formatted.
2.Storage: Once validated, the core company information is saved to the main company database. Specialized settings, such as document tracking or platform-wide configurations, are linked to this record to keep the database clean and efficient.
3.Retrieval: When viewing a company's details, the system pulls the core information and automatically gathers related data (such as tax rates, product types, and custom settings) from various linked tables to provide a complete, unified view.
4.Filtering: For lists of companies, the system applies intelligent date-range logic. If a user doesn't specify a date, the system defaults to a 31-day window, ensuring that search results are always relevant and manageable.
1.3Key Concepts
–Schema: A blueprint that defines exactly what information is required for a company profile and how that information should be formatted (e.g., ensuring an email address looks like an email).
–Validation: The process of checking incoming data against the schema to prevent errors or incomplete records from entering the system.
–JSONB: A flexible storage format used for complex settings (like POS or warehouse configurations) that allows the system to store varied, evolving settings without needing to change the entire database structure.
–Foreign Key: A link between different data tables (e.g., linking a company to a specific tax rate or customer type) that ensures data consistency across the platform.
–Pagination: A method of breaking large lists of data into smaller, manageable pages to ensure the software remains fast and responsive.
1.4Business Value
–Data Integrity: By enforcing strict rules on what data can be saved, the system prevents "dirty data," reducing errors in reporting and daily operations.
–Operational Efficiency: Standardized settings for things like document tracking and product attributes mean that the system behaves predictably, allowing staff to work faster with fewer manual adjustments.
–Scalability: The modular design—separating core company info from complex settings—allows the business to add new features or settings in the future without disrupting existing data.
–Improved Reporting: Because company data is structured consistently, generating accurate reports for tax, sales, and inventory becomes significantly easier and more reliable.
