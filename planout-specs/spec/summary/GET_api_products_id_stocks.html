1.  Logic
1.1Overview

This feature provides a detailed view of a specific product's inventory levels across all your store locations. It allows you to see not just how much stock is currently on hand, but also how much is reserved for orders or currently in transit. The system automatically calculates the total value of the stock based on your company’s specific cost settings, providing a clear snapshot for inventory management.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Verification: The system first confirms that the requested product exists in the database. If it cannot be found, the request is stopped.
2.Configuration Check: The system checks your company’s global settings to determine how to calculate the "average cost" of the product (either using a company-wide average or a store-specific cost).
3.Data Retrieval: The system gathers the base stock information for the product across all stores, applying any filters you provided (such as specific store IDs or codes) and organizing the results into pages.
4.Snapshot Integration: The system pulls in real-time data regarding items currently reserved for customers and items currently in transit to those stores.
5.Calculation: For each store, the system calculates the total cost of the inventory on hand and aggregates the reserved and in-transit quantities.
6.Filtering and Finalization: If you requested to hide items with zero stock, the system removes those from the list. Finally, it presents the detailed store-by-store breakdown along with a summary of the total quantity across all locations.
1.3Key Concepts
–Reserved Stock: Items that are currently held for existing customer orders and are no longer available for general sale.
–In-Transit Stock: Items that have been ordered or transferred but have not yet arrived at the store location.
–Effective Cost: The unit price used to calculate the total value of your stock, which adjusts automatically based on whether your company uses a global average or store-specific pricing.
–Pagination: A method of breaking large lists of data into smaller, manageable pages to ensure the system remains fast and responsive.
–Stock Visibility Filter: An option to toggle whether you want to see every store location or only those that currently have items in stock.
1.4Business Value

This feature empowers store managers and inventory planners to make informed decisions by providing a "single source of truth" for product availability. By consolidating on-hand, reserved, and in-transit quantities into one view, it reduces the risk of overselling and helps teams identify which locations need stock replenishment. The automated cost calculation ensures that financial reporting remains consistent with your company’s accounting policies without requiring manual data entry.

2.  Data
2.1Overview

This feature provides a comprehensive stock report for a specific product across all company stores. It aggregates real-time inventory data, including current quantities, reserved items, and stock currently in transit. The report is designed to be flexible, allowing users to filter by specific stores or store codes, and it automatically calculates the total financial value of the stock based on your company’s specific cost-accounting settings.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Validation: The system first confirms that the requested product exists in the database. If the product cannot be found, the request is stopped.
2.Cost Configuration Check: The system checks your company settings to determine how to calculate the "average cost" of the product. It will either use a global company-wide average or the specific average cost recorded for each individual store.
3.Data Aggregation: The system gathers data from multiple sources, including current stock levels, pending reservations, and items currently in transit. It joins this information with store details to create a unified view.
4.Filtering: The system applies any requested filters (such as specific store IDs or codes). It also checks if you want to see all stores or only those that currently have stock on hand.
5.Calculation and Formatting: The system calculates the total financial value for each store (quantity multiplied by the effective cost) and generates a summary of the total quantity across all included locations.
6.Delivery: The final report is returned, organized by store, including the calculated totals and summary metrics.
2.3Key Concepts
–Effective Cost: The unit cost used for financial reporting, which is determined by your company’s preference to either use a single global average or store-specific averages.
–Reserved Stock: Items that are currently held for orders but have not yet been shipped or deducted from the physical inventory count.
–In-Transit Stock: Items that have been ordered or transferred but have not yet arrived at the destination store.
–Stock Aggregation: The process of combining physical inventory, reservations, and in-transit data into a single, easy-to-read report for each store.
2.4Business Value

This feature provides store managers and inventory planners with a "single source of truth" for product availability. By consolidating physical stock, reservations, and in-transit items, the business can make more accurate replenishment decisions and avoid stockouts. Furthermore, by automating the cost calculation based on company policy, the system ensures that financial reporting remains consistent and accurate across all locations without requiring manual data manipulation.
