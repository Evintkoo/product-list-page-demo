1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Sales Order List feature provides a centralized view for managing and tracking company sales orders. It displays a comprehensive table of orders, allowing users to quickly review key details such as customer information, delivery status, and associated documentation. The interface includes powerful filtering capabilities to help users locate specific orders efficiently and provides direct navigation to detailed order views.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page opens, the system automatically retrieves the most recent sales orders (defaulting to the 20 newest entries) and displays them in the table.
2.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list by entering criteria into the filter bar—such as document numbers, purchase order numbers, date ranges, specific warehouses, customers, or sales statuses.
3.Search Execution: Upon clicking the "Search" button, the system applies only the active filters to the request, refreshing the table to show only the matching records.
4.Navigation: Users can click on any row in the table to be redirected to a dedicated detail page for that specific sales order.
5.Creation: A "New Sales Order" button is available to quickly initiate the process of creating a new order.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of input fields that allows users to refine the list of orders based on specific business criteria, ensuring they only see relevant data.
–Sales Order Table: The primary display area that organizes order data into columns, including document identifiers, customer names, warehouse locations, and counts for related items like invoices and returns.
–Pagination: A system that manages large volumes of data by displaying a set number of records (20) at a time, ensuring the page remains fast and easy to navigate.
–Status Tracking: Visual indicators that show the current state of an order (e.g., whether it is closed or active) and provide insight into the progress of deliveries and invoices.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the sales management process by providing a "single source of truth" for all company sales orders. By enabling quick filtering and easy access to order details, it reduces the time staff spend searching for information. Furthermore, the inclusion of delivery, invoice, and return counts directly in the list view allows for immediate operational oversight, helping teams stay informed on the fulfillment status of every order without needing to open each record individually.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales Order List feature provides a centralized view for managing and tracking company sales orders. It allows users to quickly find specific orders using various search criteria and displays a clear, organized summary of order details, status, and related activity (such as deliveries and invoices) in a table format.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you first open the page, the system automatically retrieves the 20 most recent sales orders, sorted by their creation date.
2.Filtering: You can use the filter bar to narrow down the list. You can search by document number, purchase order number, date range, warehouse, customer, salesperson, or order status.
3.Data Retrieval: Once you click "Search," the system sends your specific criteria to the database, ignoring any empty fields to ensure the search is efficient.
4.Display: The system receives the matching orders and maps the raw data into a user-friendly table, showing key information like the order reference, customer name, status, and counts for related documents like invoices or returns.
5.Pagination: The system manages the list in manageable chunks, showing you the total number of available orders and allowing you to navigate through the results.
2.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of search fields that allows you to pinpoint specific sales orders based on business-relevant criteria.
–Sales Order Mapping: The process of translating raw system data into a readable table format, ensuring that fields like "Document Number" or "Customer" are clearly labeled for the user.
–Pagination: A method of organizing large amounts of data into smaller pages, which helps maintain system performance and makes the list easier to read.
–Sorting: The ability to organize the list by specific attributes (like date) to ensure the most relevant information appears first.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing sales and administrative teams with a "single source of truth" for all company sales orders. By offering robust filtering and a clear overview of order status and related documentation (deliveries/invoices), users can resolve customer inquiries faster, track order progress accurately, and reduce the time spent manually searching through records.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales Order List feature provides a centralized, searchable view of all company sales orders within the Lumi ERP system. It is designed to help users quickly locate specific orders by applying various search criteria, sorting the results, and navigating through large volumes of data efficiently.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you first open the Sales Order page, the system automatically displays the 20 most recent orders, sorted by their creation date.
2.Applying Filters: You can narrow down the list using the filter bar. You may search by document number, purchase order number, specific date ranges, warehouse location, customer, salesperson, or order status.
3.Processing Request: Once you click "Search," the system takes your selected criteria and retrieves only the matching records from the database.
4.Displaying Results: The system presents the filtered list, maintaining your preferred sorting and pagination settings so you can easily browse through the results.
3.3Key Concepts
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down a large list of orders by entering specific details (like a customer name or order status) so you only see the information relevant to your current task.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a long list of records into smaller, manageable pages (e.g., 20 items at a time) to ensure the page loads quickly and remains easy to read.
–Sorting: The ability to organize the list based on specific columns, such as the date the order was created, to help you prioritize your work.
–Default View: The pre-configured state of the page that ensures you see the most relevant information (the newest orders) immediately upon arrival without needing to perform any manual searches.
3.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves operational efficiency by reducing the time spent searching for order information. By providing robust filtering and organized views, staff can quickly respond to customer inquiries, track order statuses, and manage warehouse activities with greater accuracy and speed.
