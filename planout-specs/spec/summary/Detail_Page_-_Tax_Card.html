1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Tax Card is a dedicated information panel within the Service Product detail page. Its purpose is to provide a clear, read-only summary of all tax-related configurations associated with a specific service product. This ensures that users can quickly verify tax settings without needing to navigate through complex configuration menus.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The card displays a "TAX" badge accompanied by a percentage icon to clearly identify the section.
2.Primary Tax Information: The system retrieves and displays the main tax name, the tax unit of measure, and the specific tax product code.
3.Secondary Tax Information: The system retrieves and displays details regarding "Other Tax" configurations, including the tax name, the associated tax account, and the tax refund account.
4.Data Presentation: All fields are presented in a clean, read-only format. If a specific piece of information has not been set for the product, the system will display a dash (-) to indicate that the field is empty.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: Information is displayed for reference purposes only and cannot be modified directly within this view.
–Service Product Entity: The central data source that holds all the tax configuration details for a specific service.
–Placeholder Logic: A standard behavior where empty or missing data is represented by a dash, ensuring the interface remains consistent and readable.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Transparency: Provides immediate visibility into tax settings, reducing the time spent searching for compliance-related information.
–Error Reduction: By surfacing tax product codes and account labels clearly, users can easily verify that the correct tax settings are applied to a service before processing transactions.
–User Experience: Consolidates fragmented tax data into a single, organized component, making the Service Product detail page easier to navigate and understand.
