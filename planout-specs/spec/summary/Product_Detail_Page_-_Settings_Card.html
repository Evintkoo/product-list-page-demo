1.1Overview

The Product Settings Card is a new component on the Product Detail Page that provides a quick, read-only summary of a product's configuration. It allows users to instantly verify the operational status of a product—such as whether it is currently active, available for ordering, or restricted—without needing to navigate through complex menus or edit screens.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When you open a product page, the system automatically fetches the latest configuration settings for that specific item.
2.Loading State: While the information is being retrieved, a temporary placeholder (skeleton loader) is displayed to ensure a smooth visual experience.
3.Displaying Status: Once the data is loaded, the card displays a list of key settings. Each setting is accompanied by a visual indicator: a green checkmark for "enabled" (active) or a red cross for "disabled" (inactive).
4.Contextual Guidance: For settings that require more clarity, a help icon is provided. Hovering over these icons reveals a brief explanation of what that specific setting does.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: This component is for informational purposes only; it allows you to see the current status of a product but does not allow you to change these settings directly from this card.
–Status Indicators: Simple visual cues (green checkmarks and red crosses) that allow for immediate identification of a product's configuration state.
–Help Tooltips: Small, informative pop-ups that provide additional context for specific settings, helping users understand the impact of features like "Product Freeze" or "Can Order."
1.4Business Value
–Improved Efficiency: Users can verify product configurations at a glance, reducing the time spent searching for details in other parts of the system.
–Reduced Errors: By clearly displaying whether a product is "Active" or "Frozen," staff can avoid mistakes during the ordering or transfer process.
–Enhanced Transparency: Providing clear, accessible definitions for complex settings (like "Product Freeze") ensures that all team members understand the operational constraints of a product, leading to more consistent inventory management.
