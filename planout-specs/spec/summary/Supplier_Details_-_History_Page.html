1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Supplier History page provides a transparent audit trail for any changes made to a supplier's profile. This feature allows users to view a chronological list of updates, including who made the change, when it occurred, and what specific details (such as contact information, credit terms, or tax details) were modified.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: When you navigate to the page, the system first verifies that you are logged in. If your session has expired or you are not logged in, you will be automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: Once authenticated, the system fetches the complete change history for the specific supplier.
3.Error Handling: If the supplier cannot be found, the system will display a notification informing you that the history is unavailable.
4.Displaying Information: The page displays the current supplier's name at the top. Below this, a table lists every recorded change, showing the date of the update, the user who performed it, and the updated values for all relevant supplier fields.
5.User Navigation: If you need to see more information about the person who made a specific change, you can click on their name in the "Updated By" column to view their user profile.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Log: A historical record of all modifications made to a supplier's information, ensuring accountability and data accuracy.
–Authentication: A security step that ensures only authorized users can view sensitive supplier history data.
–Change Tracking: The system captures specific data points—such as address, phone number, email, and credit limits—every time an update is saved, allowing you to see exactly what changed and when.
1.4Business Value
–Accountability: By tracking who updated supplier details and when, your team can easily identify the source of any data changes.
–Data Integrity: Having a clear history of modifications helps in auditing supplier records and resolving discrepancies in contact or financial information.
–Operational Efficiency: Instead of guessing when a supplier's credit terms or contact details were last changed, staff can instantly verify the information, reducing the time spent on manual record-keeping or internal inquiries.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Supplier History page provides a transparent audit trail for each supplier within the Lumi ERP system. This feature allows authorized users to view a chronological list of all modifications made to a supplier's profile, ensuring accountability and providing a clear record of how and when supplier information has changed over time.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: When a user navigates to the Supplier History page, the system first verifies that the user is logged in. If the session is invalid or missing, the user is automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: Once authenticated, the system attempts to fetch the complete change history for the specific supplier. If the supplier cannot be found, the system displays a "Not Found" message.
3.Sorting and Organization: The system organizes the history records, placing the most recent changes at the top of the list.
4.Header Update: The page header is automatically updated to display the supplier's current name, pulled from the most recent record.
5.Table Population: The system populates the history table with detailed information, including the date of the change, the person who made the update, and the specific values for fields like contact details, tax information, and credit terms.
2.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific supplier's data.
–Authentication: The security process that ensures only logged-in users can access sensitive supplier history information.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking raw system information and presenting it in a user-friendly table format, ensuring that fields like "Credit Limit" or "Tax No." are clearly labeled and easy to read.
–Change Log: A list of modifications that tracks what was changed, who changed it, and when the change occurred.
2.4Business Value
–Accountability: By tracking who updated supplier information and when, the organization can maintain better oversight of data changes.
–Data Integrity: Users can easily verify the accuracy of supplier details by reviewing the history of updates, helping to identify and correct potential errors.
–Operational Efficiency: Instead of searching through manual records or emails, staff can instantly access a digital history of changes, saving time during audits or when investigating discrepancies in supplier data.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Supplier History feature provides a transparent audit trail for all changes made to a supplier's profile. This page allows you to view a chronological record of updates, showing exactly what information was modified, who performed the update, and when the change occurred. This ensures accountability and provides a clear reference for how supplier data has evolved over time.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the complete history of changes associated with a specific supplier.
2.Header Identification: The system identifies the most recent update to display the current supplier name at the top of the page for easy identification.
3.Data Mapping: Each individual change record is processed and organized into a structured table format.
4.Table Display: The table populates with the timestamp of the change, the name of the user who made the edit, and the specific values for all supplier fields (such as contact details, credit terms, and tax information) as they existed at that moment.
3.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that tracks the history of changes to a specific supplier's profile.
–Change Log: The collection of historical data entries that detail what information was updated and by whom.
–Field Tracking: The specific supplier attributes being monitored, including contact information, registration numbers, and financial terms like credit limits.
3.4Business Value
–Accountability: By logging who made each change, your team can easily identify the source of data updates, reducing errors and unauthorized modifications.
–Data Integrity: Having a clear history of changes helps in troubleshooting discrepancies in supplier information, such as unexpected changes to credit terms or contact details.
–Operational Transparency: Provides a reliable reference point for auditors or managers to review the lifecycle of a supplier’s profile without needing to search through manual records or emails.
