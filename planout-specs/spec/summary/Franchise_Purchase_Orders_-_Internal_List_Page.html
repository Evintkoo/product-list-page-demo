1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal List page provides a centralized view for managing internal purchase orders. This feature allows users to browse, search, and sort through all internal franchise orders, making it easier to track the status of goods moving between warehouses and stores.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page is first opened, the system automatically retrieves and displays a list of all internal purchase orders.
2.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list by entering specific criteria into the filter bar, such as the Document Number, date ranges, store or warehouse location, order status, or source.
3.Searching: After entering filter criteria, clicking the "Search" button refreshes the table to show only the matching records. This action resets the view to the first page of results.
4.Sorting: Users can click on column headers (such as Document Number, Date, Store, or Status) to organize the list. Clicking once sorts the data in ascending order; clicking again toggles it to descending order.
5.Navigation: If there are many orders, users can move through the list using the pagination controls at the bottom of the page.
6.Viewing Details: Users can click on any row in the table to be redirected to a dedicated page containing the full details of that specific purchase order.
1.3Key Concepts
–Internal Purchase Order: A specific type of order used for tracking inventory movement within the franchise network (e.g., from a central warehouse to a specific store).
–Filter Bar: A set of input fields and dropdowns that allow users to refine the list based on specific business data.
–Status Badge: A visual indicator on each row that clearly displays the current state of an order (e.g., "Ordered" or "Received").
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of records into smaller, manageable pages (20 items per page) to improve performance and readability.
–Sortable Columns: Table headers that allow users to reorder the list based on specific data points, such as dates or document numbers.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing inventory managers with a clear, searchable, and organized view of internal supply chain activity. By enabling quick access to specific order statuses and details, the team can reduce time spent searching for information, resolve discrepancies faster, and maintain better oversight of inventory distribution across the franchise.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal List page provides a centralized view for managing and tracking purchase orders across your franchise network. This feature allows users to browse all internal purchase orders, apply specific filters, sort the data, and navigate through pages of records to find exactly what they need.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Data: The system retrieves a list of purchase orders based on the filters, sorting preferences, and page number you have selected in the interface.
2.Process Records: Each purchase order record is retrieved and prepared for display.
3.Format Information: The system converts raw data into a user-friendly format, including:

* Formatting dates for readability.

* Converting numerical values into currency format.

* Assigning color-coded status badges to indicate the current progress of the order.

4.Display: The processed information is presented in a clean table, replacing any previous results with the updated list.
2.3Key Concepts
–Filters: Tools that allow you to narrow down the list of purchase orders by specific criteria (such as store, supplier, or date range).
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of records into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
–Status Badges: Visual indicators that show the current state of an order (e.g., "Ordered" or "Partially Received") to help you quickly identify which orders require attention.
–Sorting: The ability to reorder the list based on specific columns, such as date or document number, to help you prioritize your work.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a clear, organized view of all franchise purchase orders. By enabling quick searching and filtering, staff can spend less time hunting for documents and more time managing inventory. The standardized status badges and currency formatting ensure that team members can quickly assess the financial and logistical status of any order at a glance, reducing errors and improving communication between the warehouse, suppliers, and stores.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal List page provides a centralized dashboard for viewing and managing all purchase orders created by franchises. This feature allows users to easily browse through orders, apply specific filters to narrow down results, and view key details such as order status, financial amounts, and associated store or supplier information in a clean, organized table format.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Requesting Data: When you open the page or apply filters (such as searching by store, supplier, or date range), the system sends a request to retrieve the relevant list of purchase orders.
2.Sorting and Pagination: The system organizes the orders based on your chosen sorting preferences and breaks the list into manageable pages to ensure the interface remains fast and easy to navigate.
3.Data Transformation: The system takes the raw order information and converts it into a user-friendly format, ensuring that dates, currency values, and status labels are displayed clearly.
4.Displaying Results: The formatted data is populated into the table, replacing any previous results with the updated list based on your current search or filter criteria.
3.3Key Concepts
–Filters: Tools that allow you to narrow down the list of orders by specific criteria like store name, supplier, or date, helping you find exactly what you need.
–Pagination: A method of dividing a large list of orders into smaller pages, which improves performance and makes the list easier to read.
–Workflow Status: A clear label (such as "Ordered" or "Received Partial") that indicates the current stage of a purchase order in the supply chain.
–Currency Formatting: The automatic conversion of raw numerical values into a standard monetary format (e.g., $1,000.00) for better readability.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a "single source of truth" for franchise inventory procurement. By enabling quick searching and filtering, staff can spend less time hunting for documents and more time managing inventory. The clear status tracking and standardized financial display reduce the risk of errors, ensuring that store managers and warehouse staff are always aligned on the progress of their orders.
