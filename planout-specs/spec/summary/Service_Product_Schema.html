1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Service Product feature provides a structured way to manage service-based offerings within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to create, edit, and view service records—such as labor or specialized tasks—while ensuring that financial details like taxes, accounting codes, and pricing are accurately captured and linked to the broader finance module.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Accessing the Form: When creating a new service or editing an existing one, the system retrieves a form containing all necessary fields (such as SKU, name, price, and tax settings) along with pre-populated dropdown options for categories, tags, and financial accounts.
2.Data Entry: Users fill in the service details. The system supports flexible pricing (open price) and time estimation (open estimated time) to accommodate services that may vary by transaction.
3.Saving Data: When the user saves, the system performs a secure, "all-or-nothing" update. It saves the core service information and synchronizes any associated tags to ensure the record is always consistent.
4.Viewing Records: Users can view a paginated list of all services, which displays key information like the name, price, and tax status. For a deeper look, the detail view provides the full record, including audit information (who created or updated the service) and calculated fields like the price excluding tax.
1.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A record representing a non-physical offering (service) that can be sold, tracked, and taxed within the ERP.
–Financial Integration: The feature pulls data from the Finance module (such as tax rates, accounting codes, and units of measure) to ensure that every service is correctly categorized for accounting purposes.
–Open Price/Time: Settings that allow a service to have a flexible price or duration, which can be defined at the moment a transaction occurs rather than being fixed in the product catalog.
–Atomic Save: A safety mechanism that ensures that if a service is updated, both the service details and its associated tags are saved successfully together, preventing data mismatches.
–Audit Trail: Automatic tracking of who created or last modified a service record and when, providing accountability for all changes.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes service management, reducing manual entry errors by providing standardized dropdowns for financial and categorical data. By integrating directly with the Finance module, it ensures that tax calculations and accounting entries are consistent across the business. Additionally, the flexibility to use "open" pricing and timing allows the business to handle custom service requests efficiently without needing to create unique product records for every minor variation.
