1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Settings" card is a component within the Customer Detail page of the Lumi ERP system. It provides a centralized, read-only view of specific configuration settings for franchisee customers. This card ensures that users can quickly reference important business flags, accounting codes, and credit terms associated with a customer without the ability to modify them directly from this view.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Display Header: The card displays a "Settings" header to categorize the information.
2.Show Business Flags: The system displays whether the customer is flagged as a "Franchise Store Owner" or a "Wholesale Customer."
3.Show Accounting Details: The system lists the specific accounting codes assigned to the customer, including Sales Tax, AR Invoice, Credit Note, Down Payment, Deposit, and Price Difference accounts.
4.Show Credit Terms: The system displays the customer's financial limits, specifically the Credit Term (in days), Credit Limit (in Rupiah), and Overdue Limit (in days).
5.Read-Only Enforcement: All information presented is pulled directly from the customer's existing records and is displayed in a read-only format to maintain data integrity.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: A display state where information is visible for reference but cannot be edited or changed by the user.
–Franchise/Wholesale Flags: Boolean indicators that define the business relationship type of the customer.
–Accounting Accounts: Specific ledger codes used to categorize financial transactions (such as taxes or invoices) for that customer.
–Credit Terms and Limits: Financial parameters that define the customer's payment schedule and the maximum amount of credit they are permitted to hold.
1.4Business Value

This feature provides transparency and quick access to critical customer configuration data. By surfacing these settings directly on the Customer Detail page, account managers and finance teams can verify a customer's status and credit parameters instantly, reducing the need to navigate to separate accounting modules. The read-only nature of the component ensures that sensitive financial and business settings remain consistent and protected from accidental changes.
