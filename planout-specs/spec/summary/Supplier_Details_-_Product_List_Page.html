1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Supplier Product List page provides a centralized view of all products associated with a specific supplier. This page allows users to easily browse, search, and filter a supplier's catalog, as well as view key details such as inventory tracking status and discount information.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When the page loads, the system automatically retrieves the full list of products for the selected supplier.
2.Displaying Information: The page displays the supplier's name at the top, followed by a table containing all relevant product data.
3.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list by entering specific criteria into the filter toolbar, including Product SKU, Product Name, or Supplier Discount.
4.Search Execution: Once the user clicks the "Search" button, the system updates the table to show only the products that match the entered filter criteria.
5.Navigation: Users can click on a product name or its SKU to be redirected to a dedicated page containing more detailed information about that specific item.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Toolbar: A set of input fields that allows users to refine the product list based on specific attributes before triggering a search.
–Product Table: A structured list displaying product details, including row numbers, names, SKUs, discount percentages, and inventory tracking status.
–Inventory Tracking Indicator: A visual status (represented by a checkmark or an "x") that indicates whether the system is currently monitoring stock levels for a specific product.
–Read-Only Data: Most information on this page is for viewing purposes only, ensuring that the product list remains a reliable reference point for the user.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing procurement and inventory teams with a quick, searchable interface to manage supplier relationships. By consolidating product data, discounts, and inventory status into one view, users can make faster, more informed purchasing decisions and easily identify which products require active inventory monitoring.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Supplier Product List feature provides a clear, organized view of all items associated with a specific supplier within the Lumi ERP system. When you navigate to a supplier's profile, the system automatically retrieves their product catalog and presents it in a user-friendly table, highlighting key purchasing information such as pricing, discounts, and inventory status.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When you open the product list page for a specific supplier, the system triggers a request to gather all relevant product data.
2.Data Retrieval: The system searches the database to find every product linked to that specific supplier.
3.Information Transformation: The raw data is processed and mapped into a readable format for the screen. This includes calculating any applicable discounts and determining the current inventory status.
4.Display: The final list is presented to you, showing the product name, SKU, discount percentage, and a visual indicator of whether the item is currently being tracked for inventory.
2.3Key Concepts
–SKU: A unique identification code used to track individual products within the inventory.
–Supplier Discount: A calculated percentage representing any price reductions applied to a product based on supplier agreements.
–Inventory Tracking: A visual status indicator (represented by a checkmark or an 'X') that tells you if the system is actively monitoring the stock levels for that item.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines procurement and supplier management by providing immediate visibility into a supplier's offerings. By automating the display of discounts and inventory status, users can make faster, more informed purchasing decisions without needing to manually cross-reference separate documents or spreadsheets. This ensures that your team always has the most accurate and up-to-date information when managing supplier relationships.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Supplier Product List feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to manage and review the catalog of items offered by a specific supplier. This page allows users to quickly browse, search, and access detailed information for any product associated with a chosen supplier, ensuring that procurement and inventory teams have accurate, up-to-date visibility into their supply chain.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Supplier Selection: The user navigates to a specific supplier’s profile, which triggers the system to load the associated product catalog.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the list of products linked to that supplier, automatically organizing them alphabetically by name.
3.Pagination: To ensure the page remains fast and easy to navigate, the system displays products in manageable groups (pages). It calculates the total number of products available to provide accurate navigation controls for the user.
4.Detailed View: If a user needs more information on a specific item, they can select a product from the list to view its full details, including pricing, stock levels, and current status.
3.3Key Concepts
–Product Catalog: A comprehensive list of all items provided by a specific supplier, including unique identifiers, pricing, and current availability.
–Pagination: A method of breaking down a large list of products into smaller, manageable pages to improve system performance and user experience.
–Stock Level: The current quantity of a specific product held in inventory, helping users make informed purchasing decisions.
–Product Status: An indicator of whether a product is currently active or available for order within the system.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the procurement process by centralizing supplier-specific data. By providing a clear, organized view of product catalogs, the system reduces the time spent searching for item information, minimizes errors in ordering, and allows teams to monitor stock levels proactively. This leads to better supplier management and more efficient inventory control across the organization.
