1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a centralized view of inventory levels for a specific product across all company warehouse locations. It allows users to monitor stock availability, track items in transit, and manage reorder thresholds (minimum and maximum stock levels) to ensure optimal inventory management.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When you open the page, the system automatically retrieves the latest stock data for the selected product from all warehouses.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list of warehouses using the filter bar (by warehouse name, code, or specific warehouse selection). Clicking the "Search" button applies these filters to the table.
3.Data Review: The table displays detailed metrics for each location, including current stock, available stock, reserved items, and items currently in transit.
4.Bulk Editing: By clicking the "Edit" button, you can toggle the table into an editable mode. This allows you to update the "Min Qty" and "Max Qty" settings for multiple warehouses simultaneously.
5.Validation: As you update these quantities, the system automatically checks that the values are not negative and that the maximum quantity is not set lower than the minimum quantity, ensuring data integrity.
1.3Key Concepts
–Available Quantity: The stock currently on hand that is free to be allocated to new orders, excluding items already reserved.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been committed to existing customer orders and is no longer available for sale.
–In Transit: Stock that is currently being moved to a warehouse (e.g., from a supplier or another branch) but has not yet been officially received into the inventory.
–Min/Max Qty: Thresholds used to manage inventory levels; the "Min" triggers reorder alerts, while the "Max" helps prevent overstocking.
–Average Cost: The calculated average purchase price per unit for the stock held in a specific warehouse.
1.4Business Value

This feature empowers inventory managers to make informed replenishment decisions by providing real-time visibility into stock distribution. By enabling bulk updates to minimum and maximum stock levels, the system reduces manual administrative effort and helps prevent stockouts or excess inventory, ultimately improving operational efficiency and cash flow management.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a comprehensive view of a specific product's inventory levels across all company warehouses. It allows users to see not only how much stock is currently on hand, but also how that stock is allocated (e.g., reserved or in transit) and the associated financial value, helping teams make informed decisions about stock replenishment and distribution.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When a user views a product page, the system automatically requests stock data for that specific product.
2.Data Retrieval: The system gathers information from both the product inventory records and the warehouse directory to ensure all locations are accounted for.
3.Calculation: The system performs real-time calculations to determine the total value of the stock in each warehouse (by multiplying quantity by average cost) and calculates the grand total of all inventory across the entire organization.
4.Display: The processed data is presented in a clear table format, showing individual warehouse breakdowns alongside the calculated summary totals.
2.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Quantity: The total amount of a product currently held in a specific warehouse.
–Available Quantity: The portion of stock that is not reserved and is ready for immediate sale or use.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been set aside for specific orders or commitments.
–In-Transit Quantity: Stock that is currently being moved between locations and is not yet available for use.
–Min/Max Quantity: The defined thresholds for stock levels used to trigger replenishment alerts.
–Average Cost: The calculated unit cost of the product, used to determine the total financial value of the inventory.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Inventory Visibility: Provides a single source of truth for stock levels across multiple locations, reducing the risk of overselling or stockouts.
–Financial Clarity: Automatically calculates the total cost of inventory per warehouse, assisting with financial reporting and asset valuation.
–Operational Efficiency: By displaying "Available" versus "Reserved" stock, warehouse managers can quickly identify which items are ready for shipment without manual cross-referencing.
–Proactive Replenishment: With clear visibility into minimum and maximum stock levels, teams can better manage supply chain timing and avoid unnecessary storage costs.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a comprehensive view of a specific product's inventory levels across all your warehouse locations. It allows you to see exactly how much stock is on hand, reserved, or currently in transit, along with the associated costs. This gives you a clear picture of your inventory distribution and helps you monitor stock levels against your defined minimum and maximum thresholds.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request: You select a specific product to view its stock distribution.
2.Data Retrieval: The system gathers all inventory records for that product, linking them to their respective warehouse names and codes.
3.Calculation: The system calculates the total value of the stock in each warehouse and aggregates the total quantity across all locations.
4.Presentation: The information is organized into a clear list, showing individual warehouse details alongside a summary of the total quantity available for that product.
3.3Key Concepts
–Available Quantity: The stock currently on hand that is ready to be sold or used.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been set aside for existing orders or specific commitments.
–In-Transit Quantity: Stock that is currently being moved between locations or is on its way from a supplier.
–Min/Max Quantity: The established thresholds for your inventory; these help you identify when stock is running low or when you are overstocked.
–Average Cost: The calculated unit cost of the product, used to determine the total financial value of the stock held in each warehouse.
3.4Business Value
–Improved Inventory Visibility: Quickly identify which warehouses hold your stock, reducing the time spent searching for product availability.
–Better Decision Making: With clear visibility into reserved and in-transit items, you can make more informed decisions about replenishment and order fulfillment.
–Financial Oversight: By displaying the total cost per warehouse, you can easily track the capital tied up in inventory across your network.
–Proactive Stock Management: Comparing current levels against your minimum and maximum quantities helps prevent stockouts and avoids unnecessary overstocking.
