1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a centralized view of inventory levels for a specific product across all warehouse locations. It allows users to monitor stock availability, track costs, and manage reorder thresholds (minimum and maximum stock levels) directly within the system.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Loading: When the page opens, the system automatically retrieves the stock information for the selected product from all warehouses.
2.Summary View: A summary bar at the top displays the total quantity of the product currently held across the entire organization.
3.Filtering: Users can narrow down the list of warehouses using the filter bar (by specific warehouse, name, or code). Clicking the "Search" button applies these filters to the table.
4.Reviewing Details: The table displays comprehensive data for each warehouse, including current stock, available stock, reserved units, items in transit, and financial data (average and total costs).
5.Adjusting Thresholds: Users can click the "Edit" button to enable bulk editing. This allows them to update the "Min Qty" and "Max Qty" for multiple warehouses simultaneously.
6.Validation: As changes are made to the minimum or maximum levels, the system automatically ensures that the minimum quantity does not exceed the maximum quantity, preventing invalid stock settings.
1.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Quantity: The total amount of a product currently held in a specific warehouse.
–Available Quantity: The portion of stock that is free to be allocated to new orders (excludes reserved items).
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has already been committed to existing orders and is not available for new sales.
–In Transit: Stock that is currently being moved to a warehouse but has not yet been officially received into the inventory.
–Min/Max Qty: Thresholds used to manage stock levels; the system uses these to help identify when a product needs to be reordered or if a warehouse is overstocked.
–Bulk Editing: A feature that allows users to modify multiple rows of data at once rather than updating each warehouse entry individually.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory accuracy and operational efficiency by providing real-time visibility into stock distribution. By allowing users to set and adjust minimum and maximum stock levels, the business can better automate reordering processes, reduce the risk of stockouts, and prevent over-accumulation of inventory, ultimately optimizing capital tied up in warehouse stock.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a comprehensive view of how a specific product is distributed across your various warehouse locations. It allows users to see not just the total quantity on hand, but also detailed breakdowns of stock status (such as reserved or in-transit items) and financial valuation per location.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When you view a product, the system automatically pulls all stock records associated with that specific item from all connected warehouses.
2.Processing & Calculation: The system organizes this data into a clear list. For each warehouse, it calculates the "Total Cost" by multiplying the current inventory quantity by the average cost of the item.
3.Summary Generation: The system calculates a grand total of the inventory quantity across all warehouses to provide an immediate high-level view of the product's availability.
4.Display: The final information is presented in a table format, showing both the granular details for each warehouse and the overall summary totals.
2.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Quantity: The total amount of a product currently physically present in a specific warehouse.
–Available Quantity: The portion of stock that is not reserved and is ready for immediate sale or use.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been set aside for specific orders or commitments and is not available for general use.
–In-Transit Quantity: Stock that is currently being moved between locations and has not yet arrived at the destination warehouse.
–Min/Max Quantity: The defined threshold levels for stock; these help identify if a warehouse is understocked or overstocked.
–Average Cost: The calculated mean cost of the product units, used to determine the financial value of the stock on hand.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory visibility and decision-making by:

* Reducing Stockouts: By displaying "Min/Max" levels, staff can quickly identify which warehouses need replenishment.

* Improving Financial Accuracy: Providing the "Total Cost" per warehouse allows for better financial reporting and valuation of assets.

* Streamlining Operations: By distinguishing between "Available" and "Reserved" stock, the team can make more accurate promises to customers regarding delivery times and order fulfillment.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a comprehensive view of inventory levels for a specific product across all your warehouse locations. It allows you to see exactly how much stock is on hand, reserved, or currently in transit, helping you maintain optimal inventory levels and make informed purchasing or distribution decisions.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request: You select a specific product to view its stock distribution.
2.Data Retrieval: The system gathers all inventory records associated with that product from every warehouse where it is stored.
3.Calculation: The system calculates the total value of the stock in each warehouse (based on average cost) and sums up the total inventory quantity across all locations.
4.Display: The information is presented in a clear table format, showing individual warehouse details alongside a summary footer that displays the total quantity of the product currently held in the company.
3.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Quantity: The total amount of physical stock currently held in a specific warehouse.
–Available Quantity: The stock that is ready to be sold or used, excluding items that are already reserved.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been set aside for existing orders or specific commitments.
–In-Transit Quantity: Stock that is currently being moved between locations and is not yet available for use.
–Min/Max Quantity: The defined thresholds for stock levels used to trigger reordering or prevent overstocking.
–Average Cost: The calculated mean cost of the product units, used to determine the total financial value of the stock on hand.
3.4Business Value

This feature provides real-time visibility into your supply chain, enabling you to:

* Improve Accuracy: Eliminate guesswork by seeing exactly where your products are located.

* Optimize Stock Levels: Use the minimum and maximum quantity indicators to prevent stockouts or excess inventory.

* Financial Oversight: Instantly view the total cost of inventory per warehouse, assisting with budget management and asset valuation.

* Streamline Operations: Quickly identify which warehouses have available stock versus those that are waiting on transit, allowing for faster fulfillment decisions.
