1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a centralized view of your product inventory across different warehouse locations. It allows you to monitor stock levels, track costs, and manage inventory thresholds (minimum and maximum stock levels) directly from a single interface.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When you open the page, the system automatically loads and displays the current stock data for all warehouses.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list by using the filter bar to search by warehouse name, warehouse code, or product name. Clicking the "Search" button applies these filters and updates the table.
3.Data Review: The table displays detailed information for each warehouse, including available stock, reserved quantities, items in transit, and calculated costs.
4.Stock Management: You can toggle the "Edit" button to enable bulk editing. This allows you to update the minimum and maximum stock levels for multiple items at once.
5.Printing: If you need to label your inventory, you can use the built-in buttons to navigate to dedicated pages for printing QR codes or product barcodes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Total Quantity Summary: A display-only field at the top of the page that shows the combined stock level of a product across every warehouse.
–Minimum/Maximum Stock Levels: Configurable thresholds that help you manage inventory; the system flags low stock based on these settings and allows you to set a cap on how much should be held.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that is currently committed to existing orders and is not available for new sales.
–In Transit: Inventory that is currently being moved between locations and is not yet available for use.
–Calculated Costs: The system automatically calculates the "Total Cost" for each row by multiplying the current inventory quantity by the average cost of the items.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management by providing real-time visibility into stock distribution. By allowing users to adjust minimum and maximum stock levels directly within the table, the system helps prevent stockouts and overstocking. Furthermore, the automated cost calculations and quick access to barcode/QR printing tools reduce manual administrative work, ensuring that warehouse data remains accurate and actionable.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a comprehensive view of inventory levels for a specific product across all company warehouses. When you view a product's details, the system automatically retrieves and displays a breakdown of stock status, costs, and replenishment thresholds, allowing you to monitor inventory health at a glance.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Trigger: The system initiates the data retrieval process automatically when you open the product details page.
2.Data Collection: The system gathers all stock records associated with that specific product, including details about the warehouses where the items are stored.
3.Data Processing: The system organizes the raw data into a clear format, calculating the total inventory quantity across all locations.
4.Display: The processed information is presented in a table, showing specific metrics for each warehouse alongside a summary of the total stock available.
2.3Key Concepts
–Available Quantity: The number of items currently on hand that are ready to be sold or used.
–Reserved Quantity: Items that are already allocated to specific orders but have not yet left the warehouse.
–In Transit: Stock that is currently being moved between locations or is on its way from a supplier.
–Min/Max Quantity: The established safety thresholds for inventory levels to help you decide when to reorder stock.
–Average/Total Cost: Financial metrics that provide insight into the value of the inventory held in each warehouse.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory visibility and operational efficiency by:

* Centralized Oversight: Eliminating the need to check multiple locations manually by providing a single, consolidated view of stock levels.

* Informed Decision Making: Helping managers identify which warehouses are overstocked or understocked based on the defined minimum and maximum thresholds.

* Financial Clarity: Providing immediate access to the cost value of inventory, which assists in better budget management and financial reporting.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Warehouse Stocks feature provides a clear, real-time view of inventory levels for a specific product across all your warehouse locations. It allows you to see exactly how much stock is on hand, how much is currently reserved or in transit, and the associated financial costs, helping you make informed decisions about stock replenishment and distribution.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system receives a request to view stock information for a specific product.
2.The system searches the database to match that product with all associated warehouse records.
3.For every warehouse where the product is stored, the system retrieves detailed inventory metrics, including current stock, reserved items, and items currently being moved (in transit).
4.The system calculates the total quantity of the product across all locations.
5.The final report is generated, displaying a breakdown by warehouse alongside the overall total quantity.
3.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Quantity: The total amount of a product physically present in a specific warehouse.
–Available Quantity: The stock that is currently ready to be sold or used, excluding reserved items.
–Reserved Quantity: Stock that has been set aside for specific customer orders or internal commitments.
–In Transit Quantity: Stock that has been ordered or transferred but has not yet arrived at the warehouse.
–Min/Max Quantity: The defined thresholds for stock levels used to trigger reordering or prevent overstocking.
–Average/Total Cost: The financial valuation of the stock held in a specific warehouse, calculated based on unit costs.
3.4Business Value

This feature eliminates guesswork regarding inventory availability. By providing a centralized view of stock levels across multiple locations, it enables your team to:

–Improve order fulfillment accuracy by knowing exactly what is available versus what is reserved.
–Optimize warehouse space and capital by monitoring minimum and maximum stock thresholds.
–Gain better financial visibility into inventory assets through automated cost calculations.
–Reduce the risk of stockouts or overstocking by tracking items currently in transit.
