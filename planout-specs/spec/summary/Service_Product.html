1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Service Product feature provides a structured way to manage non-physical offerings (services) within the Lumi ERP system. It allows your team to define specific services, categorize them for accounting purposes, manage complex tax requirements, and track estimated service durations. This ensures that services are priced accurately, taxed correctly, and easily reportable for financial and operational analysis.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Categorization: Every service is assigned a "Service Product Type." This classification links the service to specific accounting journals and refund accounts (such as PPh 23), ensuring that financial data is automatically routed to the correct ledgers.
2.Service Definition: You define the core details of the service, including a unique SKU, name, and description.
3.Pricing and Tax Configuration: You set the base price and configure tax details. The system supports both primary and secondary taxes, allowing you to specify whether the price includes tax or if it should be calculated separately.
4.Operational Settings: You can set an estimated time for the service. If a service price or duration varies by customer or situation, you can enable "Open" settings, which allow staff to manually enter the price or time at the moment of the transaction.
5.Reporting and Tagging: You can assign tags to services to group them for easier searching or reporting. Additionally, you can link services to specific tax units of measure and product codes to ensure compliance with official reporting requirements like e-Faktur.
1.3Key Concepts
–Service Product Type: A category that dictates the accounting behavior of a service, such as which financial accounts are impacted when the service is sold or refunded.
–Open Price/Time: A setting that allows flexibility during a transaction, enabling staff to override default values when a service is customized or varies in duration.
–Tax Unit of Measure (UoM): A standardized list of 39 units (e.g., hours, minutes, units) used to ensure consistent tax reporting and measurement across all service types.
–Service Product Tags: Labels used to organize and filter services, making it easier to manage large catalogs of offerings.
–Including Tax: A configuration toggle that determines if the displayed price already accounts for tax, which automatically adjusts the underlying base price calculation.
1.4Business Value
–Financial Accuracy: By linking services to specific accounting and refund accounts, you reduce manual data entry errors and ensure that tax liabilities are calculated and recorded correctly.
–Operational Efficiency: The ability to set estimated times helps with scheduling and resource planning, while "Open" fields provide the flexibility needed for dynamic service environments.
–Regulatory Compliance: Built-in support for tax product codes and standardized units of measure ensures that your business remains compliant with local tax reporting requirements (e-Faktur).
–Better Insights: Categorization and tagging allow management to generate detailed reports on service performance, helping you identify which service types are most profitable or frequently requested.
