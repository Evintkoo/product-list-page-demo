1.  Data
1.1Overview

This feature provides a structured way to retrieve a list of villages within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to search for specific villages and filter them by their parent administrative regions (District, Regency/Municipality, or Province). The system ensures that each village is returned with its full hierarchical context, making it easy to understand exactly where a village is located within the regional structure.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: The system receives a request to list villages, which may include optional search criteria such as a village name or specific parent region IDs (Province, Regency, or District).
2.Data Retrieval: The system queries the central area database, specifically looking for entries categorized as "villages."
3.Hierarchical Mapping: As it retrieves the village data, the system automatically links each village to its parent District, Regency/Municipality, and Province to provide a complete location profile.
4.Filtering and Sorting: The system applies any requested filters to narrow down the results and sorts the final list alphabetically by village name.
5.Pagination: To ensure performance, the system returns the results in manageable chunks (pages), including the total count of available records to help with navigation.
6.Response Formatting: The final data is packaged into a standardized format, ensuring that the village details and its parent hierarchy are clearly presented for the user.
1.3Key Concepts
–Hierarchical Data: The system understands the relationship between different administrative levels, ensuring that a village is always associated with its correct district, regency, and province.
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down a large list of villages by name or by selecting a specific parent region, which helps users find information faster.
–Pagination: A method of breaking large datasets into smaller, manageable pages to keep the application fast and responsive.
–Standardized Schema: A consistent "blueprint" for how village information is delivered, ensuring that different parts of the software can reliably read and display the data.
1.4Business Value

This feature simplifies regional data management by providing a reliable, searchable, and organized view of village information. By automatically linking villages to their parent regions, it reduces manual data entry errors and ensures that reporting and administrative tasks—such as assigning resources or tracking regional performance—are based on accurate, hierarchical location data.
