1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Attributes Card is a dedicated section on the product detail page designed to provide a clear, organized summary of a product's classification and specific technical details. This component acts as a "read-only" information panel, ensuring that users can quickly identify key metadata about an item without the need for editing or complex navigation.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system retrieves the product information from the central database.
2.The "Unit Bisnis" (Business Unit) is displayed to identify the product's organizational origin.
3.The "Kategori Item" (Item Category) is shown to clarify the product type.
4.The "Tipe Mobil" (Car Type) is displayed to indicate compatibility or vehicle application.
5.Three additional custom attribute slots (Attribute 4, Attribute 5, and Attribute 6) are populated to show any remaining specific metadata associated with the product.
6.All information is presented in a clean, static list format for easy viewing.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-only: Information is displayed for reference purposes only and cannot be modified directly within this view.
–Metadata: Descriptive data about the product, such as its category or business unit, which helps in organizing and identifying items.
–Custom Attribute Slots: Flexible fields that allow the system to display specific product details that may vary depending on the item type.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing staff with immediate access to critical product specifications. By centralizing this information on the product page, users spend less time searching for details in external documents or secondary systems, reducing the risk of errors during product identification and ensuring consistent information across the organization.
