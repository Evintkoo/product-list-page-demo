1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Supplier Settings Card is a read-only component located on the Supplier Detail page. Its purpose is to provide a quick, at-a-glance summary of a supplier’s operational status and their associated accounting configurations. This card ensures that users can easily verify key supplier settings without needing to navigate through multiple screens or edit records.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The card displays a "Settings" header accompanied by a gear icon to clearly identify the section.
2.Status Flag Review: The system displays three primary status indicators: whether the supplier is active, whether they are permitted to receive store purchase orders, and whether they are classified as an internal supplier.
3.Visual Status Indicators: For each status flag, the system uses a green checkmark icon to indicate a "Yes" status and a red "X" icon to indicate a "No" status.
4.Contextual Help: Each status flag includes a help icon that, when interacted with, provides additional information about what that specific setting represents.
5.Accounting Data Display: The card lists specific accounting details, including sales tax information and various accounts payable (AP) and deposit account designations, presented in a clear, read-only format.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: Information is displayed for reference purposes only and cannot be modified directly within this card.
–Status Flags: Boolean indicators (Yes/No) that use color-coded icons to help users instantly identify the operational state of a supplier.
–Tooltip: A small informational pop-up triggered by the help icon that provides clarity on the purpose of specific settings.
–Accounting Accounts: Specific financial categories (such as deposit, down payment, and invoice accounts) that define how transactions with this supplier are handled in the system.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing critical supplier information in one location. By providing immediate visibility into status flags and accounting settings, it reduces the time staff spend searching for data, minimizes the risk of errors during procurement, and ensures that users have the necessary context to make informed decisions when managing supplier relationships.
