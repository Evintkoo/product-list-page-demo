1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Customer Group card is a dedicated section on the Customer Detail page designed to provide immediate visibility into which group a customer belongs to. This component acts as a clear, read-only reference point, ensuring that users can quickly identify the organizational or categorical affiliation of a customer without needing to navigate through multiple screens.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system identifies the specific customer being viewed.
2.The application retrieves the group information associated with that customer.
3.The system checks if a valid group name exists for the customer.
4.If a group name is found, it is displayed clearly on the card.
5.If no group is assigned or the information is missing, the card displays a "No group assigned" placeholder to ensure the user knows the field is empty rather than broken.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-only field: A display-only element that prevents users from accidentally modifying the customer's group assignment directly from this view.
–Placeholder state: A user-friendly label that appears when data is unavailable, providing clarity that the field is intentionally blank.
–Customer Group: A classification or category used to organize customers within the system.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing essential customer identity information. By providing a dedicated, easy-to-read card for group affiliation, support and sales teams can quickly understand a customer's context, enabling more personalized service and accurate reporting without the need for manual data lookups.
