1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Store Sales list feature provides a centralized view for managing and monitoring retail sales transactions within the Lumi ERP system. This interface allows users to view a comprehensive list of sales, filter them by various criteria (such as store, customer, or status), and easily navigate to specific transaction details.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Accessing the View: Users navigate to the Sales section and select the "Store" tab to view the list of retail sales.
2.Initial Data Loading: Upon loading, the system automatically fetches the most recent sales records (20 per page) and displays them in a table.
3.Filtering: Users can refine the list using the filter bar, which includes options for store, document number, customer, status, salesperson, and date ranges.
4.Applying Filters: Once the desired filters are set, the user clicks the "Search" button to update the table with the relevant results.
5.Reviewing Data: The table displays key transaction details, including document numbers, dates, customer names, vehicle registrations, status badges, and total amounts.
6.Navigation: Users can click on specific document numbers, customer names, or vehicle registration numbers to jump directly to the corresponding detail pages for more information.
7.Pagination: If there are more than 20 records, users can use the pagination controls at the bottom of the table to navigate through additional pages of sales data.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of search and selection tools that allows users to narrow down the sales list based on specific business requirements.
–Status Badge: A visual indicator (e.g., "Draft" or "Completed") that helps users quickly identify the current stage of a sale.
–Pagination: A system that breaks large lists into manageable chunks of 20 records to ensure the page remains fast and easy to read.
–Drill-down Links: Interactive elements within the table that allow users to move from the summary list to detailed records for sales, customers, or vehicles.
–Data Formatting: Standardized display rules for dates (dd-mmm-yyyy) and currency (Indonesian format) to ensure consistency across the platform.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the retail sales management process by providing a single, organized location to track transaction history. By offering robust filtering and quick navigation to detailed records, it reduces the time staff spend searching for information, improves accuracy in sales reporting, and allows for better oversight of store performance and individual transaction statuses.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales List feature provides a centralized view for retail staff to monitor and manage store sales. It allows users to view a paginated list of transactions, filter results by specific stores or sales personnel, and navigate to detailed information for individual sales, customers, or vehicles.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: When the page loads, the system verifies the user's session. If the user is not logged in, they are automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Initialization: Once authenticated, the system simultaneously retrieves the initial list of sales (defaulting to the 20 most recent transactions), a list of all sales personnel, and a list of all stores.
3.Displaying Information: The retrieved sales data is mapped into a table, while the user and store lists populate the filter dropdowns.
4.Filtering: Users can narrow down the sales list by selecting a specific store or salesperson and clicking the "Search" button. The table updates to show only the relevant results.
5.Navigation: Users can move through the sales history using pagination controls, which load additional sets of 20 records as needed.
6.Drill-down: Users can click on specific items within the table (such as the document number, customer name, or vehicle registration) to view more detailed information about that specific sale, customer, or vehicle.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of data into smaller, manageable pages (20 records at a time) to improve performance and readability.
–Filtering: The ability to refine the sales list based on specific criteria, such as the store location or the salesperson who handled the transaction.
–Session Validation: A security measure that ensures only authorized users can access the sales data.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking raw system information and organizing it into a user-friendly table format with clear labels like "Status," "Amount," and "Salesperson."
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines retail operations by providing a clear, searchable overview of sales activity. By enabling staff to quickly locate specific transactions and filter by store or employee, the system reduces administrative time spent searching for records. Furthermore, the ability to drill down into customer and vehicle details directly from the sales list ensures that staff have the necessary information at their fingertips to provide better customer service and maintain accurate sales records.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales List feature provides a centralized, organized view of all retail transactions within the Lumi ERP system. It allows staff to quickly browse, search, and navigate through sales records, ensuring that key transaction details—such as the customer involved, the vehicle sold, the store location, and the current status of the sale—are easily accessible in one place.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Requesting Data: When a user opens the Sales List page, the system calculates which "page" of results to show based on the user's current view.
2.Retrieving Records: The system fetches a specific batch of sales records, sorted by the most recent transactions first.
3.Gathering Details: For every sale in that batch, the system automatically pulls in related information, including the customer's name, the vehicle's registration number, the store name, and the salesperson responsible.
4.Counting Totals: Simultaneously, the system counts the total number of sales in the database to ensure the pagination controls (e.g., "Page 1 of 10") are accurate.
5.Displaying the List: The compiled data is presented in a table format, where users can see the document number, status, total amount, and other key details for each transaction.
3.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of sales into smaller, manageable pages to ensure the software remains fast and easy to navigate.
–Workflow State: The current stage of a sale (e.g., pending, completed, or cancelled), which helps staff identify which transactions require immediate attention.
–Reference Code: A unique identifier for each sale that acts as a link, allowing users to click through to see the full transaction history.
–Linked Entities: Related information that is automatically connected to a sale, such as the specific customer, the vehicle involved, and the store where the transaction occurred.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a clear, real-time overview of retail performance. By consolidating transaction data into a single, searchable list, staff can spend less time hunting for information and more time managing customer relationships. Furthermore, the inclusion of status tracking and direct links to customer and vehicle details allows for faster decision-making and better oversight of the entire sales lifecycle.
