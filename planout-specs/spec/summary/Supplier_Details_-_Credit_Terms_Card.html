1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Credit Terms Card is a dedicated section on the Supplier Detail page designed to provide a quick, clear view of the financial arrangements established with a specific supplier. This component displays the maximum credit amount allowed and the agreed-upon payment timeframe, ensuring that users have immediate access to this critical information without needing to navigate through complex menus.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system retrieves the supplier's financial profile from the database.
2.The Credit Terms Card component receives this data and displays the "Credit Terms" header with a visual icon for easy identification.
3.The system displays the Credit Limit, automatically formatted as Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for readability.
4.The system displays the Credit Term, showing the number of days allowed for payment followed by the word "days."
5.All information is presented in a read-only format, ensuring the data remains secure and consistent while being viewed.
1.3Key Concepts
–Credit Limit: The maximum monetary value of goods or services that the supplier allows the company to purchase on credit, displayed in local currency.
–Credit Term (Days): The specific number of days the company has to settle an invoice after a purchase is made.
–Read-Only: A display setting that allows users to view information clearly without the ability to edit or change the values directly from this card.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing essential financial data in one place. By providing an instant, accurate view of credit limits and payment terms, procurement and finance teams can make informed purchasing decisions, avoid exceeding credit limits, and better manage cash flow and payment schedules.
