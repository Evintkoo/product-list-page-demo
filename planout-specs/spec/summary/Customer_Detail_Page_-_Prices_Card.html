1.1Overview

The Prices Card is a dedicated section on the Customer Detail page within the Lumi ERP system. Its primary purpose is to provide a clear, at-a-glance view of the specific pricing configuration assigned to a customer. This ensures that anyone viewing a customer's profile can quickly identify which pricing rules or lists apply to them without needing to navigate away from the page.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When you open a customer's profile, the system automatically requests the relevant pricing information associated with that specific customer.
2.Loading State: While the system is gathering this information, a temporary loading indicator is displayed to let you know the data is being retrieved.
3.Display: Once the data is ready, the system displays the "Prices" section header, marked with a price icon for easy identification.
4.Information Presentation: The system shows the name of the assigned "Pricelist" (such as "Default Wholesale Price") as a read-only field. If no specific pricelist is assigned, the system will indicate that the information is not available.
5.Error Handling: If the system encounters an issue while trying to fetch the pricing data, it will display a clear message notifying you that the information could not be loaded.
1.3Key Concepts
–Pricelist: A predefined set of pricing rules or rates assigned to a customer, which determines the costs applied to their orders.
–Read-only Field: A piece of information that is displayed for reference purposes only and cannot be edited directly within this specific view.
–Loading State: A visual placeholder that appears while the system is communicating with the database to ensure you know the page is still working.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing critical financial information. By providing immediate visibility into a customer's assigned pricelist, sales and support staff can:

* Reduce Errors: Ensure the correct pricing is applied to orders by verifying the customer's status instantly.

* Save Time: Eliminate the need to search through multiple screens or external documents to confirm pricing agreements.

* Enhance Transparency: Provide consistent information across the team, ensuring everyone is aligned on the pricing terms for each specific customer.
