1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Franchisee Customer Detail Page provides a centralized, read-only view of a specific franchisee customer's profile within the Lumi ERP system. This page allows staff to quickly review essential business information, tax details, pricing, and account settings for a selected customer, ensuring that all relevant data is easily accessible in one location.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading Data: When a user selects a specific customer, the system automatically retrieves that customer's full profile from the database.
2.Navigation & Context: The page displays a navigation bar and sub-menu to keep the user oriented within the "Customers" section. A "Customer" button is available to return the user to the main customer list at any time.
3.Header Display: The page header prominently displays the customer's name and provides an "Edit" button for users who need to update the information.
4.Information Layout: The customer's data is organized into specific, logical sections (cards) for easy reading:

* Details: Core identity, contact information, and assigned salesperson.

* Customer Group: The specific group classification for the customer.

* Tax Information: Essential tax identifiers and registration numbers.

* Settings: Account status, credit terms, and specific business flags (e.g., franchise or wholesale status).

* Prices: The specific price list assigned to this customer.

* Data Mapping: A summary of the salesperson assigned to the account.

* Changes: An audit trail showing when the record was created or updated and by whom.

5.Tab Navigation: A dedicated tab menu allows the user to switch between different customer-related views (such as Retail Customers) without leaving the context of the franchisee menu.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: A display mode where information is presented for review without allowing direct changes, ensuring data integrity until the "Edit" function is triggered.
–Audit Trail: A record of "Changes" that tracks the history of the customer profile, including timestamps and the users responsible for creating or modifying the data.
–Data Mapping: The process of organizing raw system data into clear, labeled categories (cards) so that users can find specific information like tax IDs or credit terms quickly.
–Franchisee Context: The specific business logic that filters and displays information relevant to franchise-based customer relationships.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by eliminating the need to search through multiple screens to find customer information. By consolidating identity, tax, pricing, and account history into a single, well-organized view, staff can make faster, more informed decisions regarding customer management and support. The inclusion of an audit trail also enhances accountability and transparency by clearly showing the history of any updates made to a customer's profile.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Franchisee Customer Detail page provides a comprehensive, read-only view of a specific customer's profile within the Lumi ERP system. When a user navigates to this page, the system automatically retrieves all relevant information for that customer and organizes it into logical sections, allowing staff to quickly review account details, tax information, credit terms, and administrative history.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When the page is opened, the system identifies the specific customer from the web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system requests the full profile for that customer from the database.
3.Error Handling: If the customer cannot be found, the system displays a "not found" message. If any other technical issue occurs during the request, an error message is shown to the user.
4.Information Mapping: Once the data is successfully retrieved, the system automatically sorts the information into specific cards on the screen:

* Details Card: Displays contact information and the assigned salesperson.

* Group Card: Shows the customer’s assigned group.

* Tax Information Card: Displays tax and VAT identification numbers.

* Settings Card: Shows the current account status and credit terms.

* Prices Card: Displays the specific price list assigned to the customer.

* Changes Card: Provides an audit trail, including when the record was created or updated, who performed those actions, and the original source of the data.

2.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: The page is designed for viewing information; data cannot be edited directly from this screen.
–Audit Trail: A record of "who" and "when" regarding the creation and modification of the customer profile, ensuring transparency and accountability.
–Account Status: A high-level indicator of the customer's current standing within the system (e.g., active, inactive, or on hold).
–Credit Terms: The specific payment conditions or credit limits agreed upon for that franchisee.
2.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical customer data, reducing the time staff spend searching for information across different systems. By providing a clear, organized view of a franchisee's profile—including their tax status, credit terms, and assigned salesperson—the page enables faster, more informed decision-making for customer support and account management teams. The inclusion of audit history also ensures that staff can easily verify the origin and maintenance history of customer records.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Franchisee Customer Detail Page feature provides a comprehensive, read-only view of a specific customer’s profile within the Lumi ERP system. This page acts as a centralized hub, pulling together all relevant business information—such as contact details, tax status, credit terms, and pricing assignments—to give staff a complete snapshot of a franchisee customer.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user navigates to a specific franchisee customer’s profile, the system identifies the unique customer ID from the page address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system requests the full customer record from the database using that unique ID.
3.Validation: The system checks if the customer exists. If the ID is invalid or the record is missing, the system displays a "Customer Not Found" message.
4.Data Organization: Once the record is retrieved, the system organizes the information into logical categories (such as identity, tax, settings, and pricing) to ensure the data is presented clearly on the screen.
5.Display: The organized data is populated into the corresponding read-only cards on the page, allowing the user to review the customer's full profile.
3.3Key Concepts
–Franchisee Customer: A specific type of business partner within the ERP that requires detailed tracking for sales, tax, and credit purposes.
–Read-Only View: A display mode where information is presented for review purposes only, ensuring data integrity by preventing accidental changes while viewing.
–Audit Trail: A collection of tracking information (such as who created or updated the record and when) that provides transparency into the history of the customer profile.
–Account Status: A setting that indicates the current standing of the customer, helping staff quickly identify if an account is active or restricted.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines daily operations by eliminating the need to search through multiple screens or systems to find customer information. By consolidating contact details, financial settings, and pricing data into one view, staff can make faster, more informed decisions regarding customer service, credit management, and sales support. The inclusion of audit data also ensures that the team maintains clear accountability and visibility over how and when customer records are managed.
