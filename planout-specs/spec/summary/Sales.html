1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Sales module in the Lumi ERP system is designed to manage the entire lifecycle of a sale, from the initial price offer to the final payment. It supports two primary business models: retail/e-commerce transactions and wholesale orders. The system ensures data consistency across these processes by linking related documents (like orders, deliveries, and invoices) and maintaining accurate records of inventory movement, customer billing, and returns.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Quotation & Ordering: The process often begins with a `SalesQuotation` (a price offer). Once accepted, this is converted into a `SalesOrder` for wholesale customers, or a `Sale` record for retail/POS transactions.
2.Fulfillment: For wholesale orders, the system tracks the dispatch of goods via `SalesDelivery` records. These are linked to the original order to ensure that the quantity delivered does not exceed the quantity ordered.
3.Invoicing: Once goods are delivered, a `SalesInvoice` is generated. This document links back to the delivery and the original order to maintain a clear audit trail.
4.Accounts Receivable (AR): The invoice creates an `InvoiceReceivable` record, which tracks the outstanding balance.
5.Payment: When a customer makes a payment, an `ARPayment` is recorded. This payment is then linked to the specific `InvoiceReceivable` via an `ARPaymentAllocation` to mark the invoice as paid.
6.Returns & Adjustments: If goods are returned, the system uses `SaleReturn` (for retail) or `SalesOrderReturn` (for wholesale) to process the return, update inventory, and issue a `CreditNote` to adjust the customer's balance.
1.3Key Concepts
–Denormalization: The system stores copies of certain data (like customer names or product SKUs) directly within transaction tables. This allows the system to display information quickly without needing to look up the original record every time.
–Workflow State: A status tracker (e.g., "Draft," "Confirmed," "Delivered," "Paid") that ensures documents move through the business process in the correct order.
–Polymorphic Entities: Certain records, like `Claim` or `CreditNote`, can link to multiple different types of documents (e.g., a claim could be linked to a `Sale` or a `Service`), providing flexibility in how issues are tracked.
–Trigger-Maintained Fields: Automated background rules that update totals or quantities (such as `quantity_delivered`) whenever a related document is created, preventing manual errors and ensuring data integrity.
–AR Ageing: The process of tracking how long an invoice has been outstanding, managed through the `InvoiceReceivable` table to help the business monitor cash flow.
1.4Business Value
–End-to-End Traceability: By linking every document from the initial quote to the final payment, the system provides a complete audit trail, making it easy to resolve customer disputes or investigate discrepancies.
–Operational Efficiency: Automated tracking of delivered and invoiced quantities prevents common errors like over-shipping or double-billing.
–Improved Cash Flow Visibility: The integrated Accounts Receivable and payment allocation features provide real-time insight into outstanding customer balances and expected revenue.
–Performance Optimization: By using denormalized data and indexed lookups, the system ensures that sales reports and search queries remain fast, even as the volume of transactions grows.
