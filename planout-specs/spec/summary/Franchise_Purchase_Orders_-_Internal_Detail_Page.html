1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal Detail Page provides a centralized view for managing internal stock requests between warehouses and stores. This page allows authorized users to review the specifics of a purchase order, track its progress through the supply chain, and perform necessary actions to move the order toward completion.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Loading: When you open a specific purchase order, the system automatically retrieves the order details. It verifies that the order is an "internal" franchise request before displaying the information.
2.Navigation & Status: The page displays the main navigation menu and a sub-menu set to the "Store" tab. A status bar at the top shows the current stage of the order (e.g., Draft, Confirmed, Sent, or Received).
3.Reviewing Details: The "Details" panel provides a summary of the order, including reference numbers, warehouse and store locations, key dates, and any relevant notes.
4.Itemized Breakdown: The main table lists every product included in the order, showing quantities ordered, sent, and received, along with pricing and specific remarks from both the store and the warehouse.
5.Summary Calculation: The table automatically calculates the totals for all quantities and monetary amounts at the bottom, providing an immediate overview of the order's scale.
6.Action Management: Depending on the order's current status, you can use the action buttons to edit the order, export the item list to an Excel file, or return to the main list of purchase orders.
1.3Key Concepts
–Workflow Status: A visual tracker that shows the lifecycle of an order, ensuring users know exactly where the request stands in the fulfillment process.
–Internal Detail Panel: A summary section containing administrative information such as document codes, involved parties (warehouse/store), and important timestamps.
–Itemized Table: A detailed grid that breaks down the order by product, allowing for precise tracking of quantities and remarks for each individual item.
–Tabbed Navigation: A structure that organizes information into logical categories (Details, Invoice, and Returns), with the "Details" tab serving as the primary view.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines internal inventory management by providing a single source of truth for franchise purchase orders. By clearly displaying the status and itemized details of each order, it reduces communication errors between warehouses and stores, ensures accurate tracking of stock movement, and simplifies the review process for store managers and warehouse staff. The ability to export data and easily navigate between order states helps maintain operational efficiency and accountability.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal Detail page provides a comprehensive view of specific internal stock orders. It allows users to review order details, track the current progress of the order through its lifecycle, and manage the items associated with that order. This page ensures that all relevant information—from supplier details to specific product quantities—is centralized for easy verification and management.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user selects a specific purchase order, the system retrieves the order details.
2.Validation: The system checks that the selected order is categorized as an "internal" franchise order. If it is not, the system will not display the details.
3.Data Mapping: Once validated, the system organizes the information into three main areas:

* Header Panel: Displays administrative details such as document numbers, warehouse/store locations, dates, and notes.

* Workflow Status: Shows the current stage of the order (e.g., Draft, Confirmed, Sent, or Received).

* Items Table: Lists every product in the order, including quantities (ordered, sent, and received), pricing, and any specific remarks from the store or warehouse.

4.Summary Calculation: The system automatically calculates totals for quantities and monetary amounts based on the items listed in the table to provide a quick overview of the order's scale.
2.3Key Concepts
–Internal Purchase Order: A specific type of stock request used for moving inventory between internal franchise locations or warehouses.
–Workflow State: The current phase of the order, which guides the user on what actions (such as confirming, sending, or receiving) are available next.
–Header Panel: The top section of the page containing metadata, such as reference codes, timestamps, and the parties involved in the transaction.
–Items Table: A detailed breakdown of the products, showing the lifecycle of each item from the initial order quantity to the final received amount.
–Supply Price (Inc. Tax): The cost of the product per unit, including all applicable taxes, used to calculate the total order value.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management by providing a single source of truth for internal franchise orders. By automating the calculation of totals and clearly displaying the workflow status, it reduces manual errors and helps staff quickly identify the status of their stock. This transparency ensures that both the warehouse and the store are aligned on what has been ordered, sent, and received, ultimately leading to more efficient stock replenishment and better operational oversight.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Order Internal Detail page provides a comprehensive view of a specific purchase order within the Lumi ERP system. This page is designed to give internal staff full visibility into the lifecycle of an order—from the initial request and warehouse fulfillment to the final receipt at the store. It consolidates administrative details, current workflow status, and a detailed breakdown of products, quantities, and financial totals.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the complete details for a specific purchase order, including header information, status, and a list of all items involved.
2.Header Display: The page populates the top section with key administrative data, such as document numbers, warehouse and store locations, supplier information, and important dates (creation, due date, and fulfillment timestamps).
3.Workflow Status: The system displays the current stage of the order (e.g., pending, sent, or received) to ensure staff understand the order's progress.
4.Itemized Breakdown: The system lists every product in the order, showing specific details like the SKU, product name, and individual quantities for items ordered, sent, and received.
5.Financial Summary: The system automatically calculates and displays the totals at the bottom of the item table, providing a clear view of the total quantities and monetary values for the entire order.
3.3Key Concepts
–Workflow State: The current phase of the purchase order, which helps track whether the order is still being processed, has been shipped, or has been completed.
–Header Details: The high-level metadata of the order, including reference codes, responsible parties, and relevant dates that define the order's context.
–Itemized Quantities: A three-way comparison of product counts (Ordered vs. Sent vs. Received) that allows staff to identify discrepancies between what was requested and what was actually delivered.
–Tax-Inclusive Pricing: Financial values displayed on the page include applicable taxes, ensuring that the "Order," "Sent," and "Receive" amounts reflect the actual cost.
–Summary Totals: Aggregated calculations at the bottom of the item list that provide an immediate snapshot of the total volume and financial value of the purchase order.
3.4Business Value

This feature centralizes all critical information regarding a franchise purchase order into a single, easy-to-read interface. By providing a clear comparison between ordered, sent, and received quantities, it enables staff to quickly identify fulfillment gaps or errors. The automated summary totals reduce the need for manual calculations, improving operational efficiency and ensuring that internal teams have the accurate, real-time data needed to manage inventory and supplier relationships effectively.
