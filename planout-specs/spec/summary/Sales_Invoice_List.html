1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Sales Invoice List feature provides a centralized dashboard for finance teams to view, search, and manage all sales invoices within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to quickly locate specific invoices using various filters, organize the data by clicking on column headers, and navigate directly to detailed records for further action.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When you open the Invoices page, the system automatically fetches the list of invoices using default sorting and pagination settings.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list by entering criteria into the search bar (such as Invoice Number, Purchase Order, Sales Order, or Customer Name) or by selecting specific dates and statuses.
3.Applying Search: Once your filters are set, clicking the "Search" button refreshes the list, resets the view to the first page, and displays only the records that match your criteria.
4.Sorting: You can click on any column header (e.g., Invoice Date, Total, or Status) to sort the list. Clicking once sorts the data in ascending order; clicking again reverses it to descending order.
5.Pagination: If there are many invoices, you can use the pagination controls at the bottom of the table to move between pages.
6.Navigation: You can click on specific items within a row—such as the Invoice Number, Sales Order, or Customer Name—to be taken directly to the corresponding details page for that record.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filters: Tools that allow you to refine the list of invoices based on specific data points like dates, status, or reference numbers.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of records into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
–Sorting: The ability to reorder the list based on a specific column, making it easier to find the most recent or highest-value invoices.
–Mapping: The process where the system takes raw data from the database and presents it in a user-friendly table format.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the finance workflow by reducing the time spent searching for documentation. By providing a responsive, searchable, and sortable list, users can quickly reconcile accounts, track invoice statuses, and access related customer or order information without navigating through multiple screens. This improves overall operational efficiency and ensures that financial data is always easily accessible.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales Invoice List feature provides a centralized view for users to browse, search, and manage sales invoices within the Lumi ERP system. It is designed to handle large volumes of data efficiently by using pagination and sorting, ensuring that users can quickly locate specific financial documents without performance delays.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the user opens the Sales Invoice page, the system automatically fetches the first page of invoices using default settings (sorted by issue date).
2.Data Retrieval: The system requests the invoice data from the database, applying any active filters, sorting preferences, or pagination settings.
3.Data Mapping: The raw information retrieved from the system is translated into a user-friendly table format, ensuring key details like invoice numbers, customer names, dates, and totals are clearly displayed.
4.Search Execution: When a user enters a search term and clicks the search button, the system clears the current view, resets the pagination to the first page, and fetches a new list of results matching the search criteria.
5.Display: The final processed data is rendered in the table, accompanied by pagination controls that allow the user to navigate through the full list of invoices.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of invoices into smaller, manageable pages (defaulting to 20 items per page) to improve loading speed and readability.
–Search Filtering: The ability to narrow down the invoice list by entering specific keywords, which triggers a fresh request to the system to find relevant matches.
–Sorting: The organization of invoices based on specific criteria, such as the issue date, to help users prioritize or find recent entries.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking raw system data and converting it into the specific columns and labels shown in the user interface.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the financial workflow by providing a responsive and intuitive interface for invoice management. By enabling quick searches and organized browsing, it reduces the time administrative staff spend locating documents, minimizes manual errors, and ensures that users always have access to the most up-to-date status of their sales invoices.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales Invoice List feature provides a centralized view for users to browse, search, and manage all sales invoices within the Lumi ERP system. This feature is designed to help finance teams quickly locate specific billing records by offering flexible search options, organized sorting, and a paginated view to ensure the interface remains fast and easy to navigate even as the number of invoices grows.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When you open the Sales Invoice page or perform a search, the system collects your criteria, such as search keywords, your preferred page number, and sorting preferences.
2.Filtering: The system scans the invoice database for matches based on the invoice number or the customer's name.
3.Sorting: The results are organized based on your chosen criteria (e.g., by date or amount). If no preference is selected, the system defaults to showing the most recently issued invoices first.
4.Pagination: To keep the list manageable, the system divides the invoices into smaller groups (pages). It calculates which specific set of records to display based on the page number you are viewing.
5.Data Delivery: The system compiles the requested list of invoices along with the total count of records found, ensuring you have a clear overview of your search results.
3.3Key Concepts
–Search Filter: A tool that allows you to find specific invoices by typing in either a unique invoice number or a customer's name.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of invoices into smaller, readable pages to improve performance and user experience.
–Sorting: The ability to arrange the list in a specific order, such as by date or total amount, to help you prioritize your work.
–Default View: The initial state of the page, which automatically displays the most recent invoices in a standard, easy-to-read format.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines financial operations by reducing the time spent manually searching for billing documents. By providing a responsive and searchable list, finance staff can quickly verify invoice statuses, track customer billing history, and maintain accurate records. The inclusion of pagination and sorting ensures that the system remains efficient and user-friendly, regardless of the volume of sales data being managed.
