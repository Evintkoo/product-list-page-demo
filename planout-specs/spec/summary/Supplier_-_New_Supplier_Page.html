1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "New Supplier" page is a dedicated interface within the Lumi ERP system designed to capture all necessary information to register a new supplier. This page ensures that essential business data—such as contact details, tax information, and credit terms—is accurately recorded and linked to the correct financial accounts before being saved to the system.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Entry: The user fills out the form, which includes mandatory fields (marked with an asterisk) such as Supplier Name, Contact Person, Phone Number, Address, Country, Credit Terms, and Sales Tax rules.
2.Optional Information: The user may provide additional details, including the supplier code, tax/registration numbers, communication details (fax, mobile, email), credit limits, and internal notes.
3.Configuration Settings: The user sets operational flags, such as whether the supplier is currently active, if they are an internal entity, and if they are permitted to receive store-level purchase orders.
4.Financial Mapping: The user selects the appropriate financial accounts for invoices, credit notes, down payments, and deposits to ensure proper accounting integration.
5.Validation: The system monitors the form in real-time. The "Save" button remains disabled until all mandatory fields are correctly completed.
6.Submission: Upon clicking "Save," the system packages the entered data into a structured format and sends it to the ERP database. If the user decides not to proceed, they can click "Cancel" to exit the page without saving.
1.3Key Concepts
–Mandatory Fields: Essential information required by the system to ensure every supplier record is complete and functional.
–Real-time Validation: A feature that checks the form as you type, preventing submission if required information is missing or incomplete.
–Financial Account Mapping: The process of linking a supplier to specific accounting categories (like invoices or deposits), which automates how the system handles future transactions with that supplier.
–Operational Flags: Checkboxes that define the supplier's status and permissions, such as whether they are "Active" or authorized to interact with specific store purchase orders.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the onboarding process for new suppliers by providing a structured, error-proof interface. By enforcing data entry at the point of creation and linking suppliers directly to the correct financial accounts, the system reduces manual data entry errors, ensures consistent accounting practices, and allows for immediate, seamless integration of the new supplier into the broader procurement and purchasing workflows.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The "New Supplier" page allows users to register a new supplier in the Lumi ERP system. This feature provides a structured form to capture essential business details—such as contact information, tax identifiers, credit terms, and accounting settings—ensuring that all necessary data is accurately recorded and saved to the system database.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Entry: The user fills out the "New Supplier" form with relevant details, including the supplier's name, contact information, financial settings, and internal notes.
2.Submission: The user clicks the "Save" button to finalize the entry.
3.Processing: The system triggers a loading state to indicate that the information is being processed.
4.Data Mapping: The system automatically translates the information entered in the form into the specific format required by the internal database.
5.Validation & Storage: The system sends the formatted data to the server.
–If the data is successfully saved, the system displays a confirmation message to the user.
–If an error occurs (e.g., missing required information), the system notifies the user that the save was unsuccessful.
6.Completion: The loading state is removed, returning the user to the interface.
2.3Key Concepts
–Supplier Profile: The collection of business information (contact, tax, and financial) required to manage a relationship with a vendor.
–Data Mapping: The behind-the-scenes process that ensures the information typed into the form fields is correctly labeled and organized for the system's database.
–Loading State: A visual indicator (such as a spinner or disabled button) that prevents duplicate submissions while the system is busy saving data.
–Feedback Notification: Alerts provided to the user to confirm that a supplier was created successfully or to warn them if an issue occurred during the process.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the onboarding of new vendors by providing a centralized, standardized entry point. By capturing comprehensive financial and operational data at the point of creation, the system ensures that accounting and procurement teams have the accurate information needed to process invoices, manage credit limits, and maintain tax compliance from day one.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The New Supplier Page is a dedicated interface within the Lumi ERP system designed to streamline the onboarding of new business partners. This feature allows users to input essential contact and identification details for a supplier, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and stored securely in our database to support ongoing procurement and financial operations.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Entry: The user enters the supplier's name, tax identification number, email address, phone number, and physical address into the system.
2.Uniqueness Check: The system automatically verifies that the provided tax identification number is not already associated with an existing supplier to prevent duplicate records.
3.Record Creation: Once the information is validated, the system assigns a unique internal identifier and a timestamp to the entry.
4.Status Assignment: The system automatically sets the supplier's status to "Active," making them immediately available for use within the ERP.
5.Confirmation: The completed supplier profile is saved to the database, and the system confirms the successful creation of the record.
3.3Key Concepts
–Tax Identification Number: A unique government-issued identifier used to distinguish one business entity from another for tax and regulatory purposes.
–Unique Identifier: A system-generated code that ensures every supplier record is distinct and can be referenced accurately across the platform.
–Active Status: A default setting applied to new suppliers that indicates they are ready to be engaged for business transactions immediately upon registration.
3.4Business Value

This feature provides a standardized and reliable method for adding new suppliers to the Lumi ERP. By automating the uniqueness check for tax IDs, the system prevents data duplication and maintains high data integrity. This efficiency reduces manual administrative work and ensures that procurement teams have immediate access to accurate supplier information, ultimately accelerating the onboarding process and improving operational accuracy.
