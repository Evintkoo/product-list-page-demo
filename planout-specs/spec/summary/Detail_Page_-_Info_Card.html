1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Service Product Info Card is a clean, read-only display component designed for the Lumi ERP system. Its primary purpose is to provide a quick, organized summary of a specific service product’s core information. By presenting these details in a structured layout, users can easily reference essential product data without the risk of accidental changes.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The card begins with a "DETAILS" header, marked by a blue badge and a document icon to clearly identify the section.
2.Name: The system displays the official name of the service product.
3.Tags: The system lists relevant categories or labels associated with the product, styled as distinct, dark-colored badges for quick visual scanning.
4.Type: The system shows the specific classification or type of the service product.
5.Description: The system provides a detailed summary or explanation of what the service product entails.
6.SKU: The system displays the unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) code used for inventory and identification purposes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: Information is presented for viewing purposes only; users cannot edit or modify these fields directly within this component.
–Pill Badge: A visual design element used for tags, allowing multiple categories to be grouped together in a compact, easy-to-read format.
–SKU: A unique identifier assigned to a service product to help track it within the ERP system.
–Info Grid: A structured layout that aligns labels with their corresponding data, ensuring the information is easy to scan and understand.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a standardized way to view service product details. By centralizing key information—such as the SKU, type, and description—in a consistent format, staff can quickly verify product specifications during daily tasks. The read-only nature of the card ensures data integrity, preventing unauthorized or accidental modifications while maintaining a clean and professional user interface.
