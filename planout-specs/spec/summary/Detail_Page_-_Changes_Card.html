1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Changes Card" is a dedicated information panel located on the service product detail page. Its purpose is to provide transparency and accountability by displaying a clear audit trail of who created or modified a specific record and when those actions took place.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The card displays a "CHANGES" label accompanied by a clock icon to signify that the information relates to the record's history.
2.Creation Details: The system displays the name and email address of the user who originally created the record, followed by the exact date and time of creation.
3.Update Details: The system displays the name and email address of the user who most recently modified the record, followed by the exact date and time of that last update.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record of activities that provides evidence of who performed an action and when it occurred.
–Read-Only Fields: Information presented for viewing purposes only; these fields cannot be edited by the user, ensuring the integrity of the historical data.
–User Identification: A combined display format that shows both the full name and email address of a user to ensure there is no ambiguity regarding who performed an action.
–Standardized Timestamp: A consistent date and time format (e.g., 09-Jun-2024 09:40 PM) used to ensure clarity across all records.
1.4Business Value
–Accountability: By clearly identifying who created or updated a service product, the organization can easily track responsibility for data entries.
–Transparency: Users can quickly verify the age and maintenance status of a record without needing to consult external logs or administrative reports.
–Improved Troubleshooting: If data discrepancies arise, the audit trail allows team members to identify exactly when a change occurred and who to contact for clarification.
