1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Barcode Display feature allows the Lumi ERP system to generate and visualize barcodes based on specific label formats. It ensures that any barcode entered into the system is correctly formatted and compatible with the physical label templates used in your warehouse or retail operations.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Input Validation: When a user enters a barcode, the system immediately checks it against three rules: it cannot be empty, it must contain only letters and numbers, and it must be between 8 and 20 characters long.
2.Error Handling: If the input does not meet these rules, the system displays a clear error message to the user, preventing invalid data from being processed.
3.Template Selection: Once the barcode is validated, the system identifies the chosen label template (such as Avery or continuous roll formats).
4.Rendering: The system applies the barcode data to the selected visual template, ensuring the final output matches the physical layout of your labels.
1.3Key Concepts
–Barcode Validation: A built-in safety check that ensures all barcode data is consistent, readable, and follows standard length and character requirements.
–Template Mapping: The ability of the system to automatically adjust the visual layout of a barcode to fit different physical label sizes and types.
–Alphanumeric: A requirement that the barcode must consist only of letters and numbers, excluding special symbols or punctuation to ensure compatibility with scanners.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the labeling process by removing guesswork and manual formatting. By enforcing strict validation rules, it prevents printing errors that could lead to unreadable barcodes. Furthermore, by supporting multiple label templates, the system provides the flexibility to switch between different label stocks without needing to reconfigure the software, saving time and reducing operational friction.
