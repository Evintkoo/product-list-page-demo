1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Service Product Type feature allows the Lumi ERP system to categorize services consistently. By defining these types, the system ensures that every service is correctly linked to the appropriate accounting records, which automates financial tracking and tax reporting (specifically for PPh 23 refunds) whenever a service is performed.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Definition: A new service category is created with a name, description, and links to specific accounting accounts.
2.Accounting Mapping: The system assigns a "Service Account" to the category to track revenue or costs in the general ledger, and optionally assigns a "Refund Account" for tax-related adjustments.
3.Activation: The category is marked as "active," making it immediately available for selection in service-related forms and dropdown menus.
4.Maintenance: Users can update existing categories or deactivate them if they are no longer in use, ensuring the system only shows relevant options.
5.Retrieval: When viewing service lists, the system automatically pulls the active categories and displays their associated accounting names for clarity.
1.3Key Concepts
–Service Product Type: A classification label used to group similar services (e.g., "Maintenance," "Repair," or "Consulting") for better organization and reporting.
–Chart of Accounts (COA): The master list of financial accounts used by the business; this feature links service types to these accounts to ensure money is recorded in the right place.
–Service Account: The specific accounting entry that tracks the financial activity of a particular service type.
–Refund Account: A dedicated accounting entry used to manage specific tax refunds, such as PPh 23, associated with a service.
–Active Status: A toggle that controls whether a service type appears in the system's dropdown menus, allowing you to hide outdated categories without deleting their historical data.
1.4Business Value
–Financial Accuracy: By mandating a link to the Chart of Accounts, the system eliminates manual data entry errors, ensuring that every service transaction is automatically mapped to the correct financial ledger.
–Streamlined Reporting: Categorizing services allows management to easily generate reports on which service types are performing best or incurring the most costs.
–Tax Compliance: The built-in mapping for refund accounts simplifies the complex process of handling tax withholdings like PPh 23, reducing the risk of errors during tax season.
–Operational Efficiency: Standardizing service types ensures that all team members use consistent terminology, making the system easier to navigate and maintain.
