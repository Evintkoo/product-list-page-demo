1.1Overview

The Product feature serves as the central foundation for the Lumi ERP system. It acts as a master directory for all items, managing their identity, classification, pricing, and inventory status. By linking products to specific brands, types, and locations (stores and warehouses), the system ensures that every item can be tracked accurately from purchase through to final sale.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Classification: Every product is assigned a specific brand and type to keep the catalog organized and searchable.
2.Definition: Core details—such as SKU, name, pricing, and business unit—are established in the central product directory.
3.Customization: The system allows for flexible labeling of product attributes (like category or car type) to match your company’s specific terminology.
4.Inventory Tracking: Stock levels are monitored separately for stores and warehouses, with dedicated history logs that record every movement (such as sales or deliveries).
5.Pricing & Audit: The system tracks price changes and purchase history, ensuring there is a clear record of how much an item cost and how it has been priced over time.
6.Voucher Management: Special items, such as vouchers, are managed with their own set of rules regarding validity, redemption locations, and expiration dates.
1.3Key Concepts
–Product Directory: The main database table that holds all essential information about an item, acting as the "single source of truth."
–Inventory Tracking: The process of monitoring how many units of a product are available at specific physical locations (stores or warehouses).
–Stock History: A digital audit trail that records every time a product's quantity changes, including the reason for the change (e.g., a sale or a new delivery).
–Pricing Overrides: The ability to set different prices for the same product depending on the context, such as the specific store, region, or customer group.
–Custom Attributes: Flexible fields that allow you to categorize products using your own business terminology without needing a system redesign.
–Vouchers: A specialized product type that includes unique rules for validity periods and where they can be issued or redeemed.
1.4Business Value
–Operational Accuracy: By centralizing product data and inventory, you reduce the risk of errors in stock counts and pricing across different locations.
–Better Decision Making: With detailed history logs for both stock and pricing, you can easily analyze sales trends and purchase patterns to optimize your inventory levels.
–Flexibility: The system adapts to your business needs through custom attributes and location-based pricing, allowing you to scale or change your product strategy without technical friction.
–Audit Readiness: Comprehensive history tables provide a clear, transparent record of all transactions, making it easier to manage reporting and compliance.
