1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Product feature serves as the central hub for managing your inventory within the Mini ERP system. It acts as a master registry that connects various operational details—such as branding, categorization, pricing, and stock levels across different locations—into a single, organized view. This structure ensures that every product has a unique identity and that all related business activities, like sales history, warehouse movements, and purchase records, are accurately tracked and linked.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Classification: Products are organized by specific types and brands to ensure consistent reporting and categorization.
2.Stock Management: The system tracks inventory levels across both physical stores and warehouses, maintaining a detailed history of stock changes (such as sales or transfers).
3.Pricing & History: The system records price adjustments and maintains a comprehensive history of product updates, including who made changes and when.
4.Operational Tracking: As products are purchased or tagged for specific purposes, these activities are logged and linked back to the main product record.
5.Central Registry: All the above information converges in the main Products table, which serves as the "source of truth" for every item in your catalog, identified by a unique SKU.
1.3Key Concepts
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique identifier for each product, ensuring no two items are confused in the system.
–Product Registry: The central database table that holds the core details of every product, such as its name, description, and status.
–Stock Tracking: The ability to monitor quantities in real-time across different locations (warehouses and stores) and log the reasons for any quantity changes.
–Relationship Mapping: The way the system links different data points—like purchase history or pricing—to a specific product, allowing you to see the full lifecycle of an item.
–Audit Trail: The recording of historical data (such as price changes or stock movements) to provide transparency on how and when product information was modified.
1.4Business Value
–Data Accuracy: By using unique identifiers and standardized categories, you eliminate duplicate entries and ensure consistent product information across the entire organization.
–Operational Visibility: You gain a clear, real-time view of your inventory levels across multiple locations, helping you make informed decisions about restocking and distribution.
–Improved Reporting: Because all pricing, purchase, and stock history is linked to the product, you can easily generate reports on product performance, profitability, and supply chain trends.
–Scalability: The structured design allows you to easily add new brands, product types, or store locations without disrupting existing data, supporting your business as it grows.
