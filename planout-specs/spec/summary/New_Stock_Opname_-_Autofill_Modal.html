1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Autofill Stock Opname" modal is a productivity tool designed to help users quickly populate their stock-taking (opname) forms. Instead of manually adding items one by one, users can define specific criteria—such as product category, brand, or supplier—to automatically pull a list of relevant products into their current stock opname session.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Open the Modal: The user initiates the autofill process from the New Stock Opname form.
2.Define Filters: The user selects specific criteria to narrow down the product list, including:

* Product Type, Brand, and Supplier: Using dropdown menus to filter by source or classification.

* Product Info: Selecting a category (e.g., Item Category, Business Unit, or Car Type) and entering a specific starting value.

3.Set Display Preferences: The user chooses how the results should be sorted (by SKU or Name) and whether to include items currently showing zero stock.
4.Define Movement Criteria: The user specifies a timeframe (in days) to filter products based on recent activity.
5.Validation: The system checks that the "Movement Days" entry is a valid, non-negative number.
6.Execute and Populate: Upon clicking "Save," the system retrieves the matching products and automatically adds them to the main Stock Opname form, closing the modal in the process.
7.Cancel: If the user chooses to cancel, all selections are discarded, and the user is returned to the main form without any changes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Stock Opname: The process of verifying physical inventory against system records.
–Autofill: A feature that automates data entry by fetching a group of items based on user-defined rules rather than manual selection.
–Movement Days: A filter that allows users to focus on items that have had activity (sales, receipts, or adjustments) within a specific recent period.
–Zero Stock Toggle: A setting that allows users to decide if they want to include items that are currently out of stock in their inventory count.
1.4Business Value
–Efficiency: Significantly reduces the time spent manually searching for and adding individual products to a stock-taking list.
–Accuracy: Minimizes human error by ensuring that the list of products is generated based on consistent, system-defined criteria.
–Flexibility: Provides granular control over which inventory items are included in a count, allowing teams to perform targeted stock checks (e.g., by brand or by recent movement) rather than always performing a full-warehouse count.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The "Stock Opname Autofill" feature is designed to simplify the inventory counting process by allowing users to quickly pull a specific list of products into their stock-taking sheet. Instead of manually searching for and adding items one by one, users can define specific criteria—such as product category, brand, or supplier—and the system will automatically generate a list of relevant products to be counted.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Define Criteria: The user selects specific filters in the modal, such as the product type, brand, or supplier.
2.Refine Search: The user provides additional details, such as specific keywords or product information, to narrow down the list further.
3.Set Preferences: The user chooses how to sort the results and decides whether to include items that currently have zero stock or items based on their recent movement history (e.g., items that have moved in the last 30 days).
4.Data Processing: The system collects all these selections and sends them as a request to the inventory database.
5.Autofill Execution: The system retrieves the matching products and automatically populates the stock opname sheet with the filtered list.
2.3Key Concepts
–Stock Opname: The process of physically counting inventory to ensure the actual stock matches the system records.
–Autofill Criteria: The set of filters (like brand or supplier) used to narrow down which products need to be counted.
–Movement Days: A filter that identifies products based on how recently they were sold or moved, helping users focus on active inventory.
–Zero Stock Inclusion: A toggle that allows users to decide if they want to include items that are currently out of stock in their count.
2.4Business Value
–Increased Efficiency: Reduces the time spent manually searching for and adding products to a stock-taking list.
–Improved Accuracy: Minimizes human error by ensuring the correct subset of products is pulled based on predefined business rules.
–Better Inventory Control: Allows staff to perform targeted stock counts (e.g., counting only high-movement items or specific brands), leading to more frequent and manageable inventory audits.
