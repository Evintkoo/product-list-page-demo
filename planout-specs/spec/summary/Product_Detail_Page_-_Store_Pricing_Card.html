1.1Overview

The Store Pricing Card is a dedicated section on the product detail page designed to provide clear, at-a-glance financial information for store managers. It displays the essential costs and pricing associated with a specific product, ensuring that both the purchase cost and the final selling price are transparent and easy to reference.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the specific pricing data for the selected product.
2.Displaying Supply Price: The card shows the "Supply Price," which represents the cost the store pays to acquire the product. A help icon is provided to clarify that this figure includes tax.
3.Displaying Retail Price: The card shows the "Retail Price," which is the price at which the product is sold to customers.
4.Displaying Tax Details: Alongside the retail price, the system automatically displays the tax-inclusive amount, ensuring the user understands the final cost structure.
5.Formatting: All financial figures are automatically formatted as Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for consistency and readability.
1.3Key Concepts
–Supply Price: The cost price of the product from the store's perspective, inclusive of taxes.
–Retail Price: The final price set for the product to be sold to the end customer.
–Tax-Inclusive Amount: The total price of the product after all applicable taxes have been added, displayed alongside the retail price for clarity.
–Read-Only Fields: These values are for informational purposes only and cannot be edited directly within this specific component.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing critical financial data directly on the product page. By clearly separating the store's acquisition cost from the customer's retail price, store managers can quickly assess profit margins and verify pricing accuracy without needing to navigate to separate financial reports or spreadsheets.
