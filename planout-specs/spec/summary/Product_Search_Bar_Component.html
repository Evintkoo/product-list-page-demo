1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Search Bar is a comprehensive filtering tool designed for the product page of our Mini ERP system. It allows users to quickly locate specific items by entering identifying details or selecting predefined categories. By providing a mix of text-based search fields and structured dropdown menus, the component ensures that users can perform both broad and highly specific product lookups with ease.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Input Entry: As you type or select criteria in any of the fields (such as SKU, Name, Brand, or Tax rate), the system captures your choices in real-time.
2.Validation: If you enter information into a field that requires a specific format (like the SKU), the system will immediately alert you with a red highlight if the input is invalid.
3.Search Execution: Once you have entered your desired criteria, you click the "Apply Search" button.
4.Results: The system processes your selections and updates the product list to display only the items that match your criteria. If no filters are applied, the system defaults to showing all available products.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Criteria: The collection of all search parameters (like Brand, Type, or SKU) that the system uses to narrow down the product list.
–Text Inputs: Flexible fields where you can type specific identifiers, such as a product name or code.
–Dropdown Selectors: Predefined lists that allow you to choose options like Brand or Tax rate, ensuring accuracy by preventing manual entry errors.
–Multi-select Tags: A compact feature that allows you to pick one or more descriptive labels to filter products by specific attributes.
–Visual Feedback: A safety feature that highlights fields in red if the information entered does not meet the required format, helping you correct errors before searching.
1.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves operational efficiency by reducing the time staff spend manually scrolling through product lists. By offering a robust set of search filters, users can pinpoint exact inventory items in seconds. The intuitive design—combining free-text search for quick lookups with structured dropdowns for standardized data—minimizes user error and ensures that the search process is both fast and reliable.
