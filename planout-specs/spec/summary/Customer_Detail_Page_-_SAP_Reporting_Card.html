1.1Overview

The SAP Reporting Card is a new component on the Customer Detail page designed to provide quick, read-only visibility into a customer's financial reporting attributes. This feature pulls specific data directly from our SAP system, ensuring that account managers and finance teams can easily reference key organizational codes without needing to navigate away from the customer's profile.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When you open a customer's detail page, the system automatically requests the relevant SAP reporting information for that specific customer.
2.Loading State: While the information is being retrieved from the SAP system, a brief loading message is displayed to indicate that the data is being fetched.
3.Data Display: Once retrieved, the system populates the card with the Assignment, Cost Center, and Profit Center details.
4.Read-Only Access: The information is presented in a clear, static format. These fields cannot be edited within this view, ensuring the integrity of the data synced from SAP. If no data is available for a specific field, the system will display "N/A."
1.3Key Concepts
–Assignment: A specific identifier used to categorize the customer for reporting purposes within SAP.
–Cost Center: An organizational unit within the SAP system that tracks where costs are incurred for this customer.
–Profit Center: An organizational unit that tracks the profitability and financial performance associated with this customer.
–Read-Only: A security and data integrity setting that allows users to view information without the ability to modify it directly in the interface.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical financial metadata, reducing the time spent switching between the ERP and other financial systems. By surfacing SAP-specific reporting codes directly on the customer profile, we improve operational efficiency, minimize manual data lookups, and ensure that all team members are viewing consistent, accurate financial classifications for every customer.
