1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Sales Deliveries Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific delivery record within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to review all relevant information regarding a delivery—such as customer details, warehouse origin, itemized product lists, and financial summaries—and manage the delivery lifecycle through status updates and barcode printing.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication & Loading: When you navigate to a delivery page, the system verifies your login status. If you are not logged in, you will be redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the specific details for the delivery record identified in the web address.
3.Information Display: The page organizes the data into three clear sections:

* Header: Displays key metadata, including document numbers, customer and warehouse information, dates, and the current workflow status.

* Items Table: Lists all products included in the delivery, showing quantities, pricing, discounts, and subtotals.

* Footer: Provides the final financial summary, including the subtotal, tax, and the grand total.

4.Workflow Management: Using the action bar, you can perform specific tasks:

* Deliver: Confirms the delivery status (requires a final confirmation).

* Cancel: Voids the delivery record (requires a final confirmation).

* Print Barcodes: Opens a dedicated tool to configure and print barcodes for the items in the delivery.

1.3Key Concepts
–Workflow State: The current stage of the delivery process (e.g., Draft or Delivered), which determines which actions are available to the user.
–Confirmation Modal: A pop-up window that appears before critical actions (like confirming or cancelling a delivery) to prevent accidental changes.
–Barcode Configuration: A specialized interface that allows you to select a print template and specify the number of copies needed before generating a preview or printing.
–Line Items: The individual products or services associated with a specific delivery, detailed with their respective quantities and costs.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes all critical delivery information into a single, easy-to-read interface, reducing the time spent searching for order details. By providing clear, guided workflows for confirming deliveries and cancelling errors, the system minimizes manual data entry mistakes and ensures that inventory and sales records remain accurate and up-to-date. Additionally, the integrated barcode printing capability streamlines warehouse operations, ensuring that items are correctly labeled and ready for shipment.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales Delivery Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific delivery record within the Lumi ERP system. It allows authorized users to review all relevant information regarding a delivery, including customer details, warehouse origin, itemized lists of products, and financial totals. The page also features a dynamic status bar that guides users through the delivery workflow by offering relevant actions based on the current state of the document.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication: When a user navigates to the page, the system verifies their identity. If the user is not logged in, they are automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: Once authenticated, the system fetches the specific delivery details using the unique record ID found in the web address.
3.Information Display: The system populates the page with the retrieved data, organizing it into three main areas:

* Header Information: Displays key references such as the document number, purchase order number, associated sales orders/invoices, warehouse and customer contact details, and audit timestamps.

* Delivery Items Table: Lists every product included in the delivery, showing quantities, pricing, discounts, and line-item totals.

* Financial Summary: Displays the final subtotal, tax, and grand total for the delivery.

4.Workflow Management: The system checks the current status of the delivery to determine which actions are available:

* If the status is "Draft," the user is presented with options to "Deliver" or "Cancel."

* If the status is "Delivered," the user is presented with the option to "Create Invoice."

2.3Key Concepts
–Workflow State: The current phase of the delivery process (e.g., Draft or Delivered), which dictates what actions a user can perform next.
–Sales Delivery Record: The central document containing all data related to a specific shipment of goods to a customer.
–Line Items: The individual products or services included in a delivery, detailed with their specific quantities and pricing.
–Audit Trail: Information displayed on the page (such as "Created By" and "Updated At") that provides transparency regarding who managed the record and when changes were made.
2.4Business Value

This feature centralizes all critical delivery information into a single, easy-to-read interface, reducing the time staff spend searching for data across different documents. By automating the workflow actions based on the delivery status, the system prevents manual errors and ensures that users only perform valid operations (such as creating an invoice only after a delivery is finalized), thereby streamlining the sales-to-fulfillment process.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales Delivery Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific delivery record within the Lumi ERP system. This page acts as a central hub, pulling together all relevant information—such as customer details, warehouse origin, associated sales orders, and financial totals—into a single, easy-to-read interface. It allows users to verify the status of a delivery and review the specific items included in the shipment.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Record Retrieval: When you select a specific delivery, the system gathers all primary information for that record, including the delivery date, workflow status, and internal notes.
2.Contextual Linking: The system automatically pulls in related data from other areas of the ERP, such as the original Sales Order, the corresponding Sales Invoice, the Warehouse location, and the Customer profile.
3.Itemization: The system retrieves the list of all products included in the delivery, displaying details like the SKU, product name, quantity delivered, and individual pricing.
4.Financial Calculation: The system aggregates the line items to display the final financial summary, including the subtotal, tax, and the final total amount for the delivery.
5.Display: All gathered information is presented on the screen, organized into a header (for general delivery info), a table (for the list of items), and a footer (for financial totals).
3.3Key Concepts
–Workflow State: The current stage of the delivery process (e.g., Draft or Delivered), which helps users understand where the document sits in the business lifecycle.
–Line Items: The individual products associated with a delivery, including their specific quantities and pricing details.
–Associated Records: Links to other documents or entities (like Sales Orders or Invoices) that provide the necessary context for why and how this delivery was created.
–Financial Summary: The breakdown of costs, including taxes and discounts, providing a clear view of the delivery's monetary value.
3.4Business Value
–Improved Visibility: By consolidating data from multiple sources (Warehouse, Sales, Finance) into one view, users no longer need to navigate between different screens to verify delivery details.
–Operational Efficiency: Staff can quickly confirm delivery accuracy, check customer purchase order numbers, and review warehouse information, reducing the time spent on administrative verification.
–Audit Readiness: With clear tracking of who created or updated the record and when it was delivered, the system provides a reliable trail for internal reporting and accountability.
