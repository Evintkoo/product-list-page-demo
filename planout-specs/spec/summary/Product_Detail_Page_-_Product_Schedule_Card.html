1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Schedule Card is a display component on the Product Detail Page that provides clear visibility into a product's active lifecycle. It shows the specific date range during which a product schedule is considered valid, helping users quickly identify when a product's availability begins and ends.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system retrieves the "Valid From" and "Valid To" dates associated with the product schedule.
2.The component displays these dates in a clear, read-only format for the user to review.
3.The system automatically compares the two dates to ensure the schedule is logically sound.
4.If the "Valid To" date is earlier than the "Valid From" date, the system highlights the end date in red and displays a warning message to alert the user of the configuration error.
1.3Key Concepts
–Valid From: The start date indicating when the product schedule becomes active.
–Valid To: The end date indicating when the product schedule expires.
–Validation Warning: A visual indicator that triggers if the schedule dates are set in an illogical order (i.e., the end date precedes the start date).
1.4Business Value

This feature improves data integrity and operational efficiency by providing immediate feedback on product scheduling. By automatically flagging illogical date ranges, it prevents potential errors in product availability, allowing team members to quickly spot and address scheduling conflicts without needing to manually verify the timeline.
