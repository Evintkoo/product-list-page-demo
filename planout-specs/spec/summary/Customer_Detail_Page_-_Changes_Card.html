1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Customer Changes" card is a read-only information panel located on the Customer Detail page. Its purpose is to provide full transparency regarding the history and origin of a customer record. By displaying key metadata, it allows users to quickly verify when a record was created or modified, who performed those actions, and where the information originated.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system retrieves the customer record data from the database.
2.The "Created At" field displays the exact date and time the customer profile was first established.
3.The "Updated At" field displays the date and time of the most recent modification to the profile.
4.The "Created By" field identifies the specific user or system account that initially created the record.
5.The "Updated By" field identifies the user or system account responsible for the most recent change.
6.The "Source" field displays the origin or platform from which the customer data was last updated.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific customer record.
–Read-Only: A status indicating that the information is for viewing purposes only and cannot be edited or altered by the user through this component.
–Metadata: Descriptive data that provides context about the customer record, such as timestamps and user identifiers, rather than the customer's personal information itself.
1.4Business Value

This feature enhances data governance and accountability within the Lumi ERP. By providing a clear audit trail, team members can easily track the lifecycle of a customer record, troubleshoot data discrepancies, and maintain confidence in the accuracy of the information. Because the fields are strictly read-only, the integrity of the historical data is protected from accidental changes.
