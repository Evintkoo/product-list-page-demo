1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Service Product Detail Page provides a comprehensive, read-only view of a specific service offered by the business. This page allows users to review all relevant information regarding a service—including its pricing, tax configurations, and operational settings—in one centralized location. It is designed to ensure that staff can quickly verify service details before making any necessary updates.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user navigates to the page, the system automatically identifies the specific service product and retrieves its full profile from the database.
2.Loading State: While the information is being retrieved, the user sees a loading indicator to confirm the system is processing the request.
3.Data Display: Once loaded, the page displays the service information organized into logical sections:

* Header: Shows the service name, SKU, and a status badge.

* Details: Displays core information like the description, type, and tags.

* Tax: Lists all tax-related settings, including tax categories and accounting codes.

* Settings: Shows operational flags, such as whether the service is active and its estimated completion time.

* Store Pricing: Displays the base price and currency for the service.

* Changes: Provides an audit trail showing when the record was created or last updated.

4.Navigation: The user can return to the main list of services using the "Service Products" button or transition to an edit mode using the "Edit" button to make changes to the record.
1.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A specific offering within the ERP system that represents a service rather than a physical good.
–Read-Only View: A display format where information is presented for review without allowing direct modification, ensuring data integrity.
–Audit Trail: A record of "who and when" regarding the creation and modification of the service data, found in the "Changes" section.
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique identifier used to track the specific service product within the system.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a clear, standardized view of service data. By centralizing tax, pricing, and configuration details, it reduces the time staff spend searching for information across different screens. Furthermore, the inclusion of an audit trail ensures accountability and transparency, allowing managers to track when and how service details were last modified.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Service Product Detail Page feature provides a centralized view for users to examine the complete profile of a specific service offered by the company. When a user selects a service, the system retrieves all relevant information—including pricing, tax details, and status—to ensure staff have a clear and accurate understanding of the service's configuration.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initiation: When a user navigates to the detail page for a specific service, the system identifies the unique identifier for that service from the web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system automatically requests the full record for that service from the database.
3.Tax Information Lookup: The system cross-references the service’s tax category to pull in the specific tax name and rate associated with that service.
4.Information Display: The system populates the page sections with the retrieved data, including the service description, pricing, tax details, current status, and the date of the last update.
5.Error Handling: If the system cannot locate the requested service, it will display an error message to inform the user that the information is unavailable.
2.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A non-physical offering (such as a consultation or maintenance plan) managed within the ERP system.
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique code used to identify and track a specific service product.
–Tax Category: A classification that determines the tax rate applied to a service.
–Base Price: The standard cost of the service before any additional fees or discounts are applied.
–Status (Active/Inactive): A setting that indicates whether the service is currently available for sale or use.
2.4Business Value

This feature ensures that employees have immediate access to consistent and accurate service information. By consolidating pricing, tax, and status details into one view, the system reduces the risk of manual errors during sales or administrative tasks and provides a reliable "source of truth" for all service-related inquiries.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Service Product Detail Page feature provides a comprehensive view of a specific service offered by the business. It allows users to see all relevant information for a single service in one place, including its description, pricing, tax classification, and current status. This ensures that staff have a clear and accurate understanding of the service details whenever they need to review or manage them.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When a user navigates to a specific service product page, the system identifies the unique identifier for that service from the web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system searches the database for the primary service record associated with that identifier.
3.Context Gathering: If the service is found, the system automatically pulls in the associated tax information (such as the tax category name and rate) to ensure the financial details are complete.
4.Data Consolidation: The system combines the service details, pricing, tax settings, and the most recent update timestamp into a single, organized summary.
5.Display: The consolidated information is sent to the page, where it populates the various sections—such as the pricing, tax, and status areas—for the user to view. If the service cannot be found, the system displays a clear "not found" message.
3.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A specific offering provided by the business that has its own unique identifier, description, and pricing.
–Tax Category: A classification assigned to a service that determines the specific tax rate applied to it.
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique code used to identify and track a specific service within the system.
–Audit Information: A record of when the service details were last modified, helping users track the history of changes.
3.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical service information, reducing the time staff spend searching for details across different systems. By automatically linking tax rates and pricing to the service, it minimizes the risk of manual errors during billing or reporting. Additionally, providing a clear "last modified" date ensures that users are always working with the most current and accurate service data.
