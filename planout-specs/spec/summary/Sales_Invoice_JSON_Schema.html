1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Sales Invoice feature in the Lumi ERP system provides a structured way to generate and manage customer invoices based on completed sales deliveries. It ensures that financial records are accurately linked to the goods delivered, maintains a clear workflow for invoice approval and processing, and provides a comprehensive view of invoice details, including payment status and line-item breakdowns.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Invoice Creation: When a user initiates a new invoice, the system selects one or more "delivered" sales deliveries. It automatically pulls the relevant customer, product, and pricing information from these deliveries to populate the invoice.
2.Data Consolidation: The system calculates the financial totals (subtotal, tax, and final amount due) based on the items copied from the deliveries.
3.Linking Records: Once the invoice is created, the system updates the original sales deliveries to link them to the new invoice, ensuring that each delivery is accounted for and preventing duplicate invoicing.
4.Workflow Management: Newly created invoices start in a "draft" state. Users can then move the invoice through various stages—such as "approved" or "sent"—to reflect the current status of the billing process.
5.Retrieval and Review: Users can view a detailed summary of any invoice, which includes the header information, payment history, and a list of all items included in that specific invoice.
1.3Key Concepts
–Sales Invoice: A formal document representing a request for payment for goods or services delivered to a customer.
–Workflow State: The current status of an invoice (e.g., draft, approved, sent, paid, or cancelled), which dictates what actions can be performed next.
–Sales Delivery: The source record for an invoice; it represents the physical or digital fulfillment of a customer's order.
–Transaction: A safety mechanism that ensures all parts of the invoice creation process (creating the invoice, adding items, and updating delivery records) happen together successfully or not at all, preventing data errors.
–Primary Key: A unique identifier for each invoice or item, ensuring that every record in the system can be accurately tracked and retrieved.
1.4Business Value
–Accuracy and Consistency: By automatically pulling data from existing sales deliveries, the system eliminates manual data entry errors and ensures that the invoice perfectly matches the goods actually delivered.
–Operational Efficiency: The automated workflow and linking of deliveries to invoices save time for the finance team and provide clear visibility into which deliveries have been billed and which are still pending.
–Improved Financial Control: The structured status tracking (workflow states) allows businesses to maintain better control over their billing cycle, ensuring that invoices are approved before being sent to customers and accurately marked as paid upon receipt.
