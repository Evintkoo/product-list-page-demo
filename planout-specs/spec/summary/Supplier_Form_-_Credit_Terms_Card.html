1.1Overview

The Credit Terms card is a dedicated section within the supplier management page designed to help you standardize financial agreements with your suppliers. This feature allows you to clearly define how long a supplier has to pay an invoice and set a maximum spending limit, ensuring that your financial data is captured accurately and consistently during the supplier onboarding or editing process.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Section Identification: The user navigates to the "Credit Terms" section, which is clearly marked with a credit card icon for quick visual recognition.
2.Defining Payment Duration: The user enters the number of days allowed for payment in the "Credit Term (Days)" field. If the user needs clarification, they can hover over or tap the question mark icon to see a helpful explanation of this field.
3.Setting Credit Limits: The user enters an optional maximum credit limit for the supplier to help manage financial exposure.
4.Real-time Validation: As the user types, the system automatically checks the entries. If a user enters a non-positive number for the payment duration or a negative number for the credit limit, the system immediately displays an error message to prevent incorrect data entry.
1.3Key Concepts
–Credit Term (Days): The specific timeframe (in days) granted to a supplier to settle their invoices after receipt. This is a mandatory field.
–Maximum Credit Limit: An optional financial ceiling that restricts the total amount of credit extended to a supplier.
–Input Validation: An automated safety check that ensures all entered numbers are logical (e.g., preventing negative credit limits) before the form is submitted.
–Contextual Tooltip: A helpful "question mark" icon that provides on-demand guidance to the user, reducing the need for external documentation.
1.4Business Value
–Data Accuracy: By enforcing validation rules at the point of entry, the system prevents common human errors, such as entering negative values or missing mandatory payment terms.
–Improved Efficiency: The inclusion of tooltips and clear labeling reduces user confusion, allowing staff to complete supplier profiles faster and with more confidence.
–Financial Control: Standardizing how credit terms and limits are recorded provides better visibility into your company's financial commitments and helps maintain consistent credit policies across all supplier relationships.
