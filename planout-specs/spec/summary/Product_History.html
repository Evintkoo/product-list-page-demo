1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product History feature provides a comprehensive audit trail for any given product within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to view a chronological list of all changes made to a product’s settings and pricing, ensuring transparency and accountability regarding how product data has evolved over time.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Accessing the Page: When a user navigates to the Product History view for a specific item, the system automatically triggers a request to retrieve the full history log for that product.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches all recorded changes, including timestamps, the user who performed the update, and the specific product details at that moment.
3.Displaying the Log: The information is presented in a clear, tabular format. Each row represents a single point in time when a change was saved.
4.Reviewing Details: Users can scan the table to see historical values for pricing (Company Supply, Wholesale, Supply, and Retail), as well as status flags like whether the item was active, tracked for inventory, or part of a bundle.
5.Contextual Guidance: For specific fields like "Supply Price" and "Track," users can hover over informational icons to see helpful definitions, such as clarifying that "Track" determines whether the system automatically updates stock levels during sales.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that tracks who changed what and when, providing a reliable history of product modifications.
–Supply Price: The cost at which the store purchases the item from a supplier, inclusive of tax.
–Company Supply Price: The base purchase price from the supplier, also inclusive of tax, used for internal cost tracking.
–Tracking: A setting that determines if the system should automatically manage inventory levels for an item; this is typically enabled for physical goods and disabled for services.
–Bundle: A flag indicating if the product is sold as a collection of items rather than a single unit.
1.4Business Value

This feature is essential for maintaining data integrity and operational oversight. By providing a clear history of price adjustments and configuration changes, it helps managers:

–Troubleshoot Discrepancies: Quickly identify when and why a price or setting was changed.
–Improve Accountability: See exactly which user performed an update, which is vital for team coordination.
–Support Decision Making: Analyze past pricing strategies to inform future adjustments, ensuring that the business maintains accurate records for both financial reporting and inventory management.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Product History feature provides a transparent audit trail for any given product within the Lumi ERP system. By accessing this view, users can see a chronological list of all modifications made to a product’s details, including price adjustments, warranty changes, and status updates. This ensures that team members can easily track how and when a product's information has evolved over time.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initiation: When a user navigates to the product history page, the system automatically triggers a request to retrieve the full change log for that specific product.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the historical records, which include details such as who made the change, when it occurred, and the specific values (like prices or status flags) at that time.
3.Data Processing: The system organizes the raw data into a clear, readable format, ensuring that every change is correctly labeled for the user.
4.Display: The processed information is rendered into a table on the screen, allowing the user to review the complete history of the product.
5.Error Handling: If the system is unable to retrieve the data, a notification will appear to inform the user that the history could not be loaded.
2.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific product.
–Change Log: A list of modifications, including updates to pricing (wholesale, retail, supply), status (active/inactive), and product attributes (warranty, bundle status, tracking).
–User Attribution: The system records "who" performed the update, ensuring accountability and clarity regarding changes made to product data.
2.4Business Value

This feature enhances operational transparency and data integrity. By maintaining a clear history of product changes, businesses can:

–Improve Accountability: Easily identify who updated product information and when.
–Support Decision Making: Review past pricing strategies and attribute changes to better inform future adjustments.
–Simplify Troubleshooting: Quickly identify when a specific change (such as a price or status update) occurred if an issue arises, reducing the time spent investigating data discrepancies.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Product History feature provides a comprehensive audit trail for any product within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to view a chronological list of all modifications made to a product’s details, such as pricing updates, warranty changes, and status toggles. By displaying who made a change and when, this feature ensures full transparency and accountability for all product-related data.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request: A user selects a specific product to view its history.
2.Retrieval: The system searches the database for all recorded changes associated with that specific product.
3.Sorting: The system organizes these records starting with the most recent change, ensuring the latest updates appear at the top of the list.
4.Display: The system presents the data in a clear format, showing the date of the change, the person responsible, and the updated values for pricing, warranty, and product settings (such as whether the item is active, tracked, or part of a bundle).
3.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific product.
–Product History: A detailed log capturing changes to key business attributes, including various price points (supply, wholesale, retail) and operational flags.
–Chronological Ordering: The practice of listing records by date, ensuring users can easily track the evolution of a product over time.
3.4Business Value
–Accountability: By tracking who updated a product and when, teams can easily identify the source of data changes, reducing errors and confusion.
–Informed Decision Making: Having access to historical pricing and configuration data allows managers to analyze past trends and make better-informed adjustments to current product strategies.
–Operational Transparency: This feature eliminates guesswork regarding product status, ensuring that all team members are aligned on the current state and history of any item in the inventory.
