1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Detail Page is a comprehensive dashboard within the Mini ERP system designed to provide a centralized view of all information related to a specific product. It allows users to view detailed specifications, pricing, inventory status, and historical data in one place, while also providing quick access to management tools like editing, barcode generation, and tracking.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading: When a user opens the page, the system automatically fetches the specific product's data from the database. A loading indicator is displayed until the information is ready.
2.Header & Identification: The page displays a persistent navigation bar and a "Product Chip" that clearly identifies the product by its name and SKU.
3.Action Toolbar: Users have immediate access to primary actions, including returning to the product list, editing the product details, or generating QR and barcode labels.
4.Data Organization: The page uses a two-column layout to organize information:

* Left Column: Focuses on core product data, including general details, tax information, various pricing tiers (Company, Store, and External), and tracking/bundle information.

* Right Column: Focuses on operational and administrative details, such as product attributes, dimensions, scheduling, settings, warranty, return policies, and trade-in information.

5.Deep-Dive Menus: Users can access specialized views for warehouse stock levels and historical purchase records through dedicated menu options.
1.3Key Concepts
–Product Chip: A visual summary element that displays the product's unique identifier (SKU) and name for quick reference.
–Two-Column Grid: A structured layout design that separates core product data from administrative and operational settings to improve readability.
–State Management: The system’s ability to track whether data is currently loading, has successfully loaded, or has encountered an error, ensuring the user is always informed of the page status.
–Contextual Navigation: Buttons and menus that allow the user to perform specific tasks related to the product, such as editing or viewing stock, without leaving the current page.
1.4Business Value

This feature serves as the "single source of truth" for any product in the ERP system. By consolidating complex data—ranging from pricing and tax rules to warranty and stock history—into a single, well-organized view, it significantly reduces the time staff spend searching for information. The inclusion of direct action buttons (like Edit, QR, and Barcode) streamlines daily operational workflows, allowing for faster inventory management and administrative updates.
