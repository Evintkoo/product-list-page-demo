1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Detail Page is a comprehensive view designed to provide a centralized, read-only summary of a specific product's information. It allows users to view core identity details, pricing structures, tax information, and logistical data (such as stock tracking and bundles). The page is built to be highly navigable, offering quick access to related operational data like warehouse stock levels, purchase history, and barcode/QR code generation.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading Data: When the page opens, the system automatically fetches the specific product details from the database. If the data is still loading, you will see a placeholder; if an error occurs, an error notification will appear.
2.Identity Display: A sticky header remains at the top of your screen at all times, showing the product's SKU and Name for easy reference.
3.Information Review: The page organizes data into logical "cards," such as:

* Core Details: Basic product identity.

* Pricing: Separate cards for Company, Store, and External (MRP) pricing.

* Logistics: Tracking, bundle status, and physical dimensions.

* Compliance: Tax information, warranty periods, and return policies.

4.Interactive Navigation: You can use the sub-menu to switch between different views (like Returns or Warranty) or click specific buttons to generate QR codes, print barcodes, or view the product's audit trail (Changes).
5.Operational Insights: You can tap on the "Warehouse Stock" or "Purchase History" menus to open detailed views or modals that show specific fulfillment metrics and past transaction records.
6.Editing: If changes are needed, an "Edit" button is available to transition you directly to the product modification page.
7.Returning: A "Back" button is always available to return you to the main Product List.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only Cards: Organized sections of the page that display specific categories of product data (e.g., Tax, Pricing, Dimensions) without allowing direct editing on this screen.
–Sticky Header: A persistent bar at the top of the page that keeps the product's identity (SKU and Name) visible even as you scroll through long lists of information.
–Audit Trail: A "Changes" section that tracks the history of updates made to the product, showing who made the change and when.
–Dynamic Tabs: A navigation feature that allows you to toggle between different specialized views (like Warranty or Returns) without leaving the main product page.
–Data Validation: The system enforces strict rules (JSON Schema) to ensure that essential information—such as SKU, Brand, and Pricing—is always present and correctly formatted.
1.4Business Value

This feature provides a "single source of truth" for any given product, reducing the time staff spend searching for scattered information. By consolidating pricing, stock, and compliance data into one view, it enables faster decision-making for sales and inventory management. Furthermore, the inclusion of audit trails and clear navigation to operational history (like purchase records) improves transparency and accountability across the supply chain.
