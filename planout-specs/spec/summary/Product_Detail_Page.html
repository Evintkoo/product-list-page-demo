1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Detail Page is a centralized, read-only view designed to provide a comprehensive snapshot of a single product's information. It allows users to quickly review essential data—ranging from core identity and pricing to tax, warranty, and inventory settings—without the ability to modify the information directly on this screen.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When you navigate to the page, the system automatically requests the specific product details from the database using the product's unique identifier.
2.Data Loading: While the system retrieves the information, a loading indicator is displayed to ensure a smooth user experience.
3.Information Display: Once the data is retrieved, the page populates a two-column layout with organized "cards."
–Left Column: Displays core details (SKU, name, description), tax information, various pricing tiers (Company, Store, External), and tracking/bundle status.
–Right Column: Displays administrative and logistical data, including audit changes, custom attributes, physical dimensions, product schedules, barcode settings, and customer-facing policies like warranty, returns, and trade-in values.
4.Navigation & Interaction: Users can utilize the header navigation to return to the product list or access related sub-menus (such as purchase history or warehouse stock) to view further details.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: The page is designed strictly for viewing information; no data can be edited or saved directly from this screen.
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): The unique identifier for the product, which can be easily copied to the clipboard for quick reference or use in other systems.
–Sticky Navigation: The header and product identity bar (showing the SKU and Name) remain fixed at the top of the screen, ensuring that key product information is always visible even when scrolling through long lists of data.
–Pricing Tiers: The page breaks down pricing into specific categories (Company, Store, and External/MRP) to provide clarity on costs and retail expectations.
–Audit Trail: The "Changes" card provides a record of updates, helping users track the history of the product information.
1.4Business Value

This feature serves as a "single source of truth" for product information, reducing the time staff spend searching for data across different systems. By organizing complex product data into a clean, readable, and consistent layout, it enables faster decision-making for inventory management, pricing strategy, and customer service inquiries. The inclusion of audit trails and clear policy information (like warranty and returns) also improves operational transparency and accountability.
