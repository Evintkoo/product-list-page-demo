1.  Data
1.1Overview

This feature establishes the foundational "Master Data" for the Finance module within the Lumi ERP system. By creating standardized lists for taxes, accounting structures, and product classifications, the system ensures that financial data is consistent, accurate, and compliant with reporting requirements (such as e-Faktur). These tables act as the "source of truth" that powers dropdown menus and selection fields across the entire application.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Tax Product Classification: The system maintains a list of official tax codes used to categorize goods and services, ensuring that every product is correctly identified for tax reporting.
2.Chart of Accounts (COA) Management: The system organizes the company’s financial accounts into a structured tree, allowing for clear categorization of assets, liabilities, and expenses.
3.Pricelist Configuration: The system stores various pricing strategies, allowing the business to define and select specific price lists (e.g., wholesale vs. retail) for different company operations.
4.Tax Rate Definition: The system manages specific tax rates (like VAT/PPN), allowing users to define and apply the correct tax percentages to financial transactions.
5.Unit of Measure (UoM) Standardization: The system provides a standardized list of measurement units (e.g., Kilograms, Pieces) to ensure consistency when calculating taxes and inventory values.
1.3Key Concepts
–Master Data: The core, essential information used across the ERP to ensure that everyone is using the same definitions for taxes, accounts, and units.
–Company-Scoped Data: Financial information (like the Chart of Accounts or Tax rates) that is specific to a particular business entity, ensuring data privacy and organizational separation.
–Postable Account: A specific type of account in the Chart of Accounts that is eligible to receive direct financial entries, as opposed to "parent" accounts which are used only for grouping.
–Tax Product Code: A standardized identifier required for regulatory compliance, specifically for mapping items to their correct tax treatment in e-Faktur.
–Hierarchy (Tree Structure): The ability to link accounts to a "parent" account, which helps in generating organized financial reports and summaries.
1.4Business Value
–Regulatory Compliance: By using standardized Tax Product Codes and defined tax rates, the business can automate accurate tax reporting and meet local government requirements (e-Faktur) with minimal manual effort.
–Operational Consistency: Centralizing data like Units of Measure and Pricelists prevents errors caused by inconsistent naming or manual entry, ensuring that sales and accounting teams are always aligned.
–Scalability: The company-scoped architecture allows the ERP to support multiple business entities within the same system while keeping their financial structures distinct and organized.
–Improved Reporting: A well-structured Chart of Accounts enables faster, more accurate financial analysis and easier generation of balance sheets and income statements.
