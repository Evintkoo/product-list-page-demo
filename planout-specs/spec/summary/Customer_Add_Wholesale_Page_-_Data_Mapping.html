1.1Overview

The Data Mapping section is a new component within the Wholesale Customer registration form. Its primary purpose is to ensure that every new wholesale customer is correctly linked to an internal salesperson. By requiring this assignment at the point of entry, the system maintains accurate records for sales tracking and account management.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The user navigates to the "Data Mapping" section of the Wholesale Customer Add form.
2.The user interacts with the "Salesperson" dropdown menu to select the appropriate representative from the list.
3.As the user makes a selection, the system immediately checks that a valid salesperson has been chosen.
4.If the user attempts to leave the field blank, the system displays a validation error message indicating that the salesperson is a required field.
5.Once the form is completed and submitted, the selected salesperson information is sent to the system to finalize the customer record.
1.3Key Concepts
–Data Mapping: The process of linking specific customer information to internal system records, in this case, assigning a customer to a specific staff member.
–Validation: An automated check that ensures required information is provided before the form can be successfully processed.
–Required Field: A mandatory data point (marked with an asterisk) that must be filled out to ensure the integrity of the customer record.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Accountability: By mandating a salesperson assignment, the business ensures that every wholesale account has a clear point of contact for sales and support.
–Data Integrity: Real-time validation prevents incomplete records from entering the system, reducing the need for manual data cleanup later.
–Streamlined Workflow: Integrating this assignment directly into the "Add Customer" process saves time by eliminating the need for secondary administrative steps to assign accounts after they are created.
