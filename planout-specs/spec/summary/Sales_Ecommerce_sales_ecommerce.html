1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Ecommerce Sales page provides a centralized dashboard for viewing and managing sales originating from your online channels. This feature allows staff to monitor order progress, filter sales by specific criteria (such as store location, customer, or status), and quickly navigate to detailed records for individual transactions, customers, or vehicles.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: Upon loading the page, the system verifies your login session. If you are not logged in, you are automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Initialization: The system automatically fetches the initial list of the 20 most recent ecommerce sales, along with the necessary data to populate filter dropdowns (such as available stores and user lists).
3.Filtering: You can refine the list using the filter bar, which includes options for Store, Document Number, Online Reference, Customer, Status, Delivery Method, Registration Number, and Date Ranges.
4.Search Execution: Once you have entered your desired criteria, clicking the "Search" button triggers a request to the system to update the table with the filtered results.
5.Viewing Details: The sales table displays key information for each order. You can click on specific items—such as the Document Number, Customer Name, or Registration Number—to navigate directly to the corresponding detail page for that record.
1.3Key Concepts
–Ecommerce Sales List: A paginated table showing 20 sales at a time, sorted by the most recent creation date by default.
–Filter Bar: A set of search and selection tools that allow you to narrow down the list of sales based on specific business requirements.
–Status Badge: A visual indicator for each sale that shows its current progress (e.g., Draft, In Transit, or Completed).
–Deep Linking: The ability to click on specific data points (like a Document Number or Customer Name) to jump directly to a dedicated page for that entity.
–Session Security: An automated check that ensures only authorized users can access the sales data.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the management of online orders by providing a clear, searchable interface. By consolidating ecommerce sales data into a single view, staff can quickly identify the status of specific orders, resolve customer inquiries by navigating directly to customer or vehicle profiles, and maintain operational efficiency by filtering for specific fulfillment or store-related criteria.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Ecommerce Sales dashboard provides a centralized view for tracking online sales transactions within the Lumi ERP system. When a user navigates to this page, the system automatically verifies their identity, retrieves the most recent ecommerce sales records, and populates the interface with relevant store and salesperson information to assist with order management.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication Check: Upon loading the page, the system verifies the user's active session. If the user is not logged in, they are automatically redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: Once authenticated, the system simultaneously fetches three sets of data:

* A list of the 20 most recent ecommerce sales (excluding any voided transactions).

* A list of available stores.

* A list of registered users (salespeople).

3.Interface Population: The system processes the retrieved data to fill the main sales table and update the filter dropdowns for stores and salespeople, allowing the user to begin managing the data immediately.
2.3Key Concepts
–Authentication: A security check that ensures only authorized users can access sales data.
–Ecommerce Sales: Transactions originating from online channels, filtered automatically to exclude voided or cancelled orders.
–Parallel Loading: A performance technique where the system fetches multiple pieces of information at the same time rather than waiting for each one to finish individually, resulting in a faster page load.
–Workflow State: A status indicator for each sale (e.g., completed, pending) that helps the system filter out irrelevant or voided records.
2.4Business Value
–Efficiency: By automating the retrieval of sales, store, and user data, the dashboard reduces manual effort and ensures the user is always looking at the most current information.
–Data Integrity: The system automatically filters out voided sales, ensuring that reporting and management tasks are based only on valid, active transactions.
–Security: The built-in session verification protects sensitive sales data by ensuring that only authenticated personnel can access the dashboard.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Ecommerce Sales feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to track and manage sales originating from your online channels. This feature pulls data from your online store and presents it in a clear, organized list, allowing your team to monitor order progress, customer details, and vehicle information in one centralized location.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system identifies all sales specifically marked with an "ecommerce" source.
2.Information Consolidation: The system gathers relevant details for each sale, including customer names, vehicle registration numbers, and the specific store location associated with the order.
3.Sorting: The list is automatically organized to show the most recently created sales at the top.
4.Formatting: The raw data is converted into a user-friendly format, ensuring that key information like order status, delivery method, and total amounts are clearly displayed.
5.Pagination: The system calculates the total number of ecommerce sales and presents them in manageable pages, allowing users to navigate through large volumes of orders efficiently.
3.3Key Concepts
–Ecommerce Source: A filter that ensures only online-originated sales are displayed, keeping your digital sales data separate from in-store transactions.
–Workflow State: The current status of an order (e.g., pending, processing, or completed), which helps your team track where an order is in the fulfillment process.
–Reference Codes: The system tracks both your internal ERP document number and the original ecommerce reference number, making it easy to cross-reference orders with your online storefront.
–Pagination: A navigation tool that breaks down long lists of sales into smaller, readable pages to improve system performance and user experience.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines operations by providing a single source of truth for all online sales. By integrating customer, vehicle, and store data into one view, your team can reduce the time spent switching between different platforms to verify order details. The structured list and pagination ensure that your staff can quickly locate specific orders, monitor fulfillment status, and maintain high levels of customer service for your online business.
