1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "New Stock Outgoing" feature provides a streamlined form for recording items leaving your company’s inventory. This tool allows users to select a specific source location (store or warehouse), add products to be removed, and assign those items to specific expense accounts. The system automatically calculates costs and totals, ensuring that inventory records remain accurate before the document is finalized.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: When you open the form, the system automatically loads your available stores, warehouses, and expense accounts so they are ready for selection.
2.Select Location: You must first choose the "From" location (store or warehouse). This selection is mandatory and unlocks the ability to add products.
3.Add Products: Click the "Add Product" button to create a new row. You can search for products by name, code, or SKU.
4.Automatic Data Retrieval: Once a product is selected, the system automatically displays the current stock level and the average cost for that item at your chosen location.
5.Specify Quantity: Enter the "Order Quantity." The system will instantly calculate the total cost for that line item and update the overall document totals.
6.Assign Accounts: For each product row, select the appropriate "Expense Account" to ensure the outgoing stock is tracked against the correct financial category.
7.Optional Linking: You may optionally link the outgoing stock to one or more "Service Work Orders" associated with the selected location.
8.Validation & Save: Once you click "Save," the system validates your entries (e.g., ensuring you have enough stock). If everything is correct, the document is created, and you are redirected to the details page. If there is an error, the system highlights the issue while keeping your data intact so you can make corrections.
1.3Key Concepts
–Source Location: The specific store or warehouse from which the inventory is being removed.
–Product Search: An intelligent lookup tool that helps you find items and immediately shows you how much stock is currently available at your chosen location.
–Average Cost: A system-calculated value representing the unit cost of an item, used to determine the total financial impact of the outgoing stock.
–Expense Account: A financial category assigned to each line item to track where the value of the outgoing stock is being charged.
–Service Work Order: An optional reference that links the outgoing stock to specific service tasks or jobs.
–Validation: A safety check performed by the system to ensure that you do not remove more stock than is currently available.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes inventory management by providing a controlled, error-resistant way to record stock movement. By automating cost calculations and requiring expense account assignments, it reduces manual data entry errors and ensures that inventory records are always synchronized with financial reporting. This helps the business maintain accurate stock levels and clear visibility into where company assets are being allocated.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The "New Stock Outgoing" feature provides a structured way for users to record items leaving company inventory. This form acts as a digital document that tracks where stock is coming from, why it is being removed (linked to specific work orders), and the financial details associated with the items. The system ensures accuracy by automatically pulling current cost information when a product is selected, reducing manual data entry errors.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initiate Form: The user begins with a blank "Stock Outgoing" form.
2.Define Source: The user selects the origin of the stock (e.g., a specific warehouse or store).
3.Add Items: The user adds product rows to the form. Upon selecting a product, the system automatically retrieves the current average cost from the chosen location.
4.Input Details: The user enters the quantity to be removed and any relevant reference codes, due dates, notes, or associated service work orders.
5.Save: Once the user clicks "Save," the system bundles all the information—including the header details and the list of products—and sends it to the server to create the official stock outgoing record.
2.3Key Concepts
–Source: The specific inventory location (such as a warehouse) from which the stock is being removed.
–Average Cost: The system-calculated value of a product, which is automatically populated to ensure financial consistency when stock leaves the inventory.
–Service Work Order: A reference to a specific job or service task that justifies the outgoing stock, allowing for better tracking of project-related expenses.
–Expense Account: The accounting category (COA) assigned to the outgoing stock to ensure the financial impact is recorded in the correct ledger.
2.4Business Value
–Data Accuracy: By automatically fetching the average cost and product details, the system eliminates the need for manual calculations and prevents pricing errors.
–Operational Traceability: Linking outgoing stock to specific service work orders ensures that inventory usage is clearly tied to business activities and projects.
–Streamlined Workflow: The form-based approach provides a consistent, repeatable process for staff to document inventory movements, ensuring that all necessary financial and operational data is captured before the record is finalized.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The "New Stock Outgoing" feature provides a streamlined form for recording items leaving your company’s inventory. Whether you are moving stock from a store or a warehouse, this tool allows you to select specific products, link them to relevant service work orders, and assign them to the appropriate expense accounts. The system automatically pulls current stock levels and costs to ensure your records remain accurate.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: When you open the form, the system automatically loads a list of all available stores and warehouses (for the "From" location) and a list of expense accounts to be used for each item.
2.Select Location: You choose the source location (Store or Warehouse) from which the stock is being removed.
3.Add Products: You search for products by typing their SKU, name, or code. As you select a product, the system automatically retrieves and displays the current stock quantity and average cost for that specific location.
4.Link Work Orders (Optional): You can search for and attach one or more service work orders to the transaction. The system filters these results to ensure you only see work orders associated with your selected location.
5.Assign Details: For each product row, you select the appropriate expense account and enter the quantity being removed.
6.Submission: Once you provide a reference number, due date, and any necessary notes, you submit the form to finalize the stock outgoing record.
3.3Key Concepts
–Source Location: The specific store or warehouse where the inventory is currently held and from which it is being removed.
–Product Search: An intelligent lookup tool that helps you find items quickly by SKU, name, or product code, while simultaneously showing you how much is currently in stock.
–Service Work Order: A specific job or task record that can be linked to the outgoing stock to track why the inventory was used.
–Expense Account: A financial category used to track the cost of the outgoing items for accounting purposes.
–Stock Outgoing Record: The final document that captures the "who, what, where, and why" of the inventory movement.
3.4Business Value
–Accuracy: By automatically pulling real-time stock levels and average costs, the system reduces manual data entry errors and ensures financial reporting is precise.
–Efficiency: The search-as-you-type functionality and pre-loaded location lists allow staff to process inventory movements quickly.
–Traceability: Linking outgoing stock to specific service work orders and expense accounts provides a clear audit trail, making it easier to understand inventory consumption and manage costs across different departments or projects.
