1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Company Edit page allows administrators to update essential business information and configure operational settings for their company within the Lumi ERP system. The page is organized into two main sections—Details and Settings—ensuring that all company-specific data, from contact information to inventory and point-of-sale (POS) preferences, can be managed in one centralized location.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading the Page: When you navigate to the Company Edit page, the system automatically retrieves the current company’s existing data and populates all fields. The "Details" tab is displayed by default.
2.Updating Details: You can modify core information such as the company name, address, contact details (phone, email, fax), and tax identification numbers.
3.Configuring Settings: By switching to the "Settings" tab, you can manage advanced operational preferences, including:

* Product Management: Defining custom product labels, monitoring specific product types, and setting barcode standards.

* POS & Sales: Configuring default customer settings, payment overage rules, and receipt printing preferences.

* Stock & Purchasing: Toggling permissions for editing quantities in stock orders, returns, and transfers, as well as visibility settings for purchasing and serial numbers.

* Reporting: Setting the character used to separate columns in exported CSV files.

4.Saving Changes: Once you have finished your updates, clicking the "Save" button submits all changes from both sections to the system. Upon a successful save, you will be redirected back to the main Company List page.
5.Canceling: If you wish to discard your changes, clicking the "Cancel" button will clear the form and return you to the Company List page without saving.
1.3Key Concepts
–Details Section: The primary area for managing static company identity, such as contact info, business name, and regional settings (time zone/country).
–Settings Section: A comprehensive area for operational configuration, covering inventory, sales, and reporting preferences.
–Form Persistence: The system ensures that data entered or modified in one tab remains intact while you navigate to the other, allowing for a seamless editing experience before final submission.
–Tooltips: Informational icons provided throughout the page to explain specific fields, such as internal company codes, business brand names, and custom product attributes.
1.4Business Value

This feature provides the flexibility required to keep company records accurate and operational workflows optimized. By centralizing both administrative details and granular system settings, users can quickly adapt the ERP to changing business needs—such as updating contact information, adjusting stock management rules, or refining POS behavior—without needing technical support. This ensures the system remains aligned with the company's current operational processes.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Company Edit feature provides a centralized interface for administrators to update essential company information and operational settings within the Lumi ERP system. This page ensures that business details, product configurations, and point-of-sale (POS) preferences are accurately maintained and synchronized across the platform.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: When the page loads, the system automatically retrieves the current company’s existing data and populates the form fields.
2.Data Review & Modification: The user reviews and updates information across two main areas:

* Details Section: Includes core business information such as name, address, contact details, and regional settings (time zone, currency).

* Settings Section: Includes advanced configurations for product labeling, custom attributes, barcode types, POS behavior, stock management, and purchasing workflows.

3.Validation: Before saving, the system ensures that all mandatory fields (such as company name, code, address, and tax settings) are correctly filled.
4.Submission: Upon clicking the save button, the system bundles all updated information and sends it to the server.
5.Confirmation & Navigation: If the update is successful, the system automatically redirects the user back to the main Company List page.
2.3Key Concepts
–Company Details: The foundational business information required for system identification and reporting.
–Product Attributes: Customizable labels and settings that allow the business to define how products are categorized and displayed, including mobile-specific visibility toggles.
–POS Settings: Operational controls for the point-of-sale interface, such as payment overage limits and receipt printing preferences.
–Operational Toggles: Various switches that control specific business logic, such as whether users can edit received quantities in stock orders or view specific purchasing states.
–Data Mapping: The process by which the system ensures that the information stored in the database is correctly displayed in the corresponding form fields for the user to edit.
2.4Business Value

This feature empowers users to maintain accurate company records and fine-tune operational settings without needing technical intervention. By providing a comprehensive and structured edit page, the business can ensure that its ERP configuration remains aligned with current workflows, tax requirements, and inventory management practices, ultimately leading to more reliable reporting and smoother daily operations.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Company Edit feature allows administrators to manage and update core business information and operational settings for their organization within the Lumi ERP system. This page provides a centralized interface to configure everything from basic contact details to specific inventory, purchasing, and point-of-sale (POS) preferences.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading Data: When you open the Company Edit page, the system automatically retrieves the current company’s existing information and populates the form fields.
2.Review and Edit: You can review and modify details across several categories, including:

* Company Details: Basic information like name, address, contact details, and regional settings (time zone, currency).

* Product Configuration: Custom labels, product type monitoring, and mobile visibility settings for product attributes.

* Operational Settings: Barcode types, POS preferences (such as receipt printing and overpayment limits), and stock/purchasing workflow toggles.

* Reporting: Configuration for CSV file formatting.

3.Validation: Once you submit your changes, the system performs a check to ensure all mandatory fields (such as Name, Code, Address, and Time Zone) are filled out correctly.
4.Saving and Navigation: If the information is valid, the system saves your updates and automatically redirects you back to the main Company List page.
3.3Key Concepts
–Company Profile: The foundational set of data that defines your business identity and regional operational standards within the ERP.
–Operational Toggles: Simple "on/off" switches that allow you to customize how the system handles specific workflows, such as editing received quantities in stock orders or showing specific states in purchasing.
–Custom Attributes: Flexible fields that allow you to define and label product information in a way that best fits your specific business needs.
–Validation: A built-in safety mechanism that prevents the form from being saved if essential information is missing, ensuring data consistency.
3.4Business Value

This feature provides the flexibility needed to keep your ERP configuration aligned with your evolving business requirements. By allowing users to easily update operational settings—such as stock management rules or POS behaviors—without needing technical support, the company can maintain accurate records and ensure that internal processes remain efficient and consistent across the organization.
