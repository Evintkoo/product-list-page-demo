1.1Overview

The Wholesale Settings Card is a configuration component within the "Add Wholesale Customer" page. It allows users to define specific financial and operational rules for wholesale customers, such as credit limits, payment terms, and accounting preferences. The card is designed with sensible defaults to streamline the setup process while ensuring all numeric inputs remain valid (non-negative).

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Credit Configuration: Users enter the allowed payment duration (in days), the maximum credit limit, and the grace period for overdue payments.
2.Pricing and Tax Selection: Users select the appropriate currency, pricing list, and sales tax rate from predefined options.
3.Accounting Setup: Users assign specific accounts for invoices, credit notes, down payments, and deposits to ensure financial data is categorized correctly.
4.Operational Toggles: Users use checkboxes to identify if the customer acts as a card/QRIS provider or an insurance provider, and to toggle whether tax details should appear on printed sales invoices.
1.3Key Concepts
–Credit Terms: The specific timeframe allowed for a customer to complete their payment.
–Overdue Limit: A defined grace period before a payment is officially flagged as late.
–Accounting Accounts: Pre-configured categories that ensure financial transactions (like invoices or deposits) are recorded in the correct ledger.
–Defaults: Pre-selected values (such as Indonesian Rupiah for currency) that speed up data entry while remaining fully editable.
–Validation: A built-in safety feature that prevents users from entering negative numbers in fields where only positive values are logical.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes the complex financial settings required for wholesale relationships into one intuitive interface. By providing clear, pre-filled defaults and validation, it reduces manual data entry errors and ensures that every wholesale customer is set up with consistent accounting and credit policies, ultimately leading to more accurate financial reporting and smoother payment collection processes.
