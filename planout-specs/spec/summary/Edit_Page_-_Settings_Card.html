1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Service Settings Card is a configuration interface within the Lumi ERP system designed to help you manage the core attributes of a service product. This component allows you to quickly toggle service availability, pricing flexibility, and time estimates, ensuring that your service catalog remains accurate and easy to update.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Active Status: You can toggle the "Active" checkbox to enable or disable the service product. The system will show the current status by default.
2.Open Price: You can toggle the "Open Price" checkbox to indicate if the service price is flexible or negotiable.
3.Estimated Time: You can enter the expected duration for the service in minutes. The system validates this input in real-time; if you enter a negative number or an invalid format, an error message will appear to ensure the data remains accurate.
4.Open Estimated Time: You can toggle the "Open Estimated Time" checkbox to indicate if the service duration is flexible or subject to change.
1.3Key Concepts
–Active: A status indicator that determines if the service is currently available for selection or booking.
–Open Price: A setting that signals that the price for this service is not fixed and may be determined at the time of sale.
–Estimated Time: The expected duration of the service, measured in minutes, used for scheduling and planning purposes.
–Open Estimated Time: A setting that signals that the duration of the service is flexible or variable rather than fixed.
–Real-time Validation: An automated check that immediately alerts you if the information entered (such as duration) does not meet the required criteria.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the management of service products by providing a centralized, intuitive interface for configuration. By including real-time validation for time estimates, the system prevents data entry errors, ensuring that scheduling and pricing reports remain reliable. This allows your team to maintain an up-to-date service catalog with minimal effort and reduced risk of operational errors.
