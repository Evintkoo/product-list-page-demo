1.1Overview

The Store Pricing component is a dedicated section within the Lumi ERP service product detail page. Its purpose is to provide clear, at-a-glance visibility into the pricing structure of a specific service, ensuring that users can easily distinguish between the base price and the final price including taxes.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The component displays a "STORE PRICING" label in blue, accompanied by a dollar icon, to clearly identify the section.
2.Data Retrieval: The system pulls the base price and the tax-inclusive price associated with the specific service product.
3.Currency Formatting: The system automatically formats these numerical values into the local currency (IDR) to ensure consistency and readability.
4.Final Presentation: The component displays the base price followed by the tax-inclusive amount in parentheses (e.g., "Rp 100.000 (Rp 110.000 Inc Tax)") within a clean, bordered card layout.
1.3Key Concepts
–Base Price: The standard cost of the service before any additional tax calculations are applied.
–Tax-Inclusive Price: The final amount a customer pays, which includes all applicable taxes.
–Currency Formatting: A standardized way of displaying monetary values (using the IDR format) to ensure the information is professional and easy for the user to interpret.
–Read-Only Display: The pricing information is presented for informational purposes only and cannot be edited directly within this specific component.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves transparency for users by providing immediate clarity on service costs. By explicitly showing both the base price and the tax-inclusive total, the system reduces potential confusion regarding final costs, helps users make informed decisions, and ensures that financial information is presented in a consistent, professional format across the Lumi ERP platform.
