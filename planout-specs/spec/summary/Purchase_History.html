1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a centralized view of all procurement activity for a specific product. It allows users to track where items were sourced, how much was purchased, and the associated costs. The interface is designed to help users quickly identify transaction trends and access detailed shipment documentation.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Automatic Loading: When you open the page for a specific product, the system automatically retrieves and displays the complete purchase history for that item.
2.Filtering: You can narrow down the list of purchases by selecting a specific supplier from the dropdown menu.
3.Search Execution: After selecting a supplier, clicking the "Search" button refreshes the table to show only the records relevant to your choice.
4.Reviewing Details: The table displays key information for each transaction, including the delivery document number, supplier name, quantity, unit price, and the date of the purchase.
5.Accessing Documents: You can click on any document number link to navigate directly to the full details of that specific shipment.
6.Quick Actions: At any time, you can use the "QR Code" or "Barcode" buttons to display or print product identification labels for scanning purposes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Delivery Document: A unique reference number for a shipment that acts as a link to deeper, more granular information about that specific transaction.
–Supplier Filter: A tool that allows you to isolate purchase records by the vendor, making it easier to audit or review specific business relationships.
–Sorting: The ability to organize the purchase list by date, helping you view the most recent or oldest transactions first.
–Product Identification: Built-in access to QR codes and barcodes, which simplifies inventory management and physical product tracking.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a "single source of truth" for product procurement. By consolidating supplier data, pricing, and delivery history into one view, users can quickly verify supply chain consistency, audit past costs, and easily retrieve shipment documentation without searching through physical files or separate systems.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a clear, automated view of every time a specific product has been purchased. When you open a product page, the system instantly retrieves and displays a comprehensive list of past procurement records, allowing you to see exactly when, from whom, and at what price the item was acquired.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Automatic Trigger: As soon as you navigate to a product page, the system automatically identifies the product and requests its specific purchase history.
2.Validation: The system confirms that a valid product is selected to ensure the correct data is retrieved.
3.Data Retrieval: The system searches the database for all records associated with that product.
4.Organization: The records are automatically sorted so that the most recent purchases appear at the top of the list.
5.Display: The compiled list—including supplier details, quantities, pricing, and dates—is presented to you in a table format on the screen.
2.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Record: A single entry documenting a specific transaction, including the supplier, the amount bought, the cost per unit, and the date of the purchase.
–Automated Loading: The feature functions without manual input; the history is fetched and displayed immediately upon viewing the product.
–Chronological Sorting: The list is organized by date, ensuring the most relevant and recent information is always the first thing you see.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory management and procurement oversight by providing instant visibility into supply trends. By having immediate access to historical pricing and supplier performance for every product, your team can make more informed purchasing decisions, negotiate better rates, and quickly track the lifecycle of your inventory without needing to search through manual logs or external reports.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a clear, organized view of all procurement activity for a specific product. By navigating to a product's details, users can instantly see a comprehensive list of every time that item was purchased, including details about the supplier, the quantity ordered, the cost, and when the transaction occurred.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When you open the Purchase History page for a specific product, the system automatically identifies which product you are viewing.
2.Validation: The system confirms that the selected product exists in the database. If the product cannot be found, an error message is displayed.
3.Data Retrieval: The system searches the purchase records and filters them to include only those associated with the specific product ID.
4.Sorting: The retrieved records are automatically organized by date, with the most recent purchases appearing at the top of the list.
5.Display: The final list of purchase records is presented in a table format for easy review.
3.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Record: A single entry documenting a specific transaction, containing details such as the supplier name, quantity, price, and date.
–Product ID: A unique identifier used by the system to ensure that the purchase history displayed matches the exact product you are currently viewing.
–Chronological Sorting: The method of organizing data by date, ensuring that the most relevant and recent information is prioritized for the user.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory transparency and decision-making by allowing staff to quickly audit supply trends. By having immediate access to supplier names, historical pricing, and order volumes, the business can better track spending patterns, evaluate supplier performance, and make more informed decisions regarding future reordering.
