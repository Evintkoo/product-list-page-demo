1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a centralized view of all procurement activity for a specific product. It allows users to track when items were purchased, from which suppliers, in what quantities, and at what price. The interface is designed to be interactive, enabling users to filter by supplier, sort by date, and quickly access detailed shipment documentation or product identification codes (QR and Barcodes).

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Automatic Loading: When the page opens, the system automatically retrieves all purchase records for the current product, sorted by date by default.
2.Data Display: The purchase history is presented in a table showing the delivery document number, supplier name, purchase date, quantity, and unit price.
3.Filtering: Users can select a specific supplier from a dropdown menu and click the "Search" button to narrow down the list to relevant transactions.
4.Sorting: Users can click on the "Date" column header to reorder the list as needed.
5.Document Access: Each delivery document number is a clickable link that takes the user directly to the full details of that specific shipment.
6.Product Identification: Users can click dedicated buttons to view or print the product’s QR code or barcode for scanning purposes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Record: A historical entry detailing a specific transaction, including the supplier, date, quantity, and cost.
–Delivery Document Link: A direct shortcut from the history table to the original shipment paperwork for auditing or verification.
–Supplier Filter: A tool that allows users to isolate transactions associated with a specific vendor.
–Product Identification (QR/Barcode): Digital representations of the product that facilitate quick scanning and inventory management.
–Sortable Columns: Interactive headers that allow users to organize data chronologically to better understand purchasing trends.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing immediate visibility into a product's supply chain history. By consolidating transaction data, document links, and identification tools in one place, staff can quickly verify past costs, identify preferred suppliers, and manage inventory more effectively without needing to search through physical files or separate software modules.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a clear, chronological view of every time a specific product has been purchased. When you view a product's details, the system automatically retrieves and displays a complete list of past procurement records, allowing you to track supplier information, quantities, and pricing over time without manual searching.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Automatic Trigger: When you open the product details page, the system automatically identifies the product you are viewing.
2.Data Retrieval: The system searches the database for all purchase records linked specifically to that product.
3.Sorting: The records are organized automatically, showing the most recent purchases at the top of the list.
4.Display: The information—including the supplier, date, quantity, and price—is populated into a table for easy review. If no previous purchases exist, the system will simply show an empty list.
2.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Record: A historical entry containing details about a specific transaction, such as who supplied the item, when it was bought, how much was ordered, and the cost per unit.
–Chronological Sorting: The practice of ordering data by date so that the most recent activity is always the most visible.
–Product Association: The link between a specific item in your inventory and the history of transactions related to that item.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves inventory transparency and decision-making by providing instant access to historical procurement data. By seeing exactly when and at what price a product was last purchased, your team can better manage supplier relationships, identify price trends, and ensure more accurate budgeting and forecasting for future orders.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Purchase History feature provides a clear, chronological view of all past procurement activities for a specific product. When viewing a product in the system, users can instantly see a detailed list of every time that item was purchased, including who supplied it, when it was bought, the quantity ordered, and the price paid. This ensures that procurement teams have immediate access to historical data to inform future purchasing decisions.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system identifies the specific product currently being viewed by the user.
2.The system automatically requests all associated purchase records for that product.
3.The system retrieves the relevant data, ensuring that the most recent purchases appear at the top of the list.
4.The retrieved information is displayed in a table format for the user to review.
5.If no previous purchases exist for the product, the system will display an empty list rather than an error.
6.If the system encounters an issue (such as an invalid product ID or a temporary connection problem), it will provide an appropriate notification to the user.
3.3Key Concepts
–Purchase History: A comprehensive log of all past transactions associated with a specific item, used for tracking supply trends.
–Chronological Sorting: The automatic organization of records by date, ensuring the newest transactions are always visible first.
–Data Integrity: The system’s ability to ensure that every purchase record is accurately linked to the correct product, preventing data mismatches.
3.4Business Value

This feature eliminates the need for manual record-keeping or searching through external files to understand a product's procurement background. By centralizing this information, the business can:

–Quickly verify past pricing to negotiate better deals with suppliers.
–Monitor supply consistency by reviewing historical quantities and supplier names.
–Improve inventory planning by having easy access to the frequency and timing of past orders.
