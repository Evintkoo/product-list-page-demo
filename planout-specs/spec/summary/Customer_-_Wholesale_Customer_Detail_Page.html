1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Wholesale Customer Detail Page provides a comprehensive, read-only view of a specific wholesale customer's profile within the Lumi ERP system. This page centralizes all relevant business information—ranging from contact details and tax status to credit settings and account mappings—allowing staff to quickly review customer data in one place.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user opens the page, the system automatically identifies the specific customer ID from the web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the full customer profile from the database.
3.Loading & Error Handling: While the data is being retrieved, a loading indicator is shown. If the customer cannot be found or a connection error occurs, the system displays a clear error message.
4.Information Display: Once loaded, the customer's information is organized into several logical sections (cards) for easy reading:

* Details: Core identity, contact information, and salesperson details.

* Changes: Audit trail showing when the record was created or last updated.

* Tax Information: Essential tax identifiers (NPWP, NPPKP, NITKU).

* Settings: Financial configurations, including credit limits, payment terms, and accounting accounts.

* Data Mapping, Group, and Prices: Specific operational categories for the customer.

5.Navigation: Users can return to the main customer list or click an "Edit" button to transition to the modification page if updates are required.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: The page is designed for viewing information; data cannot be changed directly on this screen to ensure data integrity.
–Audit Trail: A dedicated section that tracks the history of the record, showing who created or updated the information and when.
–Financial Configuration: A set of specific settings (such as credit limits and payment terms) that define how the system handles transactions for that specific wholesale customer.
–Data Mapping: A section that links the customer to specific internal categories or sales representatives, ensuring the customer is correctly categorized within the ERP.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a "single source of truth" for wholesale customer data. By consolidating complex information—such as tax status, credit limits, and account settings—into one organized view, staff can make informed decisions faster, reduce the time spent searching for scattered information, and ensure that customer records are accurately maintained and audited.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Wholesale Customer Detail Page provides a comprehensive, read-only view of a specific customer's profile within the Lumi ERP system. This page acts as a centralized dashboard, pulling together all relevant business, financial, and administrative data associated with a wholesale customer so that staff can quickly reference account details, tax status, and credit settings.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user navigates to a specific customer's detail page, the system identifies the unique customer ID from the web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system automatically requests the full customer record from the database using that unique ID.
3.Validation:

* If the customer is found, the system proceeds to organize the data.

* If the customer does not exist, the system displays a "Not Found" notification.

* If a technical error occurs during the request, the system displays an appropriate error message.

4.Information Mapping: The retrieved data is sorted into logical categories (such as contact details, tax information, and account settings) and displayed in the corresponding sections on the screen.
2.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: The page is designed for viewing information; data cannot be edited directly from this screen to ensure record integrity.
–Audit Trail: The "Changes" section tracks the history of the record, including when it was created or last updated and by whom.
–Financial Configuration: This includes critical business settings such as credit limits, payment terms, and specific accounting accounts (e.g., invoice or deposit accounts) used for financial reporting.
–Tax Compliance: Dedicated fields for tax identification numbers (NPWP, NPPKP, NITKU) ensure that all regulatory information is easily accessible for billing and compliance purposes.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines operations by eliminating the need to search through multiple screens or physical files to find customer information. By providing a single, organized view of a wholesale customer—ranging from their contact person and assigned salesperson to their specific credit limits and tax status—staff can make faster, more informed decisions regarding sales, credit approvals, and account management.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Wholesale Customer Detail Page is a centralized view designed to provide a comprehensive look at a specific customer's profile within the Lumi ERP system. This page aggregates all relevant information—ranging from basic contact details and tax documentation to financial settings and account history—into a single, easy-to-read interface. It is designed as a read-only reference tool to ensure staff can quickly verify customer data and account configurations.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When a user selects a specific customer from the system, the application sends a request to retrieve that customer's unique profile using their identification code.
2.Data Retrieval: The system searches the database for the matching customer record.
3.Validation:

* If the customer record is found, the system proceeds to gather all associated details.

* If the record does not exist, the system returns a "Customer not found" notification.

4.Information Display: The retrieved data is organized and presented across several logical sections on the page:

* Details: Core identity, contact information, and banking details.

* Settings: Account configurations, tax information, and credit limits.

* Data Mapping: Assigned salesperson information.

* Prices: The specific price list assigned to the customer.

* Changes: Audit logs showing when the record was created or last updated and by whom.

3.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only View: A display-only format that allows users to view information without the risk of accidentally modifying sensitive customer data.
–Audit Trail: A record of "who and when" regarding the creation and updates of a customer profile, ensuring accountability and data transparency.
–Financial Configuration: Specific settings related to how a customer is billed, including credit limits, payment terms, and tax-related accounts.
–Customer Profile: The complete collection of data points associated with a single entity, including contact, tax, and operational settings.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines operations by eliminating the need to navigate through multiple screens to find customer information. By providing a "single source of truth" for wholesale customer data, staff can:

* Improve Response Time: Quickly answer customer inquiries regarding their account status, credit limits, or assigned pricing.

* Enhance Accuracy: Ensure that sales and finance teams are looking at the same, up-to-date information, reducing errors in billing and order processing.

* Support Compliance: Easily access tax documentation (such as NPWP and NPPKP) and audit logs, which are essential for maintaining accurate financial records and regulatory compliance.
