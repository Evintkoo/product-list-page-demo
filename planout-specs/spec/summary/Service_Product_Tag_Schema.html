1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Service Product Tag feature introduces a standardized way to categorize and label service products within the Lumi ERP system. By implementing a formal data structure, we ensure that every tag associated with a service is consistent, accurately tracked, and properly validated before it is saved to the system. This prevents data errors and ensures that the tagging system remains reliable for reporting and selection purposes.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Entry: When a tag is created or updated, the system collects the necessary information, including the tag's name, its description, and its status (active or inactive).
2.Validation Check: The system automatically verifies that all mandatory information—specifically the unique tag ID, the associated service product ID, and the tag name—is present.
3.Format Verification: The system confirms that the data provided is in the correct format (e.g., ensuring IDs are numbers and names are text).
4.Status Assignment: The system checks the "active" status to determine if the tag should be available for selection in dropdown menus across the platform.
5.System Acceptance: If all checks pass, the tag is accepted and stored; if any required information is missing or formatted incorrectly, the system rejects the entry to maintain data integrity.
1.3Key Concepts
–Service Product Tag: A label or category assigned to a specific service product to help with organization and filtering.
–Validation: An automated "quality check" that ensures data is complete and correctly formatted before it is processed by the system.
–Active Status: A toggle that controls whether a tag is currently visible and usable in the application, allowing you to "retire" old tags without deleting their history.
–Required Fields: Essential pieces of information (ID, Service ID, and Name) that must be provided for a tag to exist within the system.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Data Accuracy: By enforcing strict validation rules, we eliminate "dirty data" and ensure that all service tags are correctly linked to their respective products.
–Operational Efficiency: Standardizing how tags are handled allows for smoother integration across the ERP, ensuring that dropdown menus and search filters always display reliable, up-to-date options.
–Flexibility: The ability to toggle tags as "active" or "inactive" gives your team control over the tagging environment, allowing you to manage your service catalog as your business needs evolve without losing historical data.
