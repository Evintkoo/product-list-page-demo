1.1Overview

The Customer Group Card is a dedicated section within the Lumi ERP interface designed to help you manage how customers are categorized. It provides a simple way to view or update a customer’s assigned group and offers quick access to create new groups if the one you need does not yet exist.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.View Header: The card is clearly identified by a "CUSTOMER GROUP" header and a visual icon, making it easy to locate within the customer profile.
2.Select Group: You can use the "Group" dropdown menu to see a list of all available categories and assign the customer to the appropriate one.
3.Create New Group: If you need to assign a customer to a group that isn't in the list, you can click the "New Group" button.
4.Manage via Modal: Clicking the "New Group" button opens a pop-up window (modal) where you can define and save a new customer group without leaving your current page.
1.3Key Concepts
–Customer Group: A classification label used to organize customers, allowing for better reporting and targeted management.
–Dropdown Selection: A standard menu that displays all existing groups, ensuring you can quickly reassign a customer with a single click.
–Modal Window: A focused pop-up screen that allows you to perform a specific task—in this case, creating a new group—without navigating away from the customer's profile.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines customer management by centralizing group assignments in one place. By allowing users to create new groups on the fly, it reduces administrative friction and ensures that customer data remains organized and up-to-date without requiring the user to switch between different areas of the software.
