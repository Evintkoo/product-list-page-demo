1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Service Product Edit page allows users to modify the details and pricing of an existing service within the Lumi ERP system. When a user navigates to this page, the system automatically retrieves the current information for the selected service and populates the form fields, ensuring that users can easily review and update specific attributes such as descriptions, pricing, and tax configurations.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: Upon opening the page, the system identifies the specific service product and automatically fetches its current data from the database.
2.Data Population: The retrieved information is used to pre-fill all form sections, including the Info Card (name, SKU, description), Pricing Card (price), and Tax Card (tax settings and account codes).
3.User Modification: The user updates the necessary fields. The system performs real-time checks to ensure the name is provided and the price remains a positive value.
4.Saving Changes: Once the user clicks the "Save" button, the system submits the updated information to the server.
5.Navigation: Upon a successful save, the user is automatically redirected back to the main product list. If the user chooses to discard their changes, the "Cancel" button returns them to the previous screen without saving.
1.3Key Concepts
–Info Card: A section containing core identification details for the service, such as its unique SKU, name, and descriptive information.
–Store Pricing Card: A dedicated area for managing the financial aspect of the service, specifically the price and tax inclusion settings.
–Tax Card: A specialized section for configuring tax-related settings, including tax types, account associations, and measurement units required for accurate financial reporting.
–Validation: Built-in safeguards that prevent errors, such as ensuring the product name is not left blank and that the price is not set to a negative number.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the maintenance of service offerings by providing a centralized, intuitive interface for updates. By pre-filling existing data, it reduces manual entry time and minimizes the risk of errors. Furthermore, the built-in validation ensures that all service records remain consistent and accurate, which is critical for reliable financial tracking and tax compliance within the ERP.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Service Product Edit feature allows users to modify the details and pricing of existing service products within the Lumi ERP system. This functionality ensures that service information, such as descriptions, tax settings, and pricing, remains accurate and up-to-date.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When a user navigates to the edit page for a specific service product, the system automatically retrieves the current record's information.
2.Form Population: The retrieved data is used to pre-fill all fields, including text boxes and dropdown menus (such as tax categories, account assignments, and product types), ensuring the user sees the most recent version of the product.
3.User Updates: The user modifies the necessary fields within the Info, Tax, Settings, or Pricing cards.
4.Validation: Upon clicking "Save," the system performs a check to ensure all mandatory fields—specifically the SKU, Name, Tax settings, and Price—are completed. If any required information is missing, the system displays an error message to the user.
5.Submission and Confirmation: If the data is valid, the system saves the changes. Once successfully updated, the user is automatically redirected to the Service Product Detail page to view the finalized changes.
2.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A specific service offering managed within the ERP, containing details like pricing, tax rules, and accounting codes.
–Form Validation: A safety check that prevents the system from saving incomplete or invalid data, ensuring data integrity.
–Pre-filling: The process of automatically populating form fields with existing data so the user only needs to change what is necessary rather than re-entering all information.
–Mandatory Fields: Specific pieces of information (SKU, Name, Tax, Other Tax, and Price) that must be provided for the system to process the update.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines administrative tasks by providing a reliable and efficient way to maintain service catalogs. By allowing quick updates to pricing and tax configurations, the business can respond rapidly to market changes or regulatory updates, while the built-in validation reduces the risk of human error and ensures consistent data across the ERP.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Service Product Edit feature allows users to modify the details and pricing of existing service offerings within the Lumi ERP system. This functionality ensures that your service catalog remains accurate and up-to-date by providing a structured way to update key information, such as tax settings, pricing, and product categorization.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Loading: When you navigate to the edit page for a specific service, the system automatically retrieves the current information for that product. This populates all fields and dropdown menus with the existing data so you can see exactly what is currently on file.
2.Data Modification: You can update the product’s name, description, pricing, and various settings (such as tax codes, tags, and account associations) using the provided form fields and dropdowns.
3.Validation: When you click "Save," the system performs a check to ensure all mandatory information—specifically the SKU, Name, Tax settings, Other Tax settings, and Price—is present.
4.Error Handling: If any required information is missing, the system will display an error message, allowing you to correct the input before trying to save again.
5.Completion: Once the data is successfully validated and saved, the system updates the record with a new timestamp and redirects you to the product detail page to view your changes.
3.3Key Concepts
–Service Product: A specific service offering in your catalog that includes pricing, tax configurations, and accounting details.
–Mandatory Fields: Essential pieces of information (SKU, Name, Tax, Other Tax, and Price) that must be provided for the system to process the update.
–Dropdown Population: The process of automatically filling selection menus with existing data, ensuring that you are choosing from valid, pre-defined options for categories like tax codes or product types.
–Validation: A built-in safety check that prevents incomplete or incorrect data from being saved to the system.
3.4Business Value

This feature provides a reliable and efficient way to manage service pricing and tax compliance. By enforcing data validation, it reduces the risk of human error, ensuring that financial and tax-related information remains consistent across the ERP. This ultimately supports accurate reporting and seamless billing processes.
