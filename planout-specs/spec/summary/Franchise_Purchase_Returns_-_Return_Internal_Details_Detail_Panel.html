1.1Overview

The Franchise Purchase Return Detail Panel is a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system that provides a comprehensive summary of a specific purchase return. It acts as a "digital file" for a return transaction, allowing users to quickly review all relevant administrative, logistical, and status-related information in one organized location.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: When a user selects a specific purchase return, the system automatically fetches the relevant details from the database.
2.Loading State: While the information is being retrieved, the system manages the process to ensure a smooth user experience.
3.Displaying Left-Column Details: Once loaded, the panel displays core identification and logistical data, including document and reference numbers, the originating store, the destination supplier and warehouse, user audit trails (who created/updated the record), and any associated notes.
4.Displaying Right-Column Details: The panel simultaneously displays status-based timestamps and financial references, including creation and update dates, approval and dispatch dates, and the associated accounting journal reference.
5.Handling Empty Data: If a specific field (such as an approval date or a note) has not yet been populated, the system displays a placeholder (a dash) to keep the interface clean and readable.
1.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Return: A formal record of inventory being sent back from a franchise store to a supplier.
–Detail Panel: A structured layout that organizes complex transaction data into two clear columns for easy reading.
–Audit Trail: Information showing who created or modified a record and when, ensuring accountability and transparency.
–Status-Driven Fields: Specific data points, such as "Approve Date" or "Sent Date," that remain blank until the return reaches those specific stages in its lifecycle.
–Journal Reference: A link to the accounting entry associated with the return, ensuring the inventory movement is correctly recorded in the financial system.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing all critical information regarding a purchase return. By providing a clear, at-a-glance view of the return's status, history, and logistical details, staff can quickly verify the progress of a return, resolve discrepancies faster, and maintain accurate records without needing to cross-reference multiple documents or systems.
