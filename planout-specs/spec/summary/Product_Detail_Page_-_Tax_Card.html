1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Tax Card is a new component on the Product Detail Page designed to provide a clear, at-a-glance view of tax-related information for a specific product. This feature ensures that essential tax data is easily accessible to users while maintaining a clean, read-only format that prevents accidental changes to sensitive financial information.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system retrieves the product information from the database.
2.The Tax Card component identifies the specific tax details associated with that product.
3.The system displays the Tax rate (e.g., PPN), the Tax Product Code, and the Tax Unit of Measure in a dedicated section on the page.
4.If any specific piece of information is missing for a product, the system will display "N/A" to ensure the user knows the data is unavailable rather than simply blank.
5.The information is presented in a read-only format, ensuring users can view the data without the ability to edit it directly from this screen.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-only: A display setting that allows users to view information without the ability to modify or delete it, ensuring data integrity.
–Tax Product Code: A specific identifier used to categorize the product for tax reporting and invoicing purposes.
–Tax Unit of Measure: The standard unit used to calculate or report tax for the product (e.g., per item, per weight, etc.).
–Data Placeholder: A default label (N/A) used to maintain a consistent layout when specific product information is not currently available in the system.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational transparency by centralizing critical tax information directly on the product page. By providing a clear, read-only view, it reduces the risk of manual errors during data entry and ensures that staff have immediate access to the correct tax codes and rates required for accurate invoicing and regulatory compliance.
