1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Purchase Return (Standard) Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific return transaction. It allows users to track the status of a return, review itemized details, and manage the workflow from the initial draft stage through to final approval by the supplier.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Loading: When you open the page, the system automatically retrieves all relevant details for the selected purchase return. A loading indicator is displayed while this data is being fetched.
2.Reviewing Information: The page displays key information, including the document number, status, general reference details, linked documents (such as Purchase Orders or Invoices), and audit logs (who created or updated the record).
3.Workflow Management:

* Draft Stage: You can edit the document, print it, or send it to the supplier.

* Sent to Supplier Stage: Once sent, you can print the document or begin the approval process.

* Approval Process: Clicking "Approve" triggers a confirmation modal. You will then review the quantities and enter the amounts confirmed by the supplier.

* Finalization: After entering the supplier-confirmed quantities, a final approval step saves the data, moving the document to the "Approved" status.

4.Itemized Summary: The page includes a table listing all returned items, showing quantities (remaining, warehouse, returned, and confirmed), pricing, and the reason for the return. A summary section at the bottom provides totals for these quantities and financial data.
1.3Key Concepts
–Workflow Status: A tracker that shows the current stage of the return (Draft, Sent to Supplier, or Approved).
–Supplier Confirmed Quantity: An editable field used during the approval process to record the exact quantity the supplier has agreed to accept as a return.
–Linked Documents: Direct links to related records, such as the original Purchase Order, Purchase Delivery, or Invoice, allowing for easy navigation between related transactions.
–Audit Trail: A section displaying timestamps and user information for when the document was created, updated, sent, or approved.
–Financial Summary: A breakdown of the return's value, including subtotals, discounts, taxes, and any allocated credit amounts.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes the management of purchase returns, ensuring that all stakeholders have a clear, real-time view of the return status. By enforcing a structured workflow—from drafting to supplier confirmation—the system reduces manual errors, improves inventory accuracy, and provides a transparent audit trail for financial reconciliation.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Purchase Return (Standard) detail page provides a comprehensive view of items being returned to a supplier. This feature allows users to review return documentation, track the status of the return process, and manage the workflow from the initial draft through to final approval. It ensures that all financial data, inventory quantities, and audit information are clearly visible and organized for easy verification.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Loading: When you open a specific Purchase Return record, the system automatically retrieves all relevant details, including the document numbers, associated purchase orders, invoices, and the list of items being returned.
2.Data Aggregation: The system calculates the totals for various quantity categories (such as "Return," "Warehouse," and "Confirmed" quantities) to provide a summary row at the bottom of the item list.
3.Reviewing Details: You can view financial summaries (subtotals, taxes, and discounts), audit logs (who created or updated the record), and links to related documents like journals or payments.
4.Sending to Supplier: Once the return is ready, you can trigger the "Send to Supplier" action. This updates the document status and records the exact time it was sent.
5.Approval: After the supplier confirms the return, you can use the approval function to finalize the record. This updates the status to "Approved/Confirmed" and logs the approval date.
2.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Return: A formal document used to track goods being sent back to a supplier, including the reason for the return and the quantities involved.
–Workflow Status: The current stage of the return process, which moves from "Draft" to "Sent to Supplier" and finally to "Approved/Confirmed."
–Quantity Tracking: A breakdown of item counts, including what is currently in the warehouse, what is being returned, and what the supplier has officially confirmed.
–Financial Summary: A clear view of the monetary impact of the return, including subtotals, discounts, taxes, and any allocated credit amounts.
–Audit Trail: Automatically captured information regarding who created or modified the record and when key actions (like sending or approving) occurred.
2.4Business Value

This feature centralizes the entire return process, reducing manual errors by automatically calculating totals and linking related financial documents. By providing a clear, step-by-step workflow, it ensures that inventory records remain accurate and that there is full transparency between the company and the supplier regarding returned goods and credit adjustments.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Purchase Return (Standard) detail page provides a comprehensive view of a return transaction initiated with a supplier. This feature allows users to see the full lifecycle of a return—from the initial request and financial breakdown to the final approval status. It acts as a central hub for tracking returned items, associated invoices, payments, and warehouse movements, ensuring that all inventory and accounting records remain accurate.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Record Retrieval: When a user selects a specific purchase return, the system gathers all relevant data, including the primary document details, linked financial records (like invoices and payments), and the list of items being returned.
2.Itemized Breakdown: The system displays a detailed table of all returned products, showing quantities (such as what was returned versus what the supplier confirmed), pricing, and the specific reason for each return.
3.Summary Calculation: The system automatically calculates and displays totals for all quantity columns and financial fields (subtotal, tax, discounts, and final total) to provide an immediate snapshot of the return's value.
4.Workflow Tracking: The system displays the current status of the return (e.g., Draft, Sent to Supplier, or Approved) along with key audit timestamps, such as when the document was created, sent, or approved.
5.Approval View: For management purposes, the system provides a simplified view that highlights only the essential information—such as the document number, total value, and approval status—to facilitate quick review and authorization.
3.3Key Concepts
–Purchase Return: A formal record documenting the return of goods to a supplier, which often triggers adjustments to inventory levels and financial accounts.
–Workflow Status: The current stage of the return process, which helps users understand if a document is still being drafted, has been sent to the supplier, or has been finalized.
–Audit Trail: A collection of timestamps and user identifiers that track who created or updated the record and when key actions (like approval) occurred.
–Linked Documentation: References to related records, such as the original Purchase Order, the Invoice, and the specific Warehouse involved, allowing users to navigate easily between related business documents.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational transparency and financial accuracy by centralizing all data related to a return. By providing a clear view of both item quantities and financial totals, it reduces the risk of discrepancies between warehouse records and accounting books. Furthermore, the integrated workflow and audit trail ensure that every return is properly authorized and tracked, helping the business maintain strong relationships with suppliers and clear accountability for inventory adjustments.
