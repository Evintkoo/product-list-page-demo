1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Customer Products Filter component is a new tool designed to help you quickly find specific information within the customer products list. Instead of scrolling through a long list of data, you can now narrow down your view by selecting specific criteria. This feature provides a clean, user-friendly interface to refine your search results before updating the table.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Select Criteria: You choose your desired options from three dropdown menus: Membership Tier, Vehicle, and Source. You can use any combination of these filters, or leave them blank if you wish to see all results.
2.Initiate Search: Once you have selected your preferred filters, click the "Search" button.
3.Update Results: The system takes your selected criteria and automatically refreshes the product table to display only the items that match your choices.
1.3Key Concepts
–Membership Tier: A category filter that allows you to view products associated with specific customer membership levels.
–Vehicle: A category filter that allows you to isolate products related to a specific vehicle type.
–Source: A category filter that allows you to narrow down products based on their origin or acquisition channel.
–Filter Refresh: The action of updating the table data based on your current selections, ensuring you only see the information relevant to your search.
1.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves efficiency by reducing the time spent manually searching for specific customer products. By allowing users to filter by membership, vehicle, and source, the system enables more precise data analysis and faster decision-making. It transforms a large, overwhelming list into a manageable set of relevant information, directly supporting better customer service and inventory management workflows.
