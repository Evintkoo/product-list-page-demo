1.1Overview

The Dimensions Card is a new component for the Lumi ERP product detail page. Its purpose is to provide a clear, at-a-glance view of a product's physical specifications—specifically its volume and weight. This ensures that users have immediate access to essential logistical information directly within the product profile.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Retrieval: The system automatically pulls the specific volume and weight information associated with the product currently being viewed.
2.Validation: The system checks if the information is available. If the data is missing or incomplete, the system will display "N/A" instead of leaving a blank space, ensuring the page remains clean and professional.
3.Display: The validated information is presented in a dedicated section on the product page, clearly labeled as "Volume (Mililiter)" and "Weight (Gram)."
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only Fields: Information that is displayed for reference purposes only and cannot be edited directly from this view.
–Data Consistency: A standard approach to handling missing information by displaying "N/A," which prevents layout errors and provides a better user experience.
–Product Detail Context: The specific area of the ERP where users view comprehensive information about a single product.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Decision Making: By surfacing physical dimensions directly on the product page, staff can quickly verify if a product meets shipping or storage requirements without needing to consult external documents.
–Enhanced Data Integrity: The use of standardized labels and consistent "N/A" placeholders ensures that the product page remains professional and easy to read, even when certain data points are not yet available in the system.
–Operational Efficiency: Reducing the time spent searching for basic product specifications helps streamline warehouse and logistics workflows.
