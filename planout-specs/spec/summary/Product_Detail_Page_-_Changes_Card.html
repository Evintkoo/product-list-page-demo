1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The "Changes Card" is a new feature on the Product Detail Page that provides a clear, read-only history of a product record. It acts as an audit trail, allowing users to see exactly when a product was first created and the details of its most recent modification. This ensures transparency and accountability for all changes made to product data within the Lumi ERP system.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.When a user opens a specific product page, the system automatically retrieves the audit history for that item.
2.The system displays the original creation date and time of the product record.
3.The system displays the date and time of the most recent update to the record.
4.The system identifies the specific user who performed the last update and notes the type of action they took (such as an edit or update).
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific product.
–Read-Only: Information that is displayed for viewing purposes only and cannot be modified by the user.
–Action Type: A label indicating the nature of the change made to the record, providing context for why the update occurred.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves data governance and operational transparency. By providing a clear history of who changed what and when, teams can easily track the lifecycle of a product, resolve discrepancies faster, and maintain a reliable record of truth for all product-related information.
