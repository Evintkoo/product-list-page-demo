1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Tax Card is a dedicated section within the "Add Service Product" page designed to help users accurately configure tax settings for their services. It provides a structured interface to select primary and secondary taxes, define measurement units, and assign specific accounting codes required for financial reporting and tax compliance (such as PPN and PPh 23).

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The section is clearly identified by a "% TAX" label at the top.
2.Primary Tax Selection: The user selects the main tax (e.g., PPN) from a dropdown menu. This field is mandatory.
3.Secondary Tax Selection: The user selects the "Other Tax" (e.g., PPh 23) from a second mandatory dropdown menu.
4.Measurement & Classification: The user optionally selects the "Tax Unit of Measure" and the "Tax Product Code" (used for e-faktur reporting) to further define the service.
5.Accounting Configuration: The system automatically defaults the "Other Tax Account" and "Other Tax Refund Account" to the PPh 23 account (100.104.003), though these can be adjusted if necessary.
6.Validation: If the user attempts to leave the primary or secondary tax fields empty, the system will display an error message to ensure all required information is captured before saving.
1.3Key Concepts
–Tax: The primary tax applied to the service, such as Value Added Tax (PPN).
–Other Tax: A secondary tax category, specifically used here for withholding taxes like PPh 23.
–Tax Unit of Measure: The standard unit used to calculate or report the tax for the service.
–Tax Product Code: A specific code required for electronic tax invoicing (e-faktur) to ensure compliance.
–Tax Accounts: The specific ledger accounts where tax liabilities and refunds are recorded; these are pre-filled to save time and reduce manual entry errors.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the service creation process by centralizing all tax-related configurations in one place. By providing mandatory fields for primary and secondary taxes and pre-filling accounting codes, the system reduces the risk of human error, ensures consistent financial data, and helps the business remain compliant with local tax reporting requirements (e-faktur).
