1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Tax Information Card is a dedicated section within the Customer Detail page designed to provide quick, read-only access to a customer's essential tax identification numbers. This component ensures that staff can easily verify a customer's tax status without needing to navigate away from the main customer profile.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The system identifies the specific customer being viewed.
2.The page initiates a request to retrieve the customer's profile data.
3.While the data is being retrieved, a loading indicator is displayed to show that the system is working.
4.Once the data is ready, the Tax Information Card populates three specific fields: NPWP, NITKU, and NPPKP.
5.Each field is displayed alongside an information icon that provides a brief explanation of the tax term when hovered over or clicked.
1.3Key Concepts
–NPWP: The Taxpayer Identification Number (Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak) used for tax administration.
–NITKU: The Identity Number for a Place of Business Activity (Nomor Identitas Tempat Kegiatan Usaha), used to identify specific business locations.
–NPPKP: The Taxable Entrepreneur Confirmation Number (Nomor Pengukuhan Pengusaha Kena Pajak), which identifies businesses registered to collect value-added tax.
–Read-only: A display format where information can be viewed but not modified directly within this specific card.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical compliance data, reducing the time staff spend searching for tax documentation. By providing clear labels and helpful tooltips for complex tax identifiers, the system minimizes errors during data verification and ensures that customer records remain accurate and easy to audit.
