1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Customer feature is the central hub of our Mini ERP system. It is designed to maintain a comprehensive profile for every customer, linking together their personal information, vehicle details, membership status, billing preferences, and financial history. By centralizing this data, the system ensures that staff have a 360-degree view of each customer, enabling more efficient service and accurate record-keeping.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Claim Tracking: Records individual service or sales claims associated with a customer.
2.Membership Management: Defines available membership programs and links customers to them to track points and status.
3.Financial Deposits: Manages customer deposit balances and logs every transaction movement.
4.Vehicle Registry: Tracks detailed vehicle information, including make, model, and technical specifications, linked directly to the owner.
5.Pricing & Billing: Stores custom product pricing for specific customers and maintains dedicated billing profiles.
6.Customer Master Data: The core profile that aggregates all the above information, including contact details, tax information, and internal notes.
7.Customer Grouping: Organizes customers into categories to help with reporting and management.
8.Store Association: Ensures every customer record is correctly linked to a specific store location to maintain data integrity across the organization.
1.3Key Concepts
–Customer Profile: The primary record containing contact details, tax IDs, and internal settings for each individual or business.
–Referential Integrity: A safety rule that ensures a customer cannot be linked to a non-existent store, vehicle, or membership, preventing data errors.
–Denormalized Balance: A system-calculated total of a customer's deposits that updates automatically whenever a new transaction is recorded.
–Customer Grouping: A classification tool used to organize customers for easier filtering and business analysis.
–Vehicle Registry: A specialized module that tracks technical vehicle data (like VIN or engine numbers) to support service and sales operations.
1.4Business Value
–Improved Customer Service: Staff can instantly access a customer's full history, including their vehicles, billing preferences, and membership status, reducing the time spent searching for information.
–Data Accuracy: By enforcing strict links between records (such as ensuring a customer belongs to a valid store), the system prevents "orphaned" data and keeps reports reliable.
–Operational Efficiency: Automated features—such as calculating deposit balances and tracking membership points—reduce manual data entry and minimize the risk of human error.
–Scalability: The modular design allows the business to easily add new features (like new membership types or vehicle categories) without disrupting existing customer data.
