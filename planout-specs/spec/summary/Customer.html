1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Customer feature provides a robust database structure for managing customer information within the Lumi ERP system. It is designed to handle various customer types—including retail, wholesale, franchise, and consignment—while supporting complex business requirements like credit limits, tax information, and custom pricing. The system is built to remain fast and responsive even as the number of customer records grows.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Customer Creation: A new customer record is created, capturing essential details such as name, contact information, and business type (e.g., wholesale or retail).
2.Profile Enrichment: Additional details are linked to the customer, including multiple addresses (shipping/billing), membership program enrollments, and specific occupation data.
3.Financial Setup: Financial parameters are configured, such as credit limits, payment terms, and specific accounting accounts (e.g., for deposits or receivables).
4.Operational Management: The system tracks ongoing interactions, such as sales history, product-specific pricing overrides, and any post-sale claims or returns.
5.Data Maintenance: The system automatically updates timestamps and maintains accurate counts (like the number of customers in a specific group) to ensure data integrity without manual intervention.
1.3Key Concepts
–Customer Entity: The central record containing core identity and contact information for an individual or business.
–Sub-Entities: Related data tables that extend the customer profile, such as addresses, billing profiles, and membership details.
–Keyset Pagination: A high-performance method for browsing long lists of customers that ensures the system stays fast as the database grows.
–Trigram GIN Index: A specialized search technology that allows the system to quickly find customers even if you only type a partial name or a "contains" search.
–Sanitized Phone: A system-generated field that strips formatting from phone numbers, ensuring that searches for a phone number are always accurate regardless of how the number was originally typed.
–Atomic Transactions: A safety feature that ensures when you save a customer and their related details (like addresses), the entire update succeeds or fails as one unit, preventing data inconsistencies.
1.4Business Value
–Scalability: By using advanced indexing and efficient pagination, the system ensures that searching and listing customers remains instantaneous, even with thousands of records.
–Data Accuracy: Automated triggers and generated fields (like the sanitized phone number) reduce human error and ensure consistent data quality across the organization.
–Flexibility: The schema supports diverse business models, allowing the same system to manage simple retail customers alongside complex wholesale or franchise accounts with specific pricing and credit rules.
–Operational Efficiency: By centralizing billing, address, and financial data, staff can access a complete 360-degree view of a customer from a single location, speeding up service and administrative tasks.
