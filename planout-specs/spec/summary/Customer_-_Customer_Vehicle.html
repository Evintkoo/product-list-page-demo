1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Customer Vehicle page provides a centralized view of all vehicles registered to a specific customer. This page allows users to quickly identify a customer's fleet, view key technical specifications for each vehicle, and perform management tasks such as adding new vehicles or updating existing records.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Page Initialization: When the page loads, the system automatically identifies the customer from the current web address.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the customer's name to display in the page header and retrieves the full list of vehicles associated with that customer.
3.Display: The vehicle information is presented in a table, showing details like the registration number, make, model, and year. For heavy equipment, additional engine-specific details are displayed.
4.Navigation & Actions:

* Users can click on a vehicle's registration number to open a detailed view of that specific vehicle in a new tab.

* Users can click the "New Vehicle" button to navigate to a form to add a vehicle to the customer's account.

* Users can click the "Edit" button next to any vehicle to update its information.

1.3Key Concepts
–Customer Banner: A prominent header at the top of the page that displays the name of the customer whose vehicles are currently being viewed.
–Vehicle Table: A structured list containing all relevant vehicle data, including technical specifications and engine details.
–Registration Number Link: A clickable identifier for each vehicle that provides quick access to more in-depth vehicle information.
–Action Buttons: Dedicated controls that allow users to either create a new vehicle entry or modify existing vehicle data.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines fleet management by providing a clear, organized view of customer assets. By consolidating vehicle information and providing direct links to add or edit records, the system reduces the time spent searching for data and minimizes manual errors during updates. This ensures that customer records remain accurate and up-to-date, supporting better service and operational efficiency.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Customer Vehicle feature provides a dedicated view within the Lumi ERP system to display a comprehensive list of vehicles associated with a specific customer. This page allows staff to quickly review vehicle details—such as registration, make, model, and engine specifications—and provides direct access to view or edit individual vehicle records.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Data: When a user opens a customer's profile, the system automatically requests the list of all vehicles linked to that specific customer.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the raw vehicle information, including registration numbers, physical characteristics, and engine details.
3.Information Mapping: The system organizes this raw data into a structured table format, ensuring that each piece of information (like "Make" or "Engine Power") appears in its correct column.
4.Action Linking: The system assigns unique identifiers to each vehicle row, enabling the "View" and "Edit" buttons to navigate the user to the correct specific vehicle record.
5.Display: The final list is presented to the user, allowing them to scan the customer's fleet and perform necessary updates.
2.3Key Concepts
–Vehicle List: A centralized table view showing all vehicles owned or managed by a selected customer.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking technical information from the database and translating it into clear, readable labels for the user interface.
–Row Actions: Interactive elements within the table that allow users to click on a specific vehicle to either view its full details or open an edit screen.
–Engine Specifications: Detailed technical attributes (brand, model, year, and power) included in the list to help staff identify the correct vehicle configuration.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Efficiency: Staff can instantly access a customer's entire vehicle history without searching through multiple screens or physical files.
–Data Accuracy: By standardizing how vehicle information is displayed, the system reduces the risk of misinterpreting technical details like engine models or registration numbers.
–Streamlined Workflow: Direct links to "View" and "Edit" actions allow users to transition seamlessly from reviewing a list to performing administrative tasks, saving time during customer interactions.
