1.1Overview

The Retail Purchasing feature provides a clear, organized view of a customer's purchasing history within the Lumi ERP system. It allows users to navigate from a customer's profile to a detailed list of their transactions, where they can review specific purchase documents, track their status, and verify key transaction details at a glance.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Accessing the List: From the Customer Detail page, the user clicks the "Show Purchasing Details" button to navigate to the full list of retail purchases for that specific customer.
2.Viewing the Table: The system displays a table containing all relevant purchase records.
3.Reviewing Transaction Details: Each row in the table provides specific information, including the document number, the date and time of the purchase, the total amount, the store location, the staff member who created the record, and the current status of the transaction.
4.Navigating to Documents: If a user needs more information on a specific transaction, they can click the blue, highlighted Document Number to be taken directly to that specific document's detail page.
1.3Key Concepts
–Document Number: A unique identifier for a purchase that acts as a direct link to the full document details.
–Status Badge: A visual indicator showing the progress of a purchase. "Draft" items appear in grey, while "Completed" items are highlighted in green for quick identification.
–Currency Formatting: Financial amounts are displayed using a standard format (using dots for thousands and commas for decimals) to ensure consistency and readability.
–Read-Only Columns: Most information in the table (such as date, store, and creator) is displayed for reference purposes only, ensuring data integrity.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by centralizing customer purchase data. By providing a clean, searchable, and navigable table, staff can quickly resolve customer inquiries regarding past purchases, verify transaction statuses without manual searching, and easily drill down into specific documents when necessary. This reduces the time spent on administrative tasks and provides a better experience for both the staff and the customer.
