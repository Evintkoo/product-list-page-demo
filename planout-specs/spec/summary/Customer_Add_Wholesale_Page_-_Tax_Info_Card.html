1.1Overview

The Tax Information section is a new component within the wholesale customer registration form. Its purpose is to allow users to accurately capture and record essential Indonesian tax identification numbers for business customers. The system includes built-in validation to ensure that the data entered meets the required format standards, helping to reduce errors during the onboarding process.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.The user navigates to the Tax Information section of the wholesale customer registration form.
2.The user enters the customer's NPWP (Taxpayer Registration Number) into the designated field.
3.As the user types, the system automatically checks the NPWP format to ensure it contains the correct number of digits. If the format is incorrect, an error message appears immediately.
4.The user enters the customer's NPPKP (Taxable Entrepreneur Number) into the provided field.
5.The user enters the customer's NITKU (Business Activity Location Tax Number) into the final field.
6.The system maintains these entries as part of the customer's profile data for submission.
1.3Key Concepts
–NPWP: The primary Taxpayer Registration Number used in Indonesia to identify a business for tax purposes.
–NPPKP: The Taxable Entrepreneur Number, required for businesses that are registered to collect and pay Value Added Tax (VAT).
–NITKU: The Business Activity Location Tax Number, used to identify specific business locations for tax reporting.
–Real-time Validation: A feature that checks the accuracy of the data as the user types, providing instant feedback so corrections can be made before the form is submitted.
1.4Business Value
–Data Accuracy: By enforcing specific formatting rules for tax numbers, the system prevents common data entry mistakes, ensuring that tax records are reliable and compliant.
–Efficiency: Real-time validation saves time by alerting the user to errors immediately, rather than waiting until the end of the registration process to flag issues.
–Regulatory Compliance: Capturing these specific tax identifiers is essential for wholesale operations in Indonesia, ensuring the business remains compliant with local tax reporting requirements.
