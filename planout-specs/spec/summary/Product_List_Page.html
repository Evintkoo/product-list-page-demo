1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product List Page provides a centralized view for users to manage and browse the company's product catalog. It allows users to view key product information in a structured table, search for specific items using filters, and easily navigate to detailed product records or create new entries.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page is first opened, the system automatically retrieves the full list of products and displays them in the table.
2.Filtering: Users can refine the list by entering search terms or selecting a specific brand from the filter bar.
3.Applying Search: After adjusting the filters, the user clicks the "Apply Search" button to refresh the table with the relevant results.
4.Viewing Details: Users can click on a product name within the table to be redirected to a dedicated page containing full details for that specific item.
5.Adding Products: A dedicated button is available to quickly transition the user to the "Add Product" page to create new catalog entries.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of controls at the top of the page that allows users to narrow down the product list based on specific criteria like name or brand.
–Product Table: The main display area that organizes product data (such as SKU, Name, Brand, Code, Tax, and Price) into rows and columns for easy comparison.
–Pagination: A system that breaks the product list into manageable chunks (pages) to ensure the page remains fast and easy to navigate.
–Hypertext Links: Clickable text within the product name column that acts as a shortcut to view more in-depth information about a specific product.
1.4Business Value
–Efficiency: Enables staff to quickly locate specific inventory items without manually scanning the entire catalog.
–Data Accuracy: Provides a clear, consistent view of essential product metrics like pricing and tax rates, ensuring users have the information they need to make informed decisions.
–Workflow Integration: Streamlines the product management process by providing direct access to both detailed product views and the creation of new inventory items from a single screen.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Product List Page feature allows users to efficiently browse, search, and filter the company’s product catalog. By providing a structured view of all available items, this feature enables staff to quickly locate specific products based on their name or brand, while managing large volumes of data through organized pages.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: When the user opens the Product List Page, the system automatically requests the initial set of products from the database.
2.Applying Filters: The user can narrow down the list by entering a search term or selecting a specific brand.
3.Requesting Data: Once the user clicks the "Apply Search" button, the system sends the chosen filters and the current page number to the server.
4.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the relevant product information, including details like the SKU, product name, brand, code, tax, and price.
5.Display: The system organizes this information into the product list view, ensuring the user sees only the items that match their criteria.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of products into smaller, manageable pages to improve performance and readability.
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down the product list by specific criteria, such as brand name or keyword searches.
–Product Attributes: The specific details displayed for each item, including its unique identifier (SKU), name, brand, internal code, tax rate, and price.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management and product lookup tasks. By allowing users to filter and paginate through the catalog, it reduces the time spent searching for specific items, minimizes manual effort, and ensures that staff are always working with accurate, up-to-date product information.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Product List Page feature provides a centralized view for users to browse the company's entire product catalog. It is designed to help users quickly locate specific items by offering flexible search and filtering capabilities, while ensuring the system remains fast and responsive by displaying products in manageable, bite-sized chunks.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Requesting Data: When you open the product list, the system prepares to fetch the product catalog.
2.Applying Filters: If you enter a search term (such as a product name or SKU) or select a specific brand, the system narrows down the results to match your criteria.
3.Organizing Results: The system sorts all matching products alphabetically by their name to ensure a consistent and predictable view.
4.Pagination: To keep the page loading quickly, the system displays only a set number of products at a time. It calculates which "page" of results you are viewing and skips over the items that belong on previous pages.
5.Displaying Information: The system retrieves the relevant details for each product—including its name, SKU, brand, code, tax rate, and price—and presents them clearly on your screen.
3.3Key Concepts
–Search: A tool that allows you to find products by typing in a name or a specific stock-keeping unit (SKU).
–Filtering: A way to isolate products by selecting a specific brand, removing unrelated items from your view.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of products into smaller, numbered pages to improve performance and readability.
–Mapping: The process of taking raw data from our internal database and translating it into a clean, easy-to-read format for the user interface.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by allowing staff to quickly find and verify product information without manually scrolling through thousands of records. By providing robust search and filter tools, the system reduces the time spent on administrative tasks and ensures that users can access accurate pricing and tax information instantly, supporting better decision-making and faster customer service.
