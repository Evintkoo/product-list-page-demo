1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product List Page provides a centralized view for users to manage and browse the company's product catalog. It allows users to quickly locate specific items using search and filtering tools, view key product details in a structured table, and easily navigate to create new product entries.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page is first opened, the system automatically retrieves the full list of products and displays them in the table.
2.Filtering: Users can enter search terms or select a specific brand from the filter bar to narrow down the results.
3.Applying Search: Once the desired filters are set, the user clicks the "Apply Search" button to refresh the table with the filtered results.
4.Viewing Details: Users can click on any product name in the table to be redirected to a dedicated page containing more comprehensive information about that specific item.
5.Adding Products: A dedicated button is available to redirect the user to the "Add Product" page to create new entries in the system.
1.3Key Concepts
–Filter Bar: A set of controls that allows users to refine the product list by specific criteria, such as product name or brand, to find items more efficiently.
–Product Table: A structured display showing essential product information, including the SKU, name, brand, internal product code, tax rate, and price.
–Navigation: The ability to move between the main list view, individual product detail pages, and the product creation form.
–Data Refresh: The process of updating the table content based on the user's search inputs without needing to reload the entire page.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a streamlined interface for inventory management. By enabling quick searches and clear visibility into product data (such as pricing and tax information), staff can spend less time looking for information and more time managing the catalog. The intuitive navigation ensures that users can move seamlessly between browsing, reviewing details, and adding new inventory.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Product List Page feature allows users to efficiently browse, search, and filter the company’s product catalog. It provides a structured view of all available items, ensuring that users can quickly locate specific products by name, code, or brand. The system is designed to handle large inventories by displaying results in manageable segments, ensuring the page remains fast and easy to navigate.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Initiation: When the page loads or a user clicks the "Apply Search" button, the system captures the current search terms, brand filters, and page number.
2.Data Retrieval: The system searches the product database for items that match the user's criteria (specifically looking for matches in the product name or product code) and applies the selected brand filter.
3.Sorting: The retrieved products are automatically sorted so that the most recently added items appear at the top of the list.
4.Pagination: The system calculates which specific "slice" of the data to show based on the requested page number and the limit of items per page.
5.Display: The system maps the product details—such as SKU, Name, Brand, Code, Tax, and Price—to the corresponding fields on the screen for the user to view.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of products into smaller, numbered pages to improve performance and readability.
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down the product list by specific criteria, such as brand, to help users find exactly what they need.
–Search Query: A text-based input that allows users to find products by typing in a name or a specific product code.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking raw information from the database and organizing it into the specific labels and fields shown on the user interface.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the user experience by reducing the time spent searching for products. By providing robust filtering and organized pagination, it enables staff to manage inventory more effectively and respond to customer inquiries faster. The consistent display of pricing and tax information ensures that users have accurate, up-to-date data at their fingertips, supporting better decision-making and operational efficiency.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Product List Page feature provides a centralized way for users to view, search, and navigate the company's product catalog. It is designed to handle large volumes of data efficiently by allowing users to narrow down results using specific search terms or brand filters, while presenting the information in manageable, paginated chunks.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request Processing: The system receives your search criteria (such as a keyword or brand name) and your current page location.
2.Data Filtering: The system searches the product database to find items that match your specific keywords (by name or SKU) and filters them by the selected brand.
3.Counting Results: The system calculates the total number of products that match your criteria to ensure the page navigation accurately reflects how many pages of results are available.
4.Pagination: The system retrieves only the specific subset of products required for your current view, ensuring the page loads quickly even if there are thousands of items.
5.Data Formatting: The system organizes the raw product information into a clean, readable format, including key details like the SKU, name, brand, pricing, and tax information.
6.Display: The final list of products is presented on your screen, ready for review.
3.3Key Concepts
–Filtering: The ability to narrow down a large list of products by entering a search term or selecting a specific brand.
–Pagination: A method of breaking a large list of items into smaller, separate pages to improve performance and readability.
–SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A unique identifier used to track individual products within the inventory.
–Metadata: Information about the search results (such as the total count) that helps the system manage how many pages of data are available to the user.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by allowing staff to quickly locate specific items within the ERP system. By combining search and filtering capabilities with pagination, the system remains fast and responsive, reducing the time spent searching for product information and ensuring that users can easily access the exact data they need to manage inventory or assist customers.
