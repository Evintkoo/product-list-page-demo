1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product List Page serves as the central hub for managing your inventory within the Lumi ERP system. It provides a clear, organized view of all products, allowing users to quickly locate specific items, monitor stock levels, and manage product status. The page is designed to be highly searchable and filterable, ensuring that users can efficiently navigate large catalogs to find the information they need.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Loading: When you first open the page, the system automatically retrieves and displays a list of "Active" products.
2.Status Filtering: You can toggle between "Active" and "Inactive" product lists using the tabs provided. Switching tabs automatically refreshes the table to show only the relevant items.
3.Advanced Searching: You can narrow down the product list by entering specific criteria into the filter bar (such as SKU, Name, Product Code, Brand, or Category).
4.Applying Filters: Once your search criteria are set, clicking the "Apply Search" button triggers the system to fetch and display the filtered results.
5.Viewing Details: Each product name in the list acts as a link. Clicking on a product name will take you directly to that item's dedicated details page for further review or editing.
6.Adding New Items: A dedicated button is available to transition you to the "Add Product" page whenever you need to register a new item in the system.
1.3Key Concepts
–Status Tabs: A quick-access control to switch between viewing currently active products and those that have been deactivated.
–Filter Bar: A collection of search fields that allow you to pinpoint products based on specific attributes like SKU, name, or brand.
–Product Table: The main display area that organizes product data into columns, including pricing, stock levels, and tax information, for easy comparison.
–Pagination: The system automatically manages large lists by breaking them into manageable pages, ensuring the interface remains fast and responsive.
1.4Business Value
–Operational Efficiency: By providing robust search and filter capabilities, staff can spend less time hunting for product information and more time on core tasks.
–Improved Data Accuracy: Centralizing product management with clear status indicators (Active/Inactive) helps prevent errors in inventory tracking and procurement.
–Streamlined Workflow: The direct navigation to product details and the quick-access "Add Product" feature reduce the number of clicks required to perform daily inventory management duties.
2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Product List Page is designed to help users efficiently browse and manage the company's product catalog. It provides a centralized view where users can see key product details, search for specific items, and filter the list based on their current status (Active or Inactive). The page is built to handle large amounts of data by loading products in manageable segments.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When the page first opens, it automatically displays a list of "Active" products, showing the first 20 items by default.
2.Filtering by Status: Users can toggle between "Active" and "Inactive" tabs. Selecting a tab refreshes the list to show only products matching that status.
3.Searching: Users can enter a keyword into the search bar. Upon clicking the "Apply Search" button, the system filters the product list by name or SKU to find the specific items requested.
4.Data Display: The system retrieves the relevant product information—such as the name, price, stock levels, and status—and presents it in a clear, organized table for the user.
2.3Key Concepts
–Pagination: A method of breaking the product list into smaller, manageable pages (e.g., 20 items at a time) to ensure the page remains fast and easy to navigate.
–Status Filtering: A feature that allows users to isolate products based on their operational state, specifically distinguishing between active and inactive inventory.
–Search Criteria: The ability to look up products using either their unique identification number (SKU) or their common name.
–Data Mapping: The process of taking raw product information from our database and translating it into a user-friendly format for the screen, ensuring that details like price and stock quantity are clearly labeled.
2.4Business Value

This feature streamlines inventory management by reducing the time spent searching for products. By providing quick access to active and inactive items, staff can maintain accurate records and respond to stock inquiries faster. The use of pagination and targeted search ensures that even as the product catalog grows, the system remains responsive and easy to use, directly supporting more efficient daily operations.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Product List Page feature provides a centralized hub for users to view, search, and organize the company’s product catalog. It is designed to help staff quickly locate specific items by narrowing down large lists using categories, status updates, or keyword searches. The page also includes a pagination system to ensure the interface remains fast and easy to navigate, even as the product database grows.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Category Retrieval: The system automatically loads a list of all available product categories to populate the filter dropdown menu.
2.User Input: The user enters a search term (such as a product name or SKU), selects a specific category, or chooses a product status (e.g., active or discontinued).
3.Data Filtering: The system applies these selected criteria to the product database to narrow down the results.
4.Pagination: The system divides the filtered results into manageable pages, displaying only a set number of products at a time based on the user's current view.
5.Display: The system presents the filtered list of products to the user, along with a total count of items found, allowing the user to navigate through the pages.
3.3Key Concepts
–Search Query: A text-based search that looks for matches within product names or unique identification codes (SKUs).
–Category Filtering: A way to group products by their specific type or department to reduce clutter.
–Status Filtering: A method to view products based on their current operational state, such as whether they are currently available for sale.
–Pagination: A navigation tool that breaks a large list of items into smaller, numbered pages to improve page loading speed and readability.
–Metadata: Information provided by the system—such as the total number of items found—that helps the user understand the scope of their search results.
3.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves operational efficiency by reducing the time staff spend searching for product information. By providing robust filtering and organized navigation, the system ensures that users can access accurate, real-time product data without being overwhelmed by the total volume of the catalog. This leads to faster decision-making and more accurate inventory management.
