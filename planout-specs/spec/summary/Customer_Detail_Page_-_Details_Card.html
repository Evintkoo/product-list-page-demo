1.1Overview

The Customer Details Card is a dedicated component within the Lumi ERP system designed to provide a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of a customer's profile. This component acts as a centralized information hub, displaying all essential identity, contact, and professional data in a clean, organized layout. To ensure data integrity, all information presented in this card is strictly read-only, meaning it is intended for viewing purposes rather than editing.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow

The component organizes customer information into four logical sections to ensure clarity:

1.Identity Section: Displays the core identifiers, including the customer's name, unique customer code, and national ID (KTP).
2.Contact Section: Shows communication and location details, specifically the phone number, email address, physical address, and area/region.
3.Professional/Personal Section: Provides background context, including the customer's company, occupation, gender, and birthdate.
4.Metadata Section: Displays administrative tracking information, including the assigned salesperson, the store where the customer was registered, and any additional notes.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only Display: A design approach where information is presented for reference only, preventing accidental changes to sensitive customer records.
–Data Placeholder: A fallback mechanism where the system displays a dash ("-") if a specific piece of information is missing, ensuring the interface remains clean and readable.
–Categorized Layout: The grouping of related data points (Identity, Contact, Professional, and Metadata) to help users quickly locate specific information without scanning the entire page.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing staff with a reliable, "single source of truth" for customer information. By consolidating scattered data points into one structured view, it reduces the time employees spend searching for details across different screens. Furthermore, the read-only nature of the component protects critical customer data from unauthorized or accidental modifications, supporting better data governance and accuracy across the ERP platform.
