1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Detail Header is the primary control center for viewing and managing individual items within the Mini ERP system. This feature provides a comprehensive snapshot of a product, including its core identity, current availability across various locations, and a complete audit trail of its history and movement. It is designed to give users immediate visibility into stock levels and product status, while also offering tools to manage alternatives and track past changes.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Store Inventory: Displays current stock levels across all retail store locations.
2.Warehouse Inventory: Displays current stock levels across all warehouse facilities.
3.Store Stock History: Provides a chronological log of all adjustments made to store inventory.
4.Product Catalog Details: Shows core information including the product name, SKU, and current pricing.
5.Purchase History: Lists completed customer transactions for reporting and return processing.
6.Reservations: Shows stock quantities currently held for pending customer orders.
7.Warehouse Stock History: Provides a chronological log of all adjustments made to warehouse inventory.
8.In-Transit Stock: Tracks items currently being moved between locations that have not yet been received.
9.Substitutes: Displays a list of alternative products that can be used if the primary item is unavailable.
10.Product Change History: Shows a detailed audit log of all modifications made to the product’s core details (such as price or attribute changes).
1.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Visibility: Real-time tracking of stock across different physical locations, including items currently in transit.
–Audit Trail: A chronological record of changes, covering both stock adjustments and modifications to product information.
–Order Fulfillment Support: Tools to manage reservations and view past purchase transactions to assist with customer service and reporting.
–Product Continuity: The ability to identify and suggest substitute items, ensuring sales can continue even when primary stock is depleted.
–Data Integrity: Built-in validation to ensure pricing is accurate and error messaging to alert users if data fails to load.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical product data, reducing the time staff spend switching between different screens to find information. By providing a clear view of stock levels, transit status, and reservations, the system helps prevent overselling and improves inventory accuracy. Furthermore, the inclusion of comprehensive history logs and substitute management ensures that the business can maintain high service levels, respond quickly to customer inquiries, and maintain a reliable audit trail for operational transparency.
