1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Product Detail Header is the primary information hub located at the top of every product page in the Mini ERP system. It provides an immediate snapshot of a product’s identity, current availability across different locations, and quick access to essential management tools. This feature ensures that staff can quickly identify a product and understand its stock status without needing to navigate away from the main view.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Store Inventory: Displays the current stock levels available at retail store locations.
2.Warehouse Inventory: Displays the current stock levels held in warehouse facilities.
3.Stock History: Provides a direct link to view the historical logs of changes to store stock levels.
4.Product Identity: Displays the core product details, including the name, unique SKU, and current price.
5.Purchase History: Provides a link to view the full record of past customer purchases for the item.
6.Reservations: Shows the quantity of stock currently set aside for pending orders.
7.Warehouse History: Provides a direct link to view the historical logs of changes to warehouse stock levels.
8.In-Transit Status: Displays the quantity of stock currently being moved between locations.
9.Substitutes: Shows the number of alternative products available if the primary item is out of stock.
10.Product History: Provides a link to the audit log, which tracks historical changes to the product’s details or pricing.
1.3Key Concepts
–Inventory Visibility: Real-time tracking of stock across stores, warehouses, and transit, including items reserved for orders.
–Auditability: Built-in access to historical logs for stock movements and product attribute changes, ensuring transparency and accountability.
–Fallback Logic: Intelligent display settings, such as showing "N/A" for missing stock data or "Contact for pricing" when a price is not defined, to ensure the interface remains clean and informative.
–Operational Efficiency: Quick-access links to purchase and product histories, allowing users to perform administrative tasks directly from the product header.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes critical product information, reducing the time staff spend searching for data across different screens. By providing immediate visibility into stock levels—including reservations and items in transit—the system helps prevent overselling and improves inventory accuracy. Furthermore, the inclusion of audit logs and purchase history supports better decision-making for procurement and customer service, ultimately streamlining daily warehouse and retail operations.
