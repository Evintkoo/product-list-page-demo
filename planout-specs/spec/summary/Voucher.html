1.1Overview

The Voucher feature provides a structured way to define and manage digital vouchers within the Lumi ERP system. By linking each voucher to a specific product, the system ensures that vouchers are treated as distinct items with clear pricing, validity periods, and usage rules. This feature allows businesses to control where vouchers can be sold and redeemed, providing flexibility for both company-wide promotions and location-specific campaigns.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Voucher Definition: A new voucher is created and linked to a specific product. During this setup, you define its type (e.g., standard or flexible), its monetary value, and its pricing rules (such as whether the price is fixed or can be set at the time of sale).
2.Setting Scope: You determine the "scope" for the voucher, which dictates where it can be sold and where it can be redeemed. You can choose to make these actions available at "all" stores or restrict them to "specific" stores.
3.Location Mapping: If you select "specific" for either selling or redeeming, the system uses dedicated tables to map the voucher to the authorized store locations.
4.Validation & Status: You set the validity period (start and end dates) and the current status (e.g., active or expired). The system automatically tracks who created or updated the voucher for audit purposes.
5.Usage: When a transaction occurs, the system checks the voucher’s status, validity dates, and location permissions to ensure the voucher is eligible for the current store and customer.
1.3Key Concepts
–Voucher: A digital asset linked to a product that holds a specific monetary value and set of usage rules.
–Scope: A setting that defines whether a voucher is valid globally (all stores) or restricted to a subset of locations (specific stores).
–Open Price: A setting that allows staff to manually enter the selling price of a voucher at the time of purchase, rather than using a pre-set price.
–Audit Trail: Automatic tracking of which user created or modified a voucher record, ensuring accountability.
–Validity Period: The timeframe during which a voucher is active and eligible for use, managed by start and end dates or duration-based expiry.
1.4Business Value
–Operational Control: Businesses can precisely manage where vouchers are issued and redeemed, preventing unauthorized usage and ensuring promotions are targeted correctly.
–Flexibility: The "open price" and "flexible" voucher types allow for dynamic pricing strategies, such as gift cards or custom-value vouchers, without needing to create new product records for every variation.
–Data Integrity: By linking vouchers to existing products, the system maintains consistent reporting and inventory tracking across the ERP.
–Compliance & Security: Built-in audit trails and status management ensure that voucher usage is transparent and that expired or inactive vouchers cannot be misused.
