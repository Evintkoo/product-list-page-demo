1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Sales Order Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific sales order within the Lumi ERP system. It acts as a central hub for users to review order information, track the progress of an order through its lifecycle, and perform necessary administrative actions like approving or delivering the order.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Authentication & Loading: When you open the page, the system verifies your login status. If you are not logged in, you will be redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: The system fetches the specific details for the requested order ID.
3.Information Display: The page populates with the order's header information (such as document numbers, customer details, and dates), a list of items included in the order, and the financial totals.
4.Workflow Tracking: A status bar at the top highlights the current stage of the order: Draft, Approved, or Delivered.
5.Action Execution: Depending on the current status of the order, you can perform specific actions:

* Print: Available at any time to generate a proforma invoice.

* Approve: Available if the order is in "Draft" status.

* Deliver: Available if the order is in "Approved" status.

* Cancel: Available if the order is in "Draft" or "Approved" status.

1.3Key Concepts
–Workflow Status: A visual indicator showing the current stage of the order (Draft, Approved, or Delivered).
–Header Information: Key metadata about the order, including reference codes, warehouse origin, customer destination, and timestamps for creation and updates.
–Order Items Table: A detailed breakdown of the products ordered, including SKU, quantity, pricing, discounts, and the final amount per line item.
–Financial Summary: The calculation of the order's subtotal, tax, and final total amount.
–Action Buttons: Context-aware controls that allow users to move the order forward in the workflow or print documentation.
1.4Business Value

This feature centralizes all critical information regarding a sales order, reducing the need to navigate between different screens to find data. By providing clear status tracking and role-appropriate action buttons, it ensures that the sales process remains organized, transparent, and efficient, helping teams manage order fulfillment from initial entry to final delivery with minimal errors.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The Sales Order Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific customer order within the Lumi ERP system. It acts as a central hub for users to review order information, including customer details, items purchased, financial totals, and the current progress of the order. The page is designed to be dynamic, adjusting the available action buttons based on the order's current status to ensure users only perform relevant tasks.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Security Verification: When a user navigates to the page, the system automatically checks their login session. If the user is not logged in, they are redirected to the login screen.
2.Data Retrieval: The system identifies the specific order from the web address and fetches all relevant details, such as customer information, warehouse location, item lists, and pricing.
3.Information Display: The retrieved data is organized into three clear sections:

* Header: Contains administrative details like document numbers, dates, notes, and the assigned salesperson.

* Items Table: Lists every product in the order, including quantities ordered versus delivered, pricing, and discounts.

* Totals: Displays the subtotal, tax, and final order amount.

4.Status-Based Actions: The system checks the order's status (Draft, Approved, or Delivered) to display the appropriate action buttons:

* Draft: Allows the user to "Approve" the order.

* Approved/Delivered: Allows the user to generate a "Proforma Invoice."

* All Statuses: Always provides a "Print" option.

2.3Key Concepts
–Workflow Status: A visual indicator that tracks the lifecycle of an order (Draft, Approved, or Delivered), which dictates what actions a user can take.
–Header Information: The top section of the page containing metadata, such as who created the order, when it was updated, and the associated customer or warehouse.
–Order Items Table: A detailed breakdown of the products, showing the relationship between what was requested (Order Qty) and what has been fulfilled (Delivered Qty).
–Action Buttons: Context-sensitive controls that appear based on the order status to prevent errors and streamline the workflow.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a single, reliable source of truth for sales orders. By tailoring the interface to the order's status, the system reduces user error and guides staff through the correct business process—ensuring that, for example, a draft order cannot be invoiced before it is approved. This clarity helps sales and warehouse teams coordinate effectively and maintain accurate records.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The Sales Order Details page provides a comprehensive view of a specific sales order within the Lumi ERP system. It acts as a central hub where users can review all critical information related to a transaction, including customer details, shipping information, the list of products ordered, and the final financial breakdown.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request: The system receives a request to view a specific sales order by its unique identifier.
2.Data Retrieval: The system gathers the primary order information, including the status, dates, and financial totals.
3.Information Enrichment: The system pulls in related details to provide a complete picture, specifically linking the order to the correct customer and warehouse records.
4.Line Item Compilation: The system retrieves all individual products associated with that order, including quantities, pricing, and any applied discounts.
5.Display: All gathered information is organized and presented on the page, grouping data into the order header, the itemized product list, and the final financial summary.
3.3Key Concepts
–Sales Order Aggregate: A complete collection of all data related to a single order, ensuring that the user sees a unified view rather than fragmented pieces of information.
–Line Items: The individual products or services included in an order, detailing what was ordered versus what has been delivered.
–Financial Summary: The breakdown of the order costs, including the subtotal, tax, and the final total amount.
–Workflow Status: A visual indicator (such as Draft, Approved, or Delivered) that helps users quickly understand the current stage of the order in the business process.
3.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing sales and warehouse teams with a "single source of truth" for any given order. By consolidating customer, warehouse, and product data into one view, staff can quickly verify order accuracy, track delivery progress, and resolve customer inquiries without needing to navigate between multiple screens or systems.
