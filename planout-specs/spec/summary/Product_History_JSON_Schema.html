1.  Data
1.1Overview

The Product History feature provides a reliable audit trail for your inventory. It allows the system to track and retrieve a chronological record of changes made to any specific product. By capturing snapshots of pricing, status, and configuration settings whenever a change occurs, the system ensures you have a clear view of how a product’s details have evolved over time.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Request: The system receives a request to view the history of a specific product.
2.Query: The system searches the database for all recorded changes associated with that product ID.
3.Sort: The results are organized in reverse chronological order, ensuring the most recent updates appear first.
4.Format: The raw data is converted into a standardized, readable format (JSON) that includes all relevant details, such as who made the change, when it happened, and the specific price or status updates applied at that time.
5.Delivery: The formatted history list is returned to the user or application interface.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A chronological record that provides documentary evidence of the sequence of activities that have affected a specific product.
–JSON Representation: A standardized, lightweight format used to structure data so that it can be easily read and shared between different parts of the software.
–Primary Key: A unique identifier for each specific history entry, ensuring that every change record is distinct and traceable.
–Pagination: The ability to limit the number of records returned at once (using "limit" and "offset"), which keeps the system fast and responsive even when a product has a long history of changes.
1.4Business Value
–Accountability: By recording who updated a product and when, your team can easily identify the source of any configuration or pricing changes.
–Data Integrity: Having a historical record helps in troubleshooting discrepancies and provides a safety net if a product’s settings need to be reverted to a previous state.
–Informed Decision Making: Access to historical pricing and status trends allows management to analyze past product performance and strategy shifts over time.
