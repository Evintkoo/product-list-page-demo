1.  Logic
1.1Overview

This feature provides a way to view the complete purchase history for a specific retail customer within the Lumi ERP system. By selecting a customer, authorized users can retrieve a detailed list of items purchased, including the transaction amount and the date of each purchase, presented in chronological order.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Security Check: The system verifies that the person requesting the information is logged in and has the necessary permissions to view customer sales data.
2.Customer Verification: The system checks the database to ensure the requested customer exists. If the customer cannot be found, the system returns an error.
3.Data Retrieval: Once the customer is confirmed, the system gathers all sales records linked to that specific customer.
4.Display: The system presents the list of purchases, showing the item name, the amount spent, and the date of the transaction.
1.3Key Concepts
–Authentication: The process of confirming the identity of the user to ensure they are allowed to access the system.
–Authorization: The process of checking if a verified user has the specific permission required to view sensitive sales records.
–Customer ID: A unique identifier used by the system to ensure the correct customer's data is retrieved.
–Sales Record: A stored entry containing details about a specific transaction, such as what was bought, how much it cost, and when it happened.
1.4Business Value

This feature empowers staff to provide better customer service by offering immediate access to a customer's buying habits and history. By having this information readily available, the team can more effectively manage customer relationships, resolve inquiries regarding past orders, and identify opportunities for personalized recommendations or support.

2.  DATA
2.1Overview

This feature provides a way to retrieve a detailed history of purchases for a specific retail customer within the Lumi ERP system. By providing a unique customer identifier, the system will return the customer's name alongside a chronological list of all items they have purchased, making it easy to view their buying activity.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Verification: The system receives a request for a specific customer's sales history. It first checks the database to ensure the customer exists. If the customer cannot be found, the system stops and reports that the customer does not exist.
2.Data Retrieval: Once the customer is confirmed, the system searches the sales records for all transactions linked to that specific customer.
3.Sorting: The system organizes the retrieved sales records, ensuring the most recent purchases appear first.
4.Response: The system compiles the customer's name and their sorted list of purchases into a single report and delivers it to the user.
2.3Key Concepts
–Customer Identifier: A unique code used to ensure the system retrieves data for the correct person.
–Sales Records: A collection of transaction details, including the item name, the cost, and the date the purchase was made.
–Chronological Sorting: The process of arranging the purchase history so that the newest transactions are displayed at the top of the list.
–Validation: A safety check performed at the start of the process to confirm that the requested customer is valid before attempting to pull their data.
2.4Business Value

This feature improves customer service and sales visibility by allowing staff to quickly access a customer's purchase history. By providing an organized, up-to-date view of what a customer has bought, the business can better understand customer preferences, resolve inquiries faster, and provide a more personalized shopping experience.
