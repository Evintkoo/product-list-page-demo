1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Franchise Customer Details Card is a read-only component designed for the Lumi ERP system. Its purpose is to provide a clear, organized view of essential information for franchise customers. The card is structured into three distinct sections to ensure that users can quickly locate specific data points regarding the customer, their primary contact person, and the assigned salesperson.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Header Display: The card displays a "Details" header to identify the information block.
2.Customer Identity Section: The system presents core business information, including the customer code, name, region, address, national ID (KTP), contact details (phone/email), internal notes, and bank information.
3.Contact Person Section: The system displays specific details for the customer's point of contact, including their full name, professional contact information, location details (country/region), and their role within the organization (position and department).
4.Salesperson Section: The system displays the assigned salesperson's information, split into two specific fields: the salesperson's name and their unique identification number.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: All information displayed is for viewing purposes only; users cannot edit or modify these fields directly through this component.
–Customer Identity: A collection of foundational data that defines the franchise customer within the ERP system.
–Contact Person: Specific professional details for the individual acting as the primary liaison for the franchise.
–Salesperson: The internal staff member associated with the account, identified by both name and ID for clarity.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by providing a centralized, easy-to-read summary of franchise customer data. By organizing information into logical categories, it reduces the time staff spend searching for critical details, minimizes errors during customer interactions, and ensures that all team members have consistent access to the same accurate information.
