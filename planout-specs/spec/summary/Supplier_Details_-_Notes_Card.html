1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The Supplier Notes Card is a dedicated section within the Supplier Detail page designed to provide quick, read-only access to critical reference information. It centralizes the supplier's banking information and any relevant operational notes, ensuring that team members have immediate visibility into these details without needing to navigate away from the supplier's profile.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading State: When you first open the supplier page, the system briefly displays a loading indicator while it retrieves the necessary information from the database.
2.Data Retrieval: The system automatically fetches the specific bank details and notes associated with the selected supplier.
3.Displaying the Header: The card appears with a "Note" header, accompanied by a document icon to help you quickly identify this section.
4.Presenting Bank Details: The system displays the supplier's banking information in a clear, read-only format.
5.Presenting Operational Notes: The system displays any additional free-text notes regarding the supplier (such as specific engine parts or service requirements) in a read-only format below the bank details.
6.Error Handling: If the system is unable to retrieve the information due to a connection issue, a clear error message is displayed to inform you that the data could not be loaded.
1.3Key Concepts
–Read-Only: Information that is displayed for your reference but cannot be edited directly within this specific card.
–Supplier Data: The centralized information stored in our system regarding a specific vendor, including their financial and operational details.
–Loading State: A temporary visual indicator that lets you know the system is actively working to retrieve information.
1.4Business Value

This feature improves operational efficiency by eliminating the need to search through separate documents or external files to find banking or account-specific notes. By surfacing this information directly on the Supplier Detail page, team members can make faster, more informed decisions during procurement or payment processes, while the read-only nature of the data ensures that sensitive information remains accurate and protected from accidental changes.
